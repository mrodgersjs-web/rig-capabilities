# Stats articles (one mechanism)

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

Governing contracts: rig-revenue-meta. "It's data-driven. It's trusted." (CLAIM: bRsijO5rrCc).
Data provenance is the whole game: the firm's own data or licensed/public data with citations —
never scraped-and-stolen datasets.

## Step 1. Choose the data

The firm's own anonymized figures (outcomes, volumes, timelines — professional sign-off required),
a licensed public dataset, or an original small survey. Name the source and the window.

## Step 2. Structure the report

1. Headline stat + 3-5 key takeaways up top (answer-first).
2. Methodology block: what was measured, over what window, sample size, honest limits.
3. Per-section: the number, what it means for the buyer, chart/table.
4. Named expert commentary (credential verified) — the E-E-A-T layer that makes it quotable.
5. Sources list; FAQ block; downloadable chart assets for writers to embed (with attribution).

## Step 3. Ship and pitch

1. rig-revenue-seo-council (contrarian checks: is any stat overclaimed?) → rig-revenue-publish-gate.
2. Pitch to journalists/writers covering the topic with the genuine data cut they'd want
   (rig-revenue-authority-outreach Steps 3-4). Refresh the data annually and republish with a new
   date.
