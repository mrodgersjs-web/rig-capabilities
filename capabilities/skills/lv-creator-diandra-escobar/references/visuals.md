## What she says to attach

On 2026-03-05 she tells operators to try a visual on the post even when the writing is the product: a photo, a one-pager, a diagram, a screenshot, a before/after data view, or a photo from a talk. She claims a visual beats bare text. Creator claim, unverified. Add the visual only if it helps the bucket. Decoration is not the rule. [ZAqIUNAvXXo]

Proof visuals are the how-I layer: screen shares and the actual doc, not a styled mock of work you did not do. [a4uL3wTtjFk]

For carousels she claims two retrieval hygiene moves, both unverified: put keywords in the file name, and write real alt text. She does not give slide counts, font sizes, or export dimensions in this corpus. [BPbf3JUm_dw, PxjDCR29aKc]

## The ladder, not a video-first habit

Test the idea as text. If the text hits, rebuild it as a carousel. If the carousel hits, then consider video. She says to test 10 to 15 hooks in text before any design spend. [Z3jHnYT8iJo]

That ladder is the opposite of starting in a design tool. A client post she cites broke ordinary formatting rules and still traveled (she claims about 85,000 impressions from a base near 2,000). Creator claim. Do not read that as permission to ship ugly. Read it as: the words and the proof outrank the template. [BPbf3JUm_dw]

## Talk footage is a trust layer, not a growth hack

For speaking, footage is the proof that you can hold a room. Record talks, panels, and webinars. A tripod at the back of the room is the capture she describes. Recycle one talk into a clip, a recap post, and the next posts. Tag the organizer on the recap so they can reshare. [JtpBDc46w-k]

In that August 2026 video she names Riverside as a sponsor and lists what she uses it for: local recording up to 4K, a track per speaker, edits from the transcript, and auto clips with captions. That is a sponsor mention in her video, not a RIG standard. Do not buy it, and do not treat the feature list as tested by RIG. [JtpBDc46w-k]

A stage or microphone banner is part of her speaking profile, not a default header for a B2B offer. [JtpBDc46w-k]

## Preview, and what not to style

Spacing in a doc is not spacing in the feed. Preview in a formatter, mobile first, before you publish. A single-line hook needs two manual breaks under it or the phone collapses it. [Zc4E_K48v48, ZAqIUNAvXXo]

Strip Unicode stylized letters from the name, headline, about, and the post. She claims that styling cut ChatGPT citations about 58%, while two other AI modes were unaffected in the study she cites. Creator claim. The safe move is plain text either way. [D9DKTj85wWc]

News workflows can land as a dated PDF or an HTML email with source links so a human can verify the story before anyone designs a card. The PDF is an internal check, not the LinkedIn creative. [KlawrDJuqYc]

## Applying it (RIG)

Real photo first. Clark cites about 27% more impressions for in-real-life photos across a month of client posts (his claim, not hers, and not Mike's). Escobar's wider menu (diagrams, screenshots, one-pagers) is allowed when the image is the receipt: a real dashboard, a real before/after, a real room. A branded graphic with no proof loses to the photo.

Eden generates images from a brief that names the receipt. Do not ask Eden for a mascot, a silhouette coach, or a pop-culture still on Lane B. Lane A cast rules stay in the Lane A skill, not here.

No carousel script from her videos gets copied. If a carousel is warranted, the slots are: slide 1 is the claim plus the audience, middle slides are one mechanism each with a sourced number, last slide is the ask that matches the bucket. She never published that skeleton. It is the RIG constraint on top of her ladder.

Do not adopt her sponsor tool. Do not invent slide counts. Profile banners and featured images that change the public profile are Gate-D.
