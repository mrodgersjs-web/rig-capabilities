# typography: looks like / does not

Owning main skill: ds-typography. Every row paraphrases a good-versus-bad contrast a creator showed, cited to the moment. Use as calibration before judging a screen; a card is only reusable as a visual reference after Jev ui.design scores the good side above the bad side.

| # | looks like (good) | does not (bad) | why | citation |
|---|-------------------|----------------|-----|----------|
| 1 | Long copy flush left with a one-sided rag | A centered paragraph whose rag breaks on both sides | The eye can return to a fixed left edge each line | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@01:29, QrNi9FmdlxY@00:00) |
| 2 | A capped column of 50 to 70 characters | Full-width lines stretched across a large monitor | Excess length forces head movement while reading | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@04:30, 9ut7EmnYG7I@08:30) |
| 3 | One dominant headline with small supporting type | A page of equally medium blocks | Scale contrast is what creates a reading order | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@17:30, MFUXBlnTS5Y@07:29) |
| 4 | A serif and a sans matched on x-height and era | Two geometric sans faces that are almost identical | Near-duplicates read as a rendering bug, not a pairing | CLAIM(WTUM2Y11oJo@34:31, pbhLsV-Dyho@02:02) |
| 5 | Two typefaces with restrained UI text around a complex logo | A different font on every element of one page | Fewer faces keep the page legible and the brand coherent | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@07:30, 9ut7EmnYG7I@12:29) |
| 6 | Type on a solid color or a flattened photo area | Dark type dropped on a busy, high-detail photograph | Contrast collapses where the photo is busy | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@01:00, 9ut7EmnYG7I@03:59) |
| 7 | Body copy around 130 percent leading | 100 percent leading that packs lines, or 200 percent that breaks grouping | Line spacing has to support scanning and keep paragraphs whole | CLAIM(9ut7EmnYG7I@09:59) |
| 8 | Metric kerning in body text, hand-kerned logotype | Optical kerning applied across an entire text block | The tool's guess is weaker than the designer's metrics for reading text | CLAIM(y2Tn9DS4ROs@03:29, y2Tn9DS4ROs@03:59) |
| 9 | A bold headline against light body copy | One phrase bolded, italicized, underlined and colored | Contrast needs a weight jump; stacked emphasis reads as pushy | CLAIM(QrNi9FmdlxY@00:30, 9ut7EmnYG7I@12:00) |
| 10 | Consistent sentence case through a page | A heading where random words get capitals | Inconsistent casing signals an amateur hand | CLAIM(9ut7EmnYG7I@10:59) |
| 11 | Family widths drawn by the type designer | One width stretched in software to fake another | Stretching distorts stems and stroke contrast | CLAIM(9ut7EmnYG7I@00:00, 9ut7EmnYG7I@01:30) |
| 12 | Kerning checked with the word flipped to shapes | Kerning judged while still reading the word | Reading masks uneven spacing; shapes reveal it | CLAIM(y2Tn9DS4ROs@01:30, MFUXBlnTS5Y@04:58) |
| 13 | Live HTML text over an image | Text rendered into the image file | Baked text is unreadable to search engines and assistive tech | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@07:59) |
| 14 | A scale computed from one body size by a ratio | Invented sizes like 13, 17, 21, 33 pt mixed in one layout | Ratios give harmony; one-offs give visual noise | CLAIM(MFUXBlnTS5Y@02:01, 9ut7EmnYG7I@05:30) |
| 15 | A small emblem with generous margin | Four logos crammed into one header strip | Margin lets the mark breathe instead of cluttering | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@21:00, aOPGqOOi__4@18:29) |
| 16 | Uppercase, wide tracking, small size, lots of white space for a premium mood | The same words at default spacing and size in the middle of the page | Premium is a spacing and case treatment before it is a font choice | CLAIM(Yfd5kW1ZMgQ@01:59, Yfd5kW1ZMgQ@03:31) |
| 17 | Text at 100, 70 and 60 percent opacity tiers | Every text layer at full opacity | Opacity tiers carry hierarchy without adding colors | CLAIM(pbhLsV-Dyho@13:00) |
| 18 | Bullet lists aligned to a straight left edge | A centered bullet list | Bullets need one alignment axis to scan | CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@19:30) |
| 19 | Weight of a display word set at true size, then sized up when the layout demands it | Body copy too small to read comfortably at delivery size | Point size has to be judged at the size the reader sees | CLAIM(9ut7EmnYG7I@05:00, MFUXBlnTS5Y@00:30) |
| 20 | An accent face used once, with a neutral face doing the work | Decorative faces stacked across headings, body and captions | One decorative face keeps the page from turning to noise | CLAIM(WTUM2Y11oJo@36:00) |
