# personal-admin

## Purchases and gifts
1. Read `users/mike-rodgers-actions.md` (purchasing protocol, including unfamiliar sites).
2. Shortlist with prices, delivery dates and the seller's standing.
3. Before placing the order, show one line with item, price, shipping address and payment method (last four only) and wait for Mike's yes.
4. After ordering, confirm from the order page or the confirmation email, not from the click.

## Insurance, benefits, health paperwork
Collect the documents, fill what can be filled, and list what only Mike can attest to. Never submit a sworn or official form; Mike presses submit.

## Legal lookups
Research with sources and dates; name the jurisdiction; say plainly that it is not legal advice; offer two or three attorneys with how each was found.

## Scam check
Check the sender's real address and domain, the reply-to, and where every link actually points (read, never click). Compare against known contacts in Gmail and iMessage. Verdict: scam, likely scam, or legitimate, with the evidence for each point.

## Documents and forms
Use the `pdf` skill to read and fill. Show the filled values before anything is sent or signed.

## Done-test
Each purchase, submission or signature in the transcript has Mike's explicit yes right before it, and each claimed completion is confirmed from a second place (order page, email, portal status).
