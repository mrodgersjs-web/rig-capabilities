# Creator playbook: engagement (comments, replies, borrowed attention)

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage. Sources: each creator's `engagement.md` plus Clark's `references/distribution-and-algorithm.md`. Short names: Clark, Lara, Diandra, Will, Harry, Kasey, Mark, Pierre, RYVO, Shanee, Michelle. Volume and reach figures are creator claims.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. No pods, no bought engagement, no comment tools. Clark: pods teach the feed to serve non-buyers and accounts sag after leaving [8nBIAUSIRnQ]. Will cites platform crackdowns [3UcZFurI_yw]. Diandra reads pods as suppressed [BPbf3JUm_dw]. RYVO bans them [MVbCMTE5cR8], Shanee rejects pod-style reciprocity [toTLWp4aOtI], Harry calls them the old rig [fWhBOPC1eLA]. Mark's corpus never teaches one.
2. No machine writes a comment. Clark would rather see no comment than a machine one [8nBIAUSIRnQ]; Lara says readers spot AI comments instantly [ozhwN9ndetM]; RYVO names the giveaway line pattern in suggested comments [C6CKFidysR4]; Pierre bans visible automation [DB-P9AUogXU]; Michelle says a bot restating the post looks terrible [LG_sBQB2xAc].
3. A comment carries a real point, never praise alone. Lara wants a mini case, a challenge or a tip [OSzrHwHLT6M]; Diandra, a point, a challenge or a short story [w3_109ygioY]; Kasey, only what fits a real conversation plus an insight or a sharp question [vc3FaWfXggY]; Mark, agree then add one usable idea [lzOWlqfHz-s]; Shanee, credit first then a different angle [aCUbwMrQ8GA]; Clark, a real thread beats a one-word reply [DGYZ04X5pk4].
4. Reply to everyone on your own post. Kasey names 60 to 90 minutes and admits failing it on his biggest post [hC7ZCVsG7qM]; Clark's 2026 checklist, every comment inside 30 to 60 minutes with a question that extends the thread [Fvl_wMtcz24]; Diandra keeps the habit after demoting the speed story [R8nrEQx8rEY]; Lara pairs replies with three self-comments [6d_oxo1R9gs]; RYVO names the first hour [lf7dLXWwb24]; Michelle answers before adding volume [dNDNxyoD4qE].
5. Comment targets follow the buyer, not the feed. Lara filters commenters under a target's post by headline [Slm6Ek465ow]; Diandra keeps two lists of 20 to 30 [w3_109ygioY]; Will says wrong-niche commenters import the wrong audience [UJvAtV63vk0]; Clark targets buyers, the voices buyers follow and competitors on a 15 to 30 account sheet [mJ1c1PHBoo4]; Pierre runs about 20 influencers plus 10 to 20 ICP accounts weekly [DB-P9AUogXU]; Michelle keeps a halo of about twenty [Q8rO8FCy72w].
6. Depth outranks volume. Clark: 5 to 10 thoughtful beats 20 to 50 lazy [SL8yfxUVFKE]. Kasey mocks the 50-a-day advice as noise [vc3FaWfXggY]. Will calls comment spam self-defeating [Ej7WFBI9NS4]. Pierre would skip a day rather than force a line [DB-P9AUogXU]. Michelle refuses a universal quota [LG_sBQB2xAc]. Mark rejected the daily grind in 2026-04 [iubQmgPG3ZM].
7. A strong comment is a distribution asset. Lara, Diandra, Kasey, Pierre, Michelle, Clark and RYVO each claim a comment that out-travelled a post or reached past first degree [6EYqnuncRrg, S8gabIoWUDQ, kXBXV1bQKes, O8HeAkNg6L8]. Mechanism unverified; practice shared.

## What nobody teaches

- Commenting on a client-owned account: voice ownership, approval, identity check before a click. A RIG lane problem only.
- Any tested attribution method for comment-driven results. Every reach figure here is a screenshot, self-report or guest anecdote.
- Behavior when a thread turns hostile while a positive-only rule binds the operator.
- Engagement pacing under a platform throttle signature, or how to stop a sweep mid-batch. Michelle's cap story is closest and she does not know the number [_yf76tCe-zY].
- Engagement on a minor's account. Out of scope here too.

## How to apply here

Target selection stays the stage's own: the thread floor, past-week window and 14-day author dedupe in `references/comment-targets-cadence.md` beat every creator list. Construction stays in `references/comments-on-others.md`, where the positive-only shape and link caps already override the challenge move and every volume prescription above. Replies follow `references/reply-handling.md`, and the reply habit survives the golden-hour demotion. Each comment still passes `rig-s1gate check comment.linkedin`. A creator volume number is planning context, never a quota and never a reason to exceed a sweep cap. Lane rules govern first: nothing on the Ideawake lane without Mike's client yes, Lane A stages only, minor's lane out.
