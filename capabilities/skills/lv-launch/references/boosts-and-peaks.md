# Thought-leader ad boosts and manufactured peaks

## Boosts are spend, so each boost is a Gate-D item

A thought-leader ad boost is money leaving an account. Every boost is proposed to Mike with a dollar amount and gets his explicit approval before a cent is spent. An advisory Jev PASS on a boost candidate is not permission to spend.

The mechanics, as one creator describes them (claims, not verified on this account):

- Let the post run organically for one to two weeks first. Boost proven winners and ICP-relevant posts that under-delivered; he names roughly 17-31 likes as the under-delivery band.
- Start at 20-30 dollars a day (about 900-1,000 dollars a month) and treat that as the ceiling of a first test, not a default.
- Before boosting, edit a PS line with a link into the post. Boosted posts run only on awareness or engagement objectives, so the PS line is the only call to action available.
- Target with an uploaded company list (for example an Apollo export), which he finds more accurate than native filters. Feed the paid engagers back into the outreach lane, and mine the ad demographics and the company report for target accounts.
- He claims boosted posts keep their organic engagement and look native. Verify before building a plan on it.

## Manufactured peaks

Steady posting grows slowly and linearly. Launches, fundraises and real-world stunts create spikes that own the feed for a day; the physical-to-digital version is "IRL to URL". Plan at least one peak a month on top of the cadence. [1je0kCMMstg, 29hBCF7N7Jg, Xt179qkyqQU]

## The launch echo (big moments only)

1. **Announcement:** the amount or milestone in the opening and in the image, the founding story, what it enables; tag investors and key customers who were briefed in advance.
2. **Engagement lift:** brief supporters beforehand, then send the live link within 30-60 minutes framed as an opportunity, not a favor, with an easy way to decline.
3. **Echo chamber:** founder, company page and five to seven original team posts (no shared script), optionally investors, over one to two days; the main post gets reposted about ten hours later. The company page gets a post a week at most. [Xt179qkyqQU, N2b0QVoCEPg]
4. **Demand capture:** turn engagers, site visitors and team-post engagers into enriched outreach lists, through the outreach stage, never by pitching them cold from here.
5. Prepare at least a month ahead.

## Rules that survive here

- Network mobilization means real people making real comments on real milestones. No pods, no bought signals, no shared scripts. [8nBIAUSIRnQ, Xt179qkyqQU]
- Never tag in bulk outside a fundraise or launch; mass tagging for reach is a spam signal. [Xt179qkyqQU]
- A peak is planned, not improvised: the launch date, the echo roster and the boost question are settled before the announcement is drafted.
- Any number in a boost report is a measured receipt, never an estimate.
