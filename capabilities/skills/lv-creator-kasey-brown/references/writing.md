# Writing (Kasey Brown)

Body craft, post type selection, structure and CTAs. Every claim is a creator claim from the cited video.

## Structure he repeats

- His oldest teaching frame is a four-part arc: open on the hook with a line break, give context or story, lay out explicit takeaways, then keep the conversation going with a question or a PS line. [4jnS31Fh5QE]
- The story-arc version he writes live: set the scene with concrete detail, hit a turning point, state the decision or vow, land the transferable lesson, close with a soft CTA, and stack PS asides after it. He drafts to a post goal first, dumps the raw story fast, then edits by reading aloud and deleting anything that does not move the story. [jcxbBuiW2vE]
- Story before instruction; the credibility signal sits in the first lines as experience or data rather than self-praise. [M0dAWh95NaQ, ycA0D55AEQ8]

## Post types

- The revenue formula he names: specific hook, credibility, a named framework, a specific proof point, then a soft CTA that extends the value (a guide, a newsletter, a waitlist), never a hard pitch line. [M0dAWh95NaQ]
- He taught a 16-type, 4-category system (authority, thought leadership, trust, results) with a 31-day rotation in March 2026. [ycA0D55AEQ8] For daily use he replaced it in Sept 2026 with five types: contrarian take, behind-the-scenes lesson, practical framework, newsjacking, relationship builder, mixed roughly 20-30 percent authority, 60 percent value, 20 percent relationship. [2t-EBcS43Lw, B18w0jPdXiI]
- Type recipes, paraphrased: contrarian = bold counter-claim, story, why the common view fails, why yours holds. Behind-the-scenes = what happened, what you got wrong (the wrong travels further), the lesson, how to apply it. Framework = a memorable name, three to five steps, brief explanation, one real example; this is his most-saved format. Newsjacking = what happened, what most people missed, what it means for your reader, what to do; only when you have the untaken angle. Relationship = a perspective plus a genuine question that exposes a solvable problem. [2t-EBcS43Lw, LvA5pocucUM]
- Trust and results posts do the selling work. His transformation recipe puts the best result first (number plus timeframe), then the named client, the struggle, what failed before, the step-by-step work, and the change. [ycA0D55AEQ8]

## Readability rules

- Roughly fourth-grade reading level, checked in a readability tool; his measured split is that revenue-driving posts read like that while vanity posts read like a twelfth-grade essay. [TcvoXYSHqe4, EfHijF0P6bo]
- Short sentences, active voice, whitespace, scannable layout, digits instead of spelled-out numbers, no hashtags, no filler, big blocks avoided because they slow scanning. [TcvoXYSHqe4, LvA5pocucUM, m9VFFCW_qZs, wqbIWGV9GDg]
- Long is fine on purpose when every line earns dwell; the test is whether each line advances the story. [LvA5pocucUM, hC7ZCVsG7qM]

## Voice and status

- Dated shift: through 2025 he leaned on "authenticity" as the lesson [pNhM9BttZLQ, sb0qucoOSow], and in Dec 2025 he relayed a mentor's call to retire that word for a humanness framing [7vl7hhn_y78]. By mid-2026 the operative rule is status: strip hedging phrases, drop engagement bait, stop borrowing famous frameworks and demonstrate your own receipts, and aim every post at one named person. [kuipymgFpjk]
- He reversed his own earlier "post for everyone" advice in June 2026 after measuring revenue per post. [kuipymgFpjk]
- Deliberate imperfection is part of his current voice play: PS asides, human texture, even occasional intentional typos as an authorship signal. That last one is his claim and it conflicts with RIG quality floors; see the overlays in `SKILL.md`. [jcxbBuiW2vE]

## CTAs

- Soft CTA that adds value, never a hard pitch from the post body. [M0dAWh95NaQ]
- Comment-gated delivery: ask readers to comment a keyword to receive the asset, then move to DM; the post itself never closes a sale. [jcxbBuiW2vE, jYCnMnYF9EE]
- His quality gate before publish is blunt: would a buyer pay $1,000 for this insight? He says it kills about half his drafts. [2t-EBcS43Lw]

## Applying it (RIG)

Pick the post type before drafting and write one type per slot, not a blend; for RIG B2B the authority type and the framework type are the default pair. Fill the structure slots with a real client outcome or a real internal number, run the readability pass, then run the $1,000 question. Route the finished artifact to `rig-s1gate check post.linkedin` before it goes to Eden. Mike's rule set still governs: a keyword-comment funnel that triggers an automated DM is a send, so it waits for his yes.
