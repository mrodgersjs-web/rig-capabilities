# Silo architecture (one mechanism)

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

Governing contracts: rig-revenue-meta. "A big circle of flow" — the corpus's phrase for the loop

## Step 1. Map the silos

One silo per core service/theme: pillar page (the money page or definitive guide) + 3-5 longtail
satellites (question answers, comparisons, local specifics). Categories are the streets; service
pages are the houses; the homepage is the city map (CLAIM: 7y8MwUs2Sdw).

## Step 2. Link rules

1. Every satellite links UP to its pillar/money page with descriptive anchor text.
2. The money page links BACK to its best satellites (proof articles).
3. Every new post links to ≥1 older sibling; siblings cross-link within the silo.
4. Nothing is orphaned: orphan/404/unlinked pages decay and get deindexed (CLAIM: xsB-oSb8YNE,
   UAD7_NO6cdw).

## Step 3. Click depth

Winners sit one click from the homepage (a curated "top articles" hub page where a blog exists —
CLAIM: T62I2Rch8F4). Deep-buried posts lose ranking and clicks.

## Step 4. Maintain

Monthly internal-link cleanup as part of rig-revenue-decay-refresh: fix broken links, canonical
mismatches, inconsistent URL forms ("Google samples your site and if enough of it feels
sloppy... it assumes the rest might be too" — CLAIM: -UkHh5kbKEQ).
