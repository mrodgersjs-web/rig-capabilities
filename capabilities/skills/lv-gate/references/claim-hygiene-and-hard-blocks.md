# Claim hygiene and hard blocks: what a draft must not contain

## Claim hygiene (publish-gate checklist)

Every figure carries a source or a claim tag; no ranking guarantees. Every corpus-lineage tool mention
carries its paid-endorsement label, including renamed spellings of the same tool. No excluded tactic is
present: link schemes, SERP flooding, unattended publishing, fake profile data, AI-detection evasion,
doorway pages, unprotected third-party reuse. Where YMYL claims exist, a professional-review record
must be attached; an absent record is a FAIL. The artifact sits within the current publish ramp level,
and a stop-and-verify evidence record exists. A checklist cell without its evidence is a FAIL, not a
pass [rig-revenue-publish-gate].

The publish-gate verdict vocabulary is closed: REQUEST-APPROVAL or BLOCK. There is no APPROVE in its
output space; approval is the human's typed statement. Traps it guards: any wording that reads like
self-approval, a YMYL artifact without its review record, a missing disclosure label on a renamed tool,
and treating the checklist as a rubber stamp [rig-revenue-publish-gate].

## Hard blocks (any one is a fail regardless of score)

- external link in the body
- more than 3 hashtags
- engagement bait (comment-gated giveaways and the like)
- a creator-class number restated as one of our own results
- more than one post a day implied by the plan
- no photo of self attached and the format is not a document post
- Lane B carrying a mascot, or the minor lane carrying a hook, a warm close or a commercial CTA
- AI-slop cadence left in after the voice pass
- a claim about a client, a metric or a person that cannot be sourced
[scorecard]

## Measured corrections the executable gate encodes

Born from a real flop post-mortem (152 impressions against a 1,000 checkpoint, 1 reaction, an audience
mismatched to the Denver enterprise/healthcare buyer), the executable gate encodes what was measured,
not folklore [li-post-gate]:

- The link penalty attaches to the preview card, not the URL. A bare body URL measured better than
  link-free copy (large-sample industry study). The gate flags a URL and explains the distinction
  rather than banning links outright [li-post-gate].
- Question endings do nothing (a study of roughly 417,550 posts). Close on a claim or a concrete number
  [li-post-gate, hB-04].
- Buyer vocabulary matters: LinkedIn retrieval matches on post vocabulary, so hobby or entertainment
  framing selects an audience that does not buy. The gate flags non-buyer framing and promotional CTAs
  [li-post-gate].
- Boilerplate comment text is flagged on comment artifacts [li-post-gate].
- Proximity patterns over literal keywords: the gate must fire on the artifact it was built from
  [li-post-gate].
- Contract: every finding carries a verb. Exit codes are 0 pass, 1 blocked, 2 usage error. Stdlib only,
  no network and no model inside the gate [li-post-gate].

## Standing prohibitions carried into this gate

No invented numbers, quotes, clients or results. No engagement pods, AI-written comments, hashtag
stacks or automated outreach without approval. No lane voice carried across lanes. Comments on other
people's posts are positive, human-written: validate, add one real insight, optionally point to Mike's
related post. Eden is the publisher of record and the retired Postiz route is never used. Clark material
is synthesis only and never pasted into posts [viral-core].
