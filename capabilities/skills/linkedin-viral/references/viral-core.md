# Viral core: the operating card every LinkedIn job applies

Sources: `tommy-clark-doctrine` (current 2026 rules, cited by video ID) plus RIG standing rules (lanes, Eden, Gate-D, Jev). Where they conflict, the RIG rule wins and the output names the conflict.

## What "viral" means here

Not raw impressions. A post worked when it reached the right buyers and produced a measurable next step: in-ICP profile views, DMs, booked calls, in-ICP followers. Clark would take 5,000 impressions from the right people over 100,000 from the wrong ones [yBKOrgQS-q8]. Judge every post against the account's own baseline, not against other creators.

## The post recipe (seven checks before a draft goes to Eden or to Mike)

1. **Lane and person:** which lane, which single buyer, and which behavior (like, comment, save, DM)? [szN0DF3Oqik]
2. **Content-market fit:** the buyer cares, it sits next to the offer, and Mike holds a receipt. No receipt, no post. [E0uu_GLIkw0]
3. **Moat:** choose one before writing: a real story, proprietary data, or physical proof (photo, artifact, screenshot). [gZwWBtiZ7qE]
4. **Hook:** three variants with two or three levers each (specific number, narrative, negativity, borrowed name or trend, packaging, open loop), in a natural voice a stranger understands. Rank them through Jev. [UjDZLhuEpnQ, SL8yfxUVFKE]
5. **Media:** a real photo or screenshot when one exists; otherwise the Eden asset the lane rules allow. The image matches the copy. [szN0DF3Oqik]
6. **Spine:** does it take a position? Raise safe drafts about 20% (Mike's own posts only; comments on others stay positive). [HFCfw6gznRM]
7. **Next step:** one clear call to action or a reason to follow; a PS-line link if the post may be boosted. [QKNc515DSro]

## The week

- Three strong posts (Clark 2026) or the lane's existing rhythm while it beats baseline: Lane A runs its themed days, Lane B runs `rig-daily-linkedin-post`. The weekly review decides.
- Funnel: one top (story, milestone), one middle (authority, data, framework), one bottom (case study, offer); or one moonshot, one scrappy post, one experiment. [s6KMjhznXqs]
- One planned peak a month: a launch, an event, a stunt, or a big data post. [1je0kCMMstg]
- Bank winners; rerun proven posts after about three months and the origin story quarterly. [0EA0n1Ka41k, fHha-KIZeMg]

## The daily engine (about 30-35 minutes)

- Reply to every comment on Mike's posts within the first hour, ideally with a question.
- Five to ten real comments on buyers, the creators they follow, and competitors: positive, human-written.
- Connection requests to named ICP people inside `linkedin-site-mechanics` throttles (Clark cites about 20 per weekday and about 100 a week as the ceiling). No automated sends without Mike's yes.
- Review profile viewers; warm signals get a short value-first note or a Loom audit, drafted for Mike's approval.

## The first 24 hours after a post

Be present in the first hour and reply. Ask two or three real people for real comments only on big moments. Do not delete a slow post early; distribution is spiky and can arrive hours later [s6KMjhznXqs]. At 24 hours, log one to three learnings in `linkedin-analytics-tracker`.

## The weekly review (30 minutes, and every number drives an action)

- Outlier review of the last five or six posts against baseline, then rank formats, then set next week's slate.
- Buyer mix: who engaged, who viewed the profile, which DMs arrived, what self-reported attribution says.
- Boost candidates: ICP-relevant posts that under-delivered, listed for Mike with a dollar amount (Gate-D).
- Each metric maps to one action: rerun, rewrite the hook, change the media, retire the format, or boost.

## Never

- Invent numbers, quotes, clients or results.
- Use engagement pods, AI-written comments, hashtag stacks, or automated outreach without approval.
- Carry one lane's voice into another.
- Paste Clark transcript text into posts; his material is third-party and the doctrine is synthesis.
