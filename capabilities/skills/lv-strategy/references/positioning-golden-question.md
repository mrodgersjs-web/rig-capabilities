# Positioning: the golden question, content-market fit, which game

Sub-skill of lv-strategy. Clark doctrine is synthesis; bracketed IDs are the source videos named in
the Clark files.

## 1. The golden question

One sentence, written before anything else: why should one specific member of the ICP follow this
account? Everything posted sits inside that answer. Scattered topics are the most common diagnosis
for accounts that stall. [w2_zVhExo1s, tay_EcbOi7E, _YPmpy4fVGw, -WGCvECeTcU, QKNc515DSro]

- Pair it with 1-3 contrarian beliefs about the industry and defend those beliefs repeatedly; they
  become the messaging pillars. [mTXBTznvNZI, _YPmpy4fVGw]
- Aim to be the go-to resource on one topic rooted in real experience. [UKEUZHJUOCM, 5lcZl4iEjI0]
- 2026 refinement: when the goal is pipeline, the niche is a topic next to the product and the
  buyer, not the personality. [s6KMjhznXqs]

## 2. Content-market fit (the admission gate)

A topic earns a slot only where three circles overlap: the buyer cares about it, it sits next to
what the lane sells, and the lane holds credibility on it. [E5vxr1IZCpI, mTXBTznvNZI, vcFh5-fynHc, 1je0kCMMstg]

- Credibility multiplies quality. Without a record, document what is being learned instead of
  prescribing, or put a credible teammate forward. [omkQ61UazZ0, L--kLyKyqa0, LVDaPxHbjwQ]
- Never grow an audience the offer cannot serve. Clark's most retold failure is a client with a
  large audience that would never buy the product. [RpOt0kEWAFc, HFCfw6gznRM, 2XeAckxuAKM]
- RIG application: no receipt, no slot. Receipts are real numbers, artifacts, shipped work or a
  named client story Mike can stand behind.

## 3. Choose the game

Decide what the account is for (leads, recruiting, fundraising, awareness) and weight the pillars
accordingly: lead generation leans on sharp middle-of-funnel opinions, fundraising on momentum
stories, recruiting on culture and hiring posts. [HFCfw6gznRM, vcFh5-fynHc, ftBbMDb91ZA]

- Customers over clout. A smaller audience of buyers beats a large generic one. [XCT5Mubw4AI, Y8ewcq03zxc, vdT9a62LrqA, 1qzSPOPlmuQ]
- Only a small share of buyers are shopping at any moment, so most posts educate the rest. [-WGCvECeTcU]
- RIG trade-off to surface, never to blend silently: Mike's three goals (top influencer, job,
  clients) compete for one profile and one schedule. An account optimized for one goal underperforms
  on the others. Put the choice in front of Mike and record the pick.

## 4. One post, one person

Picture one specific buyer at their desk and decide whether the post should earn a like, a comment
or a save. Most posts address buyers, not fellow founders or marketers. [szN0DF3Oqik, XCT5Mubw4AI]

## 5. Vocabulary and repetition

- Name the frameworks. Prospects repeat coined terms back on sales calls. [TH63A9m1VNA]
- Repeat the same 1-3 core ideas for years. Audiences remember far less than the author assumes, and
  repetition is how a person becomes known for one thing. [2Yv8osegsKY, vcIAAUP6b8Y, szN0DF3Oqik, HFCfw6gznRM]

## 6. Building in public only when the buyer mirrors you

Radical transparency (metrics, legal letters, P&L) pulls customers only when the buyer is a founder
or revenue leader; otherwise it attracts the wrong crowd. Share directional momentum, keep unresolved
hard periods off the feed, and tell resolved struggles rather than live ones. [lnMjrfaT48I, 2XeAckxuAKM, sDuWUBoHsRI, nXY6Tl6-uh0]

## 7. Personality as a moat

One or two personal interests and a relaxed tone prove a human is writing. Keep anecdotes tied to
the buyer's world; off-topic personal posts dilute the audience. [N2b0QVoCEPg, UjDZLhuEpnQ, gZwWBtiZ7qE]

## Strategy document skeleton (personal brand work)

When a lane needs a full strategy document rather than a test, deliver these sections in order and
keep each one self-contained:

1. Short version: the one-sentence positioning.
2. Topic tree: the exhaustible list of angles under the pillars.
3. What you are building: the audience and the outcome.
4. The two poles: the belief the lane defends and the belief it refuses.
5. What to write about: admitted topics with the receipt that backs each.
6. What is already working: market receipts from this run's research only.
7. Your first five posts: ready-to-write hooks.
8. How this could make money: the offer the topics sit next to.

Honesty rules for the document: never invent a creator, post, metric or link; everything cited came
back from a research tool this run; when research comes thin, say so in one line and broaden the
interest; never discard a misfit interest; keep the low point in a story as trust, never spectacle.

## Checklist before a strategy ships

- Golden question names one buyer, one outcome, one receipt.
- Content-market fit is written per topic, not assumed.
- The game is named and Mike has picked the goal that owns the profile.
- Contrarian beliefs are stated as 1-3 sentences the lane will repeat.
- Every cited number carries an evidence class or is cut.
