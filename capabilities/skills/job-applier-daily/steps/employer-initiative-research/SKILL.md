# Employer initiative research

## Inputs
Current JD/persona, confirmed employer history and dated public sources.

## Actions
Research target priorities and relevant former-employer initiatives. Label timing and source uncertainty. Separate company evidence from personal claims. Produce concise questions for unknown participation and separately for inclusion.

## Outputs
Initiative evidence table and claim-bound question queue.

## Gates
No first-person ownership inferred from employer association, code ownership, date overlap or news.

## Recovery
Keep unreachable/stale sources as gaps. Reuse only source-bound research after freshness review.
