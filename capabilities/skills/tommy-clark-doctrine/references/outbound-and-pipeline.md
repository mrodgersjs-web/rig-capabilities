# Profile, outbound engine, and pipeline

Sub-skill of `tommy-clark-doctrine`. Synthesis; bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims.

## 1. The profile is a landing page (set once, 5-10 minutes)

- A clear, real headshot (no AI portrait).
- Headline: role, company, and one line of value for a named audience.
- About: a handful of sentences written by you: what you do, proof, what readers get, one call to action. (He said 4-6 sentences in 2025 and 5-10 earlier.)
- Featured: one or two links (three at most), each pointing at the next step: book a call, the newsletter or a lead magnet, a case study. Images work like thumbnails with value and proof baked in.
- Profile button (Premium) points at the same next step. Tag every link for tracking.
- Visitors check recent activity, so going quiet costs conversions.

## 2. Audience design: the daily engine (about 30-35 minutes)

- **Connection requests:** about 20 per weekday (he cites roughly 100 a week as the platform ceiling), sent without a note unless you have actually met, favoring people with mutual connections and people who posted in the last 30 days (a Sales Navigator filter). Earlier versions said 20-30 a day (July 2024) and 20-25 (March 2025). [w2_zVhExo1s, mJ1c1PHBoo4, Fvl_wMtcz24, 8VqGXuFxd3Q, gZwWBtiZ7qE]
- **Comments:** five to ten thoughtful comments a day beat 20-50 lazy ones. Targets: buyers, the creators buyers follow, and competitors. An older variant spread them three each across buyers, industry voices and broad business accounts. Keep a sheet of 15-30 target accounts; 15-20 minutes a day, 30 at most. [SL8yfxUVFKE, mJ1c1PHBoo4, cWfOlNGZ6eg, szN0DF3Oqik]
- **Never AI-written comments.** He would rather see no comment than a machine one. [8nBIAUSIRnQ, UjDZLhuEpnQ]
- If buyers never post, comment where they read. [szN0DF3Oqik]
- Send a connection request to every booked prospect before the call. [-WGCvECeTcU]
- 2026 note: his agency automates requests (a lead list into HeyReach, about 15 a day, auto-withdraw if ignored) and reports acceptance above 50% on engager-sourced lists. See the RIG overlay below. [UjDZLhuEpnQ, szN0DF3Oqik]

## 3. Converting warm signals

- Signals: profile viewers (checked daily), people who engaged with a post (exported with tools such as Trigify), and people who engaged with a boosted post. [29hBCF7N7Jg, E5vxr1IZCpI]
- The move: a personal Loom of about 60-90 seconds (up to 2-3 minutes) that opens with proof, gives one or two quick wins from a look at their own material, and closes softly. One to five a day. [E5vxr1IZCpI, mJ1c1PHBoo4, TH63A9m1VNA]
- Nurture in DMs by referencing a specific recent post, giving value, and holding the pitch; act like a friend, not a solicitor. [nASaJrw0TW0, LVDaPxHbjwQ]
- Message only warm signals. Cold outbound alone is an uphill fight. [MXBaHCO4WI8]

## 4. Launch and fundraise playbook

1. **Announcement:** the amount or milestone in the opening and in the image, the founding story, what the money or launch enables; tag investors and key customers who were briefed in advance.
2. **Engagement lift:** brief supporters beforehand, then send the live link within 30-60 minutes framed as an opportunity, not a favor, with an easy way to decline.
3. **Echo chamber:** founder, company page and five to seven original team posts (no shared script), optionally investors, over one to two days; the main post gets reposted about ten hours later.
4. **Demand capture:** turn engagers, site visitors and team-post engagers into enriched outreach lists.
5. Prepare at least a month ahead.

## 5. Lead magnets and owned audience

- Comment-a-keyword for a genuinely useful asset about once a month, then follow up in DMs. [8VqGXuFxd3Q, mTXBTznvNZI]
- A newsletter is the owned exit from rented reach. [sDuWUBoHsRI, aL-6NagH7cM]

## 6. Measurement that drives action

- Ask every lead how they heard about you; do not obsess over per-post tracking links. [lCeE49rDcH0, E0uu_GLIkw0]
- Every week, review the last five or six posts against the account's own baseline and double down on the formats that beat it. Rerun winners verbatim after about three months; rerun the origin story quarterly. [E0uu_GLIkw0, 0EA0n1Ka41k, fHha-KIZeMg]
- Write one to three learnings 24 hours after each post. [Fvl_wMtcz24]
- Check who is paying attention: profile viewers, ad demographics, the company report. He would take 5,000 impressions from the right people over 100,000 from the wrong ones. [QKNc515DSro, yBKOrgQS-q8]
- First 90 days: leading metrics. After that: inbound DMs, self-reported leads, and win rate among content consumers. [L--kLyKyqa0]
- Inbound is lumpy week to week; organic alone is too volatile to be an entire go-to-market. [szN0DF3Oqik]
