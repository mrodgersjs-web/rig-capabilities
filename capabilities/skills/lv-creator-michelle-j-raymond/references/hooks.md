# Hooks and openings (Michelle J Raymond)

She is not a formula-library hook teacher. Her hook doctrine is a small set of principles plus slot skeletons, and she refuses score-flex templates and copied viral lines outright [nC06xaoVWPw, Dd42KDThdPY]. Where other creators teach three drafted variants per post, her testing move is thinner: ship one risky post, then sort what the winners shared [nC06xaoVWPw]. Treat the slots below as empty frames for Mike's own facts.

## Principles

- **The strongest sentence goes first.** The opening line decides whether anyone taps to read the rest, so the body is wasted when the first line is flat [Dd42KDThdPY].
- **Lines one and two carry an opinion or a sharp question.** Neutral, corporate-safe openings are the default failure mode; a bold stance or a real question stops the scroll [Dd42KDThdPY].
- **Open inside the first 150 characters.** The named moment, problem or claim belongs above the fold, not after a warm-up [xR6t2llZRKQ].
- **The remove-name test.** With the author's name hidden, a reader should still know who wrote it. Word choice and signature details carry the identity [Dd42KDThdPY].
- **Meet the reader where their day is.** An opening built on their constraint beats an opening built on your announcement [udtNia_2Onk].
- **An image, GIF or news item can be the hook** when the line is not the stop [Dd42KDThdPY, _HK3z6D0SRY].

## Slot skeletons (fill with your own story and numbers, never copy an example)

- Mid-story open: begin inside a scene, then their situation, then the proof, then one ask [xR6t2llZRKQ].
- Contrarian stance about a common practice the industry treats as settled [Dd42KDThdPY].
- Their constraint is why your announcement can wait [udtNia_2Onk].
- Wasted time with no result, then what that does to their confidence, then the fix that fits their day (problem-agitation-solution shape) [2JI55PLI9Zw].
- Confession-style challenge to a behavior the reader thinks is hidden, then the real cost of it [hlAoLfF7t2A].
- First-post intro: name the show or source you listened to, admit where you were stuck, then say what people can expect from you [_7hbfgS9lTw].
- Poll question aimed at one named role's hardest problem [k3dCMwu8Nmk].
- Event or talk post: say what you will argue there, then name the event. Never open with attendance alone [p_jnafHMun4].
- Loss-framed pull, then a story whose main character is the customer, not the product [rW1CGbmLvzA].

## Where hooks come from

- **Harvest, do not copy.** Competitors' opening lines are research material in an audit; the move is to see what stops the reader in your niche and then write your own [x3d6HUw2T6s].
- **Winners share traits, not templates.** In her reading, strong posts share an opening line with tension, a real photo, or a payload the reader keeps; score-flex templates are to be dropped [nC06xaoVWPw].
- **A model can critique a weak hook.** She uses AI to review an opening that is not landing, but holds that a model cannot invent the story you lived [Dd42KDThdPY, nC06xaoVWPw].
- **Opinion without punching down.** The intensity dial is real, and the target is industry habits and your past self, never a weaker party [nC06xaoVWPw, Dd42KDThdPY].

## Limits of her hook teaching

- No character-count spec beyond the 150-character open, no A/B test protocol, and no hook-ranking rubric anywhere in the 52 videos.
- She pushes back on hook-obsession while the buyer is still unknown: research first, then the hook [R0OE2jitOo0].
