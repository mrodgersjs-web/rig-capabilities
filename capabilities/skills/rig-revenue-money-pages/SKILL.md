# Money pages (one mechanism)

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

Governing contracts: rig-revenue-meta. One page per service — a single services menu page is the

## Step 1. Page inventory

One money page per service, plus per use case, per client type, per comparison ("X vs Y",
"X alternatives") and per location the firm genuinely serves. Target shape: 20-40 decision pages
for a full install (CLAIM: NAASPeprKPc) — but every page must earn its place with unique substance
(rig-revenue-forced-variables), or it does not get built.

## Step 2. Page framework (per page)

1. Headline = service-or-problem + location ("emergency AC repair in Miami" class).
2. First 3-5 lines answer: what it is, who it's for, what it costs or how pricing works (publish
   pricing where the firm approves — retrievability rule, rig-revenue-meta rule 5).
3. Who it's for / who it's not for — filter honestly.
4. Features/how-it-works + benefits paired with proof (real reviews, approved cases, named expert).
5. FAQ block (rig-revenue-answer-first), correct schema.
6. CTA ×3: above the fold, mid-page, bottom (book/call/consult).

## Step 3. Wire the loop

Money page ↔ best supporting articles both directions (rig-revenue-silo-architecture). Decision
pages feed rig-revenue-ai-citation-gaps (they are the AI-recommendation material).

## Step 4. Compliance pass

Protected titles → credential check + escalation (rig-revenue-meta). YMYL claims → professional
review slot. Nothing ships through rig-revenue-publish-gate without it.
