# Type Hierarchy And Mood

Purpose: mood levers inside one family, opacity tiers, scale dominance, one attention anchor.

Load when: the task touches this part of typography, or a ds-typography critique names it. Part of `ds-typography`.

## Checklist
1. Work through every law below against the current artifact before calling the sub-topic done.
2. Cite the CLAIM when a law drives a decision; a law you cannot cite is labeled own reasoning.
3. Where creators disagree (see the SKILL.md), state both positions and test rather than assume.

## Anti-patterns for this sub-topic
- Stretching one width of a family to imitate another width; stems and stroke contrast break. CLAIM(9ut7EmnYG7I@00:00)
- Insufficient contrast: hairline weights in body copy, or type over a busy photo. CLAIM(9ut7EmnYG7I@03:59, aOPGqOOi__4@01:00)
- Random capitalization inside headings and paragraphs. CLAIM(9ut7EmnYG7I@10:59)
- Centered paragraphs, centered bullet lists, forced justification, widows and orphans. CLAIM(aOPGqOOi__4@19:30, QrNi9FmdlxY@01:59)
