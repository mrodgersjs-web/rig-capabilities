# Worked examples: preserve what the evidence means

These are Mike-specific adaptations based on the derivative references in [the source map](source-map.md). The figures are candidate-reported; this package does not independently audit them. Recheck the current historical sources before using them. Employer dates and claim periods remain source fields, not embedded assumptions.

## Actuant: business result before technology

Supported in `RET-26`: 20% cost reduction through restructuring operations around market-based customer value streams. A separate statement concerns integrating a $12 million acquired business. The cost baseline/period and meaning of the business-size measure need confirmation. No AI mechanism is established.

**Original practice answer:**

“At Actuant, I reduced costs by 20% by restructuring operations around customer value streams. The change was in how the operation worked around customer needs. That experience is relevant to this role because I would start with the operating result and connect process and technology decisions to it.”

Do not insert AI into the historical mechanism. Keep the acquired business separate unless a source establishes its relationship to the restructuring. Do not say Mike paid $12 million or created $12 million of savings. Preparation questions: which cost base, what period, and what was Mike's exact integration responsibility?

## Caterpillar: stock, not an annual savings stream

Supported in `RET-27`: inventory reduction of more than $200,000, with startup logistics, engine/parts capacity, and supplier-flow context. The precise personal action, causal mechanism, and period need confirmation.

**Original practice answer:**

“At Caterpillar, I reduced inventory by more than $200,000 while working on logistics, capacity and supplier flow. That gives me relevant operating experience. In grocery, I would apply that discipline with retail specialists to examine availability and working capital. I would not present that industrial experience as a past grocery engagement.”

“While working on” preserves context; it does not invent how the reduction happened. Do not annualize the amount, call it recurring profit, or claim a new forecasting system without evidence. Ask what Mike personally changed before writing a mechanism-specific resume bullet.

## Advocate Aurora: prototype milestone

Supported in `RET-24`: Mike built 83 Tech Harbor; by the end of 2019, the team had four projects in prototype.

**Original practice answer:**

“At Advocate Aurora, I built 83 Tech Harbor to develop growth opportunities into businesses. By the end of 2019, our team had four projects in prototype. That gives me a relevant starting point for an emerging mandate: I would make the next investment depend on evidence from a bounded test.”

The milestone is four projects in prototype. Do not replace it with four scaled companies, achieved revenue, or successful acquisitions. The proposed investment approach is not a claim that each historical investment followed that process.

## A direct-industry gap

Requirement: established grocery leadership. The available evidence describes industrial operations and healthcare technology.

**Original practice answer:**

“My strongest evidence is in industrial operations and healthcare technology. I would bring that operating discipline alongside grocery specialists. I would want to understand how essential prior grocery leadership is to this mandate, and where the team needs a leader who can connect business priorities with delivery.”

Fit status remains adjacent evidence or gap. A credible approach does not satisfy a tenure or direct-experience requirement by itself.
