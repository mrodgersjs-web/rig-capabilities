# Before/after examples

## Ideal-achievement working draft first

**IDEAL-ACHIEVEMENT WORKING DRAFT — PENDING MIKE'S EVIDENCE REVIEW**

| JD requirement | Polished working bullet | Mike supplies |
|---|---|---|
| Improve working capital while preserving availability | Reduced [inventory carrying cost] by [confirmed amount] through [actual intervention], across [scope] during [period]. | Actual employer, personal ownership, meaning of the amount, intervention, scope and period |
| Turn strategy into adoption and operating impact | Converted [business priority] into [implemented operating change], achieving [confirmed outcome] through [actual delivery and adoption mechanism]. | Actual employer/role, delivered change, actual outcome and mechanism |

These patterns choose the ideal achievement before the evidence-mapping stage. They are not representations that Mike completed the stated events. Keep the draft label outside the bullet; do not force resume patterns into “I would” interview wording. When Mike says “I've done all of that,” leave the factual fields pending until he supplies their specific values. Generate the draft without making those answers a prerequisite.

## Repairing sourced history

Examples illustrate editing decisions; they are not independently verified resume facts. Source pointers and provenance are in the sibling [source map](../../lucy-executive-evidence/references/source-map.md). Actual titles and dates must come from the current historical source.

| Before: claim needing repair | Draft after, within available evidence | Review note |
|---|---|---|
| Delivered 20% AI savings and a $12M acquisition at Actuant. | Reduced costs by 20% by restructuring operations around market-based customer value streams. | `RET-26`: candidate-reported. AI is unsupported; baseline and period are unknown. Keep the separate integration statement separate. |
| Acquired a business for $12M. | Integrated a $12 million acquired business. | `RET-26`: the figure describes business size; it does not establish purchase price or the exact size measure. Confirm that measure and Mike's integration scope before adding detail. |
| Saved $200K annually by deploying predictive AI at Caterpillar. | Reduced inventory by more than $200,000 at Caterpillar. | `RET-27`: inventory stock reduction; annualization and predictive AI are unsupported. The personal action/mechanism remains a fact question. Put the employer in its normal role heading if already clear. |
| Scaled four successful healthcare companies. | Built 83 Tech Harbor at Advocate Aurora; the team had four projects in prototype by the end of 2019. | `RET-24`: preserve team attribution and prototype stage. Do not imply scaled revenue, launch, or success beyond the milestone. |

If the source confirms only participation, use the supported participation verb instead of claiming sole delivery. If it confirms a specific personal action, incorporate that action without copying a larger team result into personal ownership.

Proposed interview approach: “I would pilot a replenishment exception workflow with store teams.” A working pattern could read: “Improved [confirmed availability measure] through [actual replenishment intervention], across [scope] during [period].” Keep it under the ideal-achievement draft label, with employer and ownership fields pending. Even if Mike likes the wording, it becomes application-ready history only after he confirms the actual event and supported facts.
