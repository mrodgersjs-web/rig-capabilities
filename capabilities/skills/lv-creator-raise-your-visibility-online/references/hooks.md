# Hooks (Louise Brogan)

Openings are a search field and a stop, not a diary. The same noun should lead the headline, the post, and the video's first seconds. Slot skeletons only. Do not copy a caption.

Do not open with a diary line or with attendance [K_sGlIEm_7E, 4VoP0RnZ5H4]. Name the topic in the first line [5Hjq6U4xziI]. For a news take, say what it means for the buyer, not that it shipped [ji2Zr7zsZ_c].
