# Review engine (one mechanism)

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

Governing contracts: rig-revenue-meta. Compliance is the product: the reply that keeps a firm out
of trouble is the same one that helps AI recommend it (CLAIM: AUlMzRPDdRw).

## Step 1. Ask

Short personal ask after each job (QR/link direct to the review form). Steady velocity beats a
stale pile — frequency lets a smaller profile overtake a bigger one (CLAIM: kvrlyPiWxDs). Never
buy, seed, or incentivize reviews (excluded tactic).

## Step 2. Reply to every review — compliance rules (all mandatory)

1. Never confirm a client relationship or case details (HIPAA for clinics; confidentiality for
   law/CPA). Thank without specifics.
2. Restate the service naturally ("glad the water heater repair went well") — a keyword surface
   done honestly (CLAIM: aXhO5hhUqeE).
3. Never name a reviewer negatively, never argue facts in public. 1-star: apologize, move it
   offline ("Please reach out so we can make it right." — CLAIM: wO_8Gcw2kr8).
4. Sensitive details a reviewer didn't volunteer first are never added ("none of it feels like a
   violation at 9pm. It reads like one to a stranger 3 months later" — CLAIM: AUlMzRPDdRw).

## Step 3. Drafts-first

Reply drafts route through rig-revenue-publish-gate (human approval) — corpus automation for
replies exists inside PAID ENDORSEMENT products (Arvow review automation); a governed operator
keeps a human on every reply for regulated firms.

## Step 4. Read the signal

Reviews are copy research: mine real vocabulary, fears, outcomes for rig-revenue-forced-variables
and page copy ("in a language their customers actually use" — CLAIM: 0l_weqm-GX4).
