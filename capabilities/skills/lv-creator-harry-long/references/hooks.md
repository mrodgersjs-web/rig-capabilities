## Why the first lines get the time

He treats the opening as a two-second decision. On 19 July 2026 he said he had studied over 10,000 posts; three days later the same study was "over 1,000." Use the later, smaller figure when citing the study, and label it a creator claim either way [_ddwk79BB3s, fWhBOPC1eLA]. ## Five rules (19 July 2026)

Name one role. Open a question or a gap. Hint at the payoff without spoiling it. Match the proof you actually have. Write as you talk, not as a press release [_ddwk79BB3s]. Questions that earn a real reply beat comment-a-word bait [_ddwk79BB3s, fWhBOPC1eLA].
