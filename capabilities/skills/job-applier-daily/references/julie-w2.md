# Julie W2 portable adaptation

Adapted from the canonical julie-w2-applypilot skill, version 1.1.0. This reference carries its application quality and coordination principles. It does not carry historical action grants, private answers, credential locations, outreach permissions, or assumptions about a specific browser/runtime.

Applications and receipt reconciliation come first. Submitted means an actual employer or ATS receipt, not a click, OTP, generated document, or worker assertion. Keep the original resume history and format. Tailor only from supported facts. An independent reviewer inspects the current final documents and extracted text with their exact hashes before upload.

Use no more than three workers and one coordinator ledger writer. Assign distinct job packets to workers; the author cannot be final verifier. Keep a visible task list with stable ID, owner, state, next action, and evidence or blocker. Verify returned artifact hashes. Compute totals from reconciled unique receipts. End idle, blocked, or handoff when workers stop.

Check current and prior employment against confirmed resume history and employer aliases. A client, investment, partnership, or public company initiative is not proof of employment or personal contribution. Unknown screening answers, attestations, and declarations stay pending Mike's response. Never copy historical private-answer files into this package.

Respect live browser ownership errors and platform restrictions. Pause the affected operation, preserve its identifiers, and require the appropriate current human release. Do not create another browser space to bypass a pause. Continue independent permitted preparation where possible.

The portable daily extension requires specific application approval every time. It grants no outreach, account creation/recovery, credential change, paid service, or schedule activation authority. Host and current-task permissions govern those actions separately.
