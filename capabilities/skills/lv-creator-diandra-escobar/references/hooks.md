# Hooks (Escobar, 2026)

A hook has one job: earn the click from a person and a read the retrieval model can score. She treats that as two audiences at once, not a copy trick. [Zc4E_K48v48, PxjDCR29aKc]

## Three jobs, body first

Every opening does one of three jobs: open a curiosity gap, challenge a belief, or promise a specific value. If it does none of those, format will not save it. [BPbf3JUm_dw, ZAqIUNAvXXo, Zc4E_K48v48]

Write the body first, with the numbers and the mechanism, then mine the body for the line that creates the gap between what the reader expects and what you can actually claim. If nothing in the body is worth pulling up, rewrite the post. Do not decorate a thin draft. [ZAqIUNAvXXo, Zc4E_K48v48, _ZFncQNrOhw]

On growth posts, the brand, the news event, or the known person sits in the headline as the pattern interrupt. The insight after that interrupt has to be yours. A summary of what happened is not a hook. [twBrrKkhpOg, KlawrDJuqYc]

## Four shapes, and a fifth she later dropped

On 2026-05-28 she collapsed 131 outlier hooks (her count, from 21 creators across several B2B niches, over about 3 years of her own hook writing) into five shapes. [Zc4E_K48v48]

1. **Dense.** Packed to the fold, about three mobile lines. Slot: `[specific number] + [pain the buyer already feels]`.
2. **Punchy plus context.** A short claim, a blank line, then the line that earns the click. She calls this the highest hit rate of the set (creator claim, no rate published). Slot: `[short claim]` / `[why it matters to one audience]`.
3. **Single-line bomb.** One tense line. Highest variance (creator claim). On mobile it collapses unless you put two manual line breaks under it. [Zc4E_K48v48]
4. **Stacked.** Two to four parallel lines with a real series, not a list of slogans. Slot: `[before state]` / `[after state]`, or `[number] [lessons]` with the last line implied.
5. **Hybrid.** A custom mix of the above. Named on 2026-05-28 only.

On 2026-07-21 she restates four repeating shapes and does not name hybrid as its own format. Current list for this doctrine: dense, punchy plus context, single-line bomb, stacked. Hybrid is optional craft, not a fifth rule. [_ZFncQNrOhw, Zc4E_K48v48]

How-I slots, not filled examples: `[number] [artifacts] I [tested] over [timeframe], what that taught [one audience]`. `We changed [process] after [failure]. Here is what we do now.` `I [made a large change] and lost [a counted thing]. Why I would do it again.` [a4uL3wTtjFk]

## The fold is pixels, and the model reads the opening

She claims LinkedIn lays out the mobile fold by pixels, not characters: about 110 width units a line, and a wide letter near four times a narrow one, so there is no fixed character cap. Creator claim, unverified. Preview in a post formatter before you publish, because a doc tool and the feed do not share spacing. [Zc4E_K48v48, ZAqIUNAvXXo]

What the model reads is stated three ways. Do not merge them:

- 2026-02-12: three lines before the see-more control. [BPbf3JUm_dw]
- 2026-04-10: retrieval truncates to about the first 60 tokens, which she glosses as roughly 45 to 50 words, and that slice decides whether you are even a candidate. [PxjDCR29aKc]
- 2026-05-28 and 2026-07-21: the model reads about the first 40 to 50 words, and a failed hook kills both curiosity and reach together (creator claim, unverified). [Zc4E_K48v48, _ZFncQNrOhw]

Operator rule that survives the drift: topic and audience in the first line. No slow setup. Audit the opening of the last five posts before you ship the next one. [PxjDCR29aKc]

She also claims hook playbooks from 2023 and 2024 now hurt, because the feed learned the old shapes. Creator claim, unverified. Do not revive a dated template just because it once traveled. [Zc4E_K48v48]

## How she tests

Test 10 to 15 hook lines in plain text before you spend design time. A text post is the cheap lab. [Z3jHnYT8iJo]

If you hand a model a hook job, give it the full post so the hookable facts are in context. Then apply judgment. Do not accept the first line it returns. [Zc4E_K48v48, KlawrDJuqYc]

A growth hook still has to pass her nervousness test for hot takes: if posting the claim does not make you slightly uneasy, it is probably a summary. That test is for your own post, not a license to jab someone else's. [twBrrKkhpOg]

Do not paste her client stories (brand frames, founder name-drops, impression counts) into Mike's posts. Those numbers are her claims.

Clark's 2026 hook rule is the same spine (body first, natural voice, formula phrasing reads as AI) with a different count: two or three stacked levers, three drafts. Use her four shapes as a menu, not as a requirement to stack all of them. Her single-line bomb fights Lane B, which needs the receipt visible without a tap. Prefer dense or punchy-plus-context when the buyer has to see the number above the fold.
