# Engagement (Pierre Herubel)

His engagement position moves over time, and the current rule depends on stage. The ledger is at the bottom; read it before quoting any single line.

## What he teaches today

- Stage one (first 2,000 followers): engage far more than you post, and make every comment substantive rather than generic. He claims a good comment can out-impress your own post (5,000 to 10,000 impressions, creator claim) and that your headline on a comment is free advertising [4OVxO2j24sw].
- Quality bar: skip a day rather than forcing a comment. Never comment bots, never visibly automated engagement, and never the auto-DMs triggered by someone liking a post, which he names as a trust-eroder [DB-P9AUogXU, 6RJb6qYq1gA].
- Two target lists: about 20 industry influencers and 10 to 20 ICP accounts per week (creator claim on the counts), commented on genuinely [DB-P9AUogXU].
- Replies, comments and useful DMs sustained over months build the familiarity that later makes outreach warm; he claims about six months of relationship building before outreach stops being cold (creator claim) [mV1Ma2ZVgU8].
- His own replies land within 24 to 48 hours as a business practice, not as a growth tactic [vzQrN8BVE9c].

## DMs and conversation

- Daily DM quota: 10 per working day, which he counts as 100 messages over 10 working days (creator claim), found through search or Sales Navigator. Connect and converse first; never pitch in the first message [5wmCL8tA04I].
- In phase two of growth, DMs to mapped industry names are relationship building, not pitching [4OVxO2j24sw].
- Engagement data is an intent signal for who to talk to, not the growth engine itself [BkdOFSfFPD4].
- The nine-tactic follower playbook treats weekly commenting on influencer and ICP lists as one checklist item among nine, alongside profile and cadence fixes [DB-P9AUogXU].

## What he rejects

- Commenting marathons and mass connection-request blasts as a growth strategy, rejected explicitly in July 2025 after he had taught comment lists in January 2025 [BkdOFSfFPD4, DB-P9AUogXU].
- The hyperactive posting archetype: replying to every comment for hours is a waste, and overposting with pitches in every post reads as desperation [6RJb6qYq1gA].
- All-day engagement as a substitute for positioning; consistency without attention design wastes the effort [c_2g0KM7fqY].

## Debate design on your own posts

- An infographic built with deliberately simplified figures invites experts to correct the percentages, and those corrections become reply threads that lift the post. He applies this to expert posts and refuses it on personal story posts [Io7Uq-S8dpk].
- Polls double as audience research: ask, then serve the winning topic in the next post [CphfSIw7WV8].
- A numbered rules post invites practitioners to react rule by rule [CphfSIw7WV8].
- Small engagement bait he uses: inviting readers to name their entry offer in the comments for feedback [7NTNAIErphE].

## Dated ledger of his engagement position

- Jan 2025: weekly comment lists on 20 influencers and 10 to 20 ICP accounts, plus the daily DM quota [DB-P9AUogXU, 5wmCL8tA04I].
- Jul 2025: commenting marathons and connection blasts rejected; engagement data demoted to an intent signal [BkdOFSfFPD4].
- Jan 2026: commenting returns for beginners at roughly 10 times posting volume, smart rather than generic; he does not reconcile this with the July 2025 rejection beyond the stage difference [4OVxO2j24sw].
