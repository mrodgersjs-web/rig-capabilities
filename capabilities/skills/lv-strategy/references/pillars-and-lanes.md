# Pillars and lanes: the slot system

Sub-skill of lv-strategy. Pillars turn content decisions into slot-filling instead of daily
reinvention. This file owns the pillar design; lv-plan owns which date each slot lands on.

## Pillar procedure (3-5 pillars, never more)

1. Inventory the receipts first: what can be proven with real numbers, artifacts, shipped work or
   named stories? A pillar with no receipt source dies here.
2. Cluster the themes against the audience's top jobs-to-be-done, not against Mike's interests.
3. Draft 3-5 pillars. Five is the ceiling; more dilutes every one of them.
4. Give each pillar its six fields (below).
5. Map the weekly slots: which pillar owns which post. Hand the slot map to lv-plan; do not date it here.

## The six fields every pillar carries

| field | what it must contain |
|---|---|
| name | short and specific enough to say out loud |
| thesis | one line stating the belief this pillar defends |
| formats | the 2-3 post shapes it supports (story, framework, data, milestone) |
| cadence share | how often it earns a slot, as a share of the week |
| proof source | the receipts that power it (metrics, war stories, artifacts) |
| kill criteria | a numeric retirement rule, for example: retire if engagement stays under the account baseline for 8 consecutive posts |

## Lane map (the rules that travel with each lane)

| lane | who publishes | voice | boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: personal brand | Mike's themed daily stream, generated in Eden | warm Ted Lasso register, pop-culture opening allowed | Birch Tuesday-only; The Coach only from behind or in silhouette; imagery by day per the Lane A skill |
| B: RIG B2B | Rodgers Intelligence Group | evidence-led, operational authority, named receipts | no mascots, no em dashes, no banned words; the proprietary cast never appears; AntiGenericForce at or above 85 on finished copy |
| Client: Ideawake | Coby Skonord's account | Coby's voice | the browser may be signed in as Coby, so assert the handle before acting; nothing fires without Mike's explicit client approval |
| Job search | Mike's profile for recruiters | plain, credential-forward | profile edits shown as exact before/after values and applied only after Mike approves |
| Minor (Jacob) | out of scope | n/a | STOP here; route to the minor-lane skill; never auto-scheduled |

## Pillar quality checks

- Every pillar has a receipt source named. "Experience" is not a proof source.
- Kill criteria are numeric, so the weekly review can act on them without discussion.
- The pillars together answer the golden question; a pillar that serves a different audience gets cut.
- Cadence shares sum to the lane's weekly slot count, and two pillars do not fight for the same slot.
- The slot map handed to lv-plan states pillar, format and job per slot, and nothing else.

## Common failure modes

- Pillars built from what Mike likes talking about instead of what the buyer will pay to solve.
- Six or more pillars, which is just unstructured posting with extra steps.
- Kill criteria written as adjectives ("if it underperforms") that no weekly review can execute.
- One lane's pillar system reused for another lane's account.
