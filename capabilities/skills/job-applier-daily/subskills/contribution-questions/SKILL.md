# Contribution questions

## Inputs
Source-backed initiative, employment timing, claim ID and proposed wording with draft hash.

## Actions
Ask “Did you personally work on this initiative? What did you do, in which role and period, and what result can we support?” Then ask “May I include that confirmed contribution in this role's resume, cover letter, both, or neither?” Capture the two decisions separately and preserve corrections.

## Outputs
Participation record and inclusion record with actual responses and scope.

## Gates
“Looks good” approves neither an unspecified event nor invented numbers. Employer overlap is not ownership. Withheld inclusion excludes the claim.

## Recovery
Keep questions bounded to missing facts. If unknown or declined, use supported alternatives and keep the gap visible.
