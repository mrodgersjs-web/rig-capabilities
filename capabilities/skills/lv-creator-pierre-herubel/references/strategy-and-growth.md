# Strategy and growth (Pierre Herubel)

## Positioning

- The one-one rule: one audience, one expertise, one offer, with a 20-second clarity test (can a stranger say what you do in 20 seconds). Dated shift: his Jan 2025 formulation was one audience, one offer, one channel; by Jan 2026 the channel had become its own step and expertise replaced it in the triple [wbVwUpopQgI, mV1Ma2ZVgU8].
- Niche B2B does not need a broad audience, and broad funnel-stage content (his dismissal of the classic top/middle/bottom funnel model) attracts unqualified followers who stop engaging the moment you sell [zVC1HEH9060, 2HSavr17yq0].
- Content pillars plus a point of view plus a named enemy your work protects clients from. His pillar recipe: three core pillars (industry context, your expertise, your work in progress or signature concept) plus one personal pillar, and posts matching none of them get refused [vzQrN8BVE9c, BkdOFSfFPD4, c_2g0KM7fqY].
- The personal-brand iceberg: followers are the visible tip; the submerged layers are focus, trial-and-error experience, deep expertise producing a signature concept, and strategic content creation. Copying the visible part produces nothing [2QbMkODGVPw].
- Five personal-brand strategies he derives from studying 100+ brands (creator claim): storyteller (talk to who your reader was 3 to 5 years ago), builder (build in public), expert teacher (lead with visual frameworks), opinion leader (zoom out on the industry, needs long experience), and representative (speak for a defined group, earned through legitimacy, never self-declared) [ZoE84jNZ4gQ]. He reports running the expert-teacher strategy himself for three years [ZoE84jNZ4gQ].
- The eight posting patterns that leak your business state: beginner, phantom, hyperactive, megaphone, trend hopper, philosopher, hidden salesman, and industry authority as the target state. Diagnosis first, then behave like the authority: teach from experience, pair theory with cases, sell rarely and indirectly [6RJb6qYq1gA].

## Offers and revenue

- The front-end offer system: one low-risk entry purchase (his examples: a $300 one-hour call or a $1,000 audit, creator claims), delivered inside 1 to 2 weeks with tangible results and a written recommendation list, then upsell to implementation ($4K to $15K projects or $3K/month, creator claims) and offer a lighter downsell if the big one is refused [7NTNAIErphE, h9BQP6-IzZo].
- Four entry formats he names: audit plus action plan, a 1 to 2 week implementation sprint, a mini-course with implementation support, a one-hour strategy call [h9BQP6-IzZo].
- A menu of offers creates decision paralysis; he illustrates it with three price points in one view and argues for one obvious next step instead [7NTNAIErphE, h9BQP6-IzZo].
- Show the offer in every profile slot: banner with an outcome tagline and client logos, headline with what you do and for whom, an About section in problem-agitate-solution form (include the price when it is low), a Featured tile linking one landing page with one offer, a Services entry with visual testimonials, and an image link in the experience entry [7NTNAIErphE, 3sf8fojVfpM, h9BQP6-IzZo].
- Bridge assets: a roughly 10-minute video sales letter (problem and fit, how the system solves it, three customer results, optional guarantee, one call CTA) and a customer survey run on at least 10 customers at 1, 3, 6 and 12 month checkpoints (creator claims) [5wmCL8tA04I].

## Profile as a landing page

- Seven slots, audited quarterly: photo (clear, natural, face centered, 400x400 pixels), banner, headline, About, Featured, Services, Experience [3sf8fojVfpM].
- Headline in one short sentence covering what you do, who you help and the outcome; never a bare job title. Draft three and poll 10 to 20 people (creator claim on the sample size) [3sf8fojVfpM].
- Three visitor outcomes to design for: contact you, click through to a tracked link, or get prospected by you through Premium profile-visitor review [3sf8fojVfpM].

## Growth phases and repurposing

- Phase model (Jan 2026): 0 to 2,000 followers is foundations (one audience, one problem, one offer, 30 days of short authority content, engagement 10 times posting volume); 2,000 to 5,000 is authority (mine your last 30 to 60 posts for what worked, move to one-idea infographics, connect with the named people in your niche); 5,000 to 10,000 is systems (a repurposing engine, wider format variety on one voice, about 5 peer collaboration posts, and a signature format studied from big creators at their growth stage, not their current stage) [4OVxO2j24sw].
- Repurposing math (creator claims): one strong insight into 10 to 15 formats, a 30/70 split between creating and repurposing, and a six-month log of what you tried and what failed [4OVxO2j24sw, 9o4BqmpgVT8].
- His own growth record as reported: 2,000 followers in Jan 2022 to 170,000 in 2026, 50M to 60M impressions, results starting after about four months and a six-month flat stretch in between (all creator claims, and the numbers climb across videos) [vzQrN8BVE9c, mc7nK3PYgjw, 9o4BqmpgVT8].

## Outbound

- Warm outbound only: publish expertise to the ICP for at least a month at 3+ posts per week, offer a tracked content upgrade, log every touch (post engagement, profile visit, download, email open and forward) in a CRM, and score it: like or comment +1, profile visit +2, download +3, email open +2, forward +4 (creator claims). Message the top of the list manually at a score of roughly 15 to 20, referencing exactly what they consumed, and offer a case study as the next step [IAn0_pfzero].
- Case-study content is the bridge into a sales conversation; the message is short and never a cold pitch [IAn0_pfzero, BkdOFSfFPD4].
- Permission-based selling: use profile views and engagement as intent signals, open a real conversation, and ask before sending anything like a case study [BkdOFSfFPD4].

## Team-led content (his 2026 reversal)

- June 2026: he says a saturated feed makes the solo personal brand a wall-hitting play and he would not start with a personal brand today. The replacement is 3 to 5 coordinated company voices (typically founder, a product VP, a revenue leader), each with a topical map of 3 topics plus 1 personal angle, each topic tied to the offer [mc7nK3PYgjw].
- Match content level to buyer seniority: strategy and market views for executives, frameworks and playbooks for managers, tutorials and templates for practitioners. Run one shared calendar, one weekly sync, and a central content factory so the experts only supply insight [mc7nK3PYgjw].
- He claims 60+ content ideas from one mapping session (creator claim) [mc7nK3PYgjw]. Dated shift: after four years teaching personal branding, this is his biggest single reversal in the corpus [mc7nK3PYgjw, zVC1HEH9060].

## Measurement

- Judge formats by their own results: his 10-format test measured 353,000 impressions across 14 days and he reuses the winners rather than the newest idea [CphfSIw7WV8].
- The revenue figures he reports ($700K+ of LinkedIn inbound in a year, a business at $90K months, an agency past $85K a month, $2M in content-driven revenue) are creator claims and rolling self-reports, never audited [h9BQP6-IzZo, mV1Ma2ZVgU8, ZoE84jNZ4gQ, mc7nK3PYgjw].
