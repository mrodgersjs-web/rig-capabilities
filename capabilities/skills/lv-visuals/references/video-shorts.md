# Video shorts: LinkedIn video and vertical shorts

Video builds authority and trust faster than text and converts better for high-trust services, and
LinkedIn rewards consistency in a format with distribution. Creator-reported multipliers on engagement
and sharing stay creator claims: the corpus contradicts itself on video versus carousel supremacy, so
choose by payload and never quote a multiplier [video-shorts].

## Formats

| Format | Spec | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Feed video | up to about 10 minutes, any orientation; archived under the profile Video tab | tutorials, screen shares, long demos; a searchable library |
| Short video | 45s to 2min sweet spot, vertical, autoplay muted in feed | the default: quick authority hits, idea testing |
| LinkedIn Live | replay lives in the Events tab | scheduled community moments; intent engagement is reported higher |

Start with about 10 shorts to learn what resonates before committing to longer formats; the exception
is technical screen-share content, which starts at feed-video length [video-shorts].

## Mechanics that decide performance

1. Hook in the first 3 seconds and land it by 10. Views are easy; retention is the game. Hook patterns:
   a one-sentence summary of the payoff plus a curiosity gap, or a list framing that makes viewers wait
   for the next step [video-shorts].
2. Burned-in graphic captions, not auto-captions. Most viewing is muted; burned captions are accurate,
   styled and act as transitions. Bold text plus a headline block at the top [video-shorts].
3. Length: 30-90 seconds as the rule of thumb, stretching to a few minutes only when the content earns
   it. House default: vertical 9:16, 45-90 seconds [video-shorts].
4. Vertical wins: mobile-majority audience and one render cross-posts to other short-form platforms
   with minor edits [video-shorts].
5. Quality is credibility for professional audiences and premium pricing: a decent mic, light from
   behind the camera or a window (never overhead), clean editing with captions and B-roll. Creative but
   clean, not gimmicky [video-shorts].
6. The visual pacifier: a passive background activity (process footage, screen-work B-roll, subtle loop
   motion behind the talking head) holds just enough attention to stop churn while the burned captions
   carry the payload [video-shorts].
7. A repeated opening phrase or visual beat becomes a recognition signature; lane voice rules govern
   what that signature may be [video-shorts].
8. Build videos as standing assets: one well-built video can warm cold prospects for months, so brief
   for durability, not just the campaign [video-shorts].
9. Thumbnails are optional craft: screenshot a key captioned moment, trim vertical, upload it. Do not
   burn design budget on custom thumbnails [video-shorts].

## Copy pairing (the text above the video is the thumbnail)

People read the text first or never watch; weak copy cuts the audience hard. Workflow: transcribe the
video, rewrite it into post copy, post. Caption length is a live contradiction in the corpus (long
formatted transcription versus short and engaging): A/B it on this account inside the 10-post evidence
bar rather than importing either as law. Hashtags: 2-4 maximum, categorization only, not a reach lever.
Tag people or brands featured [video-shorts].

## Content architecture

One idea per video at about a minute; ideation from the most-asked questions. Formats that work:
testimonials told as stories, educational tips, podcast-style clips, behind-the-scenes. A useful
anti-salesy shape is a relevant non-business open, the direct offer in the middle, and indirect value
to close. CTA: not every video, and when used exactly one ask, no choice menus; an alternative home is
the profile or comment section. Repurposing runs roughly half and half with originals; learn the
platform on originals first and tailor per destination when cross-posting [video-shorts].

## Cadence and operations

3-4 videos a week is the reported sustainable band mixed with text and photo posts; 2 a week also
reported working. Consistency beats spikes. Batch 10 or more videos per session and let the edit pass
add branding, captions, B-roll and sound. Golden hour applies: be present in the first hours and reply
to every comment fast. Weekday mornings, Tuesday to Thursday strongest; house slots (7-9 AM / 12-1 PM
Denver) already comply [video-shorts].

## Dependency: the profile

Video drives profile visits and conversion happens on the profile. If the headline, banner and featured
section are not working, video reach converts to nothing [video-shorts].
