# Creator playbook: distribution (newsletter and owned audience)

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage. Sources: the newsletter and owned-audience parts of each creator's `strategy-and-growth.md`, plus Clark's `references/outbound-and-pipeline.md` section 5. Short names: Clark, Lara, Diandra, Will, Harry, Kasey, Mark, Pierre, RYVO, Shanee, Michelle. Coverage is uneven and that is the finding: Diandra teaches capture routing only, never a newsletter doctrine; Harry's owned-audience section is two lines. Every subscriber and revenue figure is a creator claim.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. The platform is rented, the list is owned. Clark calls a newsletter the owned exit from rented reach [sDuWUBoHsRI]; Kasey, that social reach is leased by the algorithm while email is owned [l7l782TiXDk]; Shanee, rent the platform and own the list, with the exportable backend as financial security [zpi5MCB3Wzs]; Michelle, grow on LinkedIn and own on email [TtJFDeUqUNM]; Lara, the email list is the asset [7ZfRNgn3oHs]; Will, email matters because you own it [pyPoIYDg_a0]; Harry names the newsletter the only off-platform ask in his system [kK5H2Uu1L6Y].
2. The LinkedIn newsletter is discovery, the exportable list is the asset. Michelle splits them, LinkedIn for discovery, owned list as insurance, Substack for community plus paid [TtJFDeUqUNM]; Shanee uses the LinkedIn newsletter for front-facing trust and a separate backend [93Wgv8WAHw4]; RYVO wants the newsletter card first in Activity so visitors meet Subscribe [gRHeFSRav1U].
3. Capture with a genuinely useful asset traded for the address. Clark: a keyword comment for a real asset about monthly, then DM follow-up [8VqGXuFxd3Q]. Lara: one burning problem, one free resource that answers it fully, one promo post with partial value [hDknZIF9Wsc]. Kasey: a lead magnet trading real value, built from material you already have in about 30 minutes [l7l782TiXDk]. Pierre: a tracked content upgrade logged as a scored touch [IAn0_pfzero]. Firth: a weekly CTA into an email list in 2024-05, a free download in Featured in 2025-05 [MourIQGOHQ8, rKwbCjanWCo].
4. The list is a warm prospect map, not a broadcast pipe. Shanee reports contracts landing within four editions and silent readers converting after years [BDW169gFDy0]; Michelle has subscribers get a question rather than a pitch [TtJFDeUqUNM]; Lara, under 50 subscribers, talks to warm people directly and helps only after they name a frustration [9QiqofqNHq8]; Kasey asks every subscriber to reply and answers personally [l7l782TiXDk].
5. One promise, one audience, one CTA. Michelle writes the promise before the name and holds one clear idea per issue [SjhzoOD6Vmk]; Shanee runs one category and one CTA across feed, lives and newsletter [6f3ZzY5-gUg]; Will pivoted his newsletter to sit on the offer after a broad version failed [pyPoIYDg_a0]; Kasey's landing page states the transformation, not the asset [l7l782TiXDk]; RYVO wants free content leading to the offer you sell [PXe1NrL-0CE].
6. Weekly cadence, judged on business signals. Lara sends weekly and sells in short windows [7ZfRNgn3oHs, Qz003lp_5vA]; RYVO ran about one edition a week, 74 editions by 2025-10 [gRHeFSRav1U]; Michelle wants a sustainable cadence with no approval bottleneck [SjhzoOD6Vmk]; Kasey watches three weekly numbers only: meaningful DM conversations, email signups, opportunities [2t-EBcS43Lw]; Shanee counts emails and calls, and reads four lives with zero consults as a messaging problem, not a channel failure [qSt2LtOCNy8].

## Conflicts and dated shifts

1. Do lead magnets still make buyers. Mark declares them dead on 2025-09-10 [g2MSqSUOX6c] and puts a free download back in Featured on 2025-11-21 without repealing the claim [i4lHm-CGiwU]. Against him: Lara's magnet cadence through 2026 [L7a694gzKG4], Kasey's capture system [l7l782TiXDk], Pierre's tracked upgrade [IAn0_pfzero], Clark's monthly asset [8VqGXuFxd3Q]. Newest: the 2026 capture teaching. The reconciled reading is the one Mark's own record supports: a magnet builds the list and rarely closes the deal, so judge it on signups, Will's test [A9acfOArp98], not buyer counts.
2. Where the ask lives. Lara's corpus never settles it: author comment in 2024, a pinned comment in 2024-12 that no longer exists as a platform feature, calls returning in 2026-01, a weekly priced post in 2026-05, email by 2026-07 [UiKT7lxE4bo, 7SKd2cchGd4, 9QiqofqNHq8]. Harry never says at all [q_EkkPZ1vX4]. RYVO says the subscribe card beats inquiry links when the goal is the list [gRHeFSRav1U]. RIG override: the route-back map in `references/channel-order-and-gates.md` decides placement per channel, and no creator link habit overrides it.
3. The AI-citation newsletter bet. Michelle bets in 2026-03 that headed newsletters and articles are the surface AI search cites [vMfQ3tewli8], then warns in 2026-06 that launching a newsletter purely for AI citations mostly damages brands [SjhzoOD6Vmk]. The 2026-06 warning is newer and is the operative line.
4. One channel first or three at once. Michelle says master one platform first [kyFnmN5Gu78]; Shanee runs feed plus live video plus newsletters as one machine from 2026 [ZohBpiAEvus, bCRYL6aL3ec]. Both dated inside 2026, neither retracted. RIG override: this stage already runs channels in a fixed cost order, which is Michelle's shape applied to a multi-channel brief.
5. Figures drift inside single sources. Michelle's own count moves from 8,500 to 82,000 in one episode [kyFnmN5Gu78]; RYVO's file holds three dated cuts plus one garbled header line [gRHeFSRav1U, rkud8XdrQAY]; Kasey's revenue ladder climbs across videos [TcvoXYSHqe4]. No figure in this playbook is safe to print as a fact.

## What nobody teaches

- Newsletter deliverability, consent handling, list hygiene, or what a LinkedIn subscriber export actually contains versus a real mailing list.
- Any working attribution method from newsletter to revenue. Kasey names three weekly numbers and Will uses UTMs on case studies only; nothing joins a send to a signed deal.
- Owned-audience work on a client's account or any lane where the list owner is not the poster. Ownership across lanes is a RIG problem only.
- What to do when a newsletter launch collides with a first-run exclusivity owed to an outlet, the exact conflict this stage's queue can create.
- Paid newsletter economics beyond Michelle's passing mention of paid subscriptions.

## How to apply here

Capture and newsletter work rides the stage procedure, not a creator cadence: the asset enters the claims ledger in `references/repurpose-method.md` first, channel versions are re-architected rather than copied, and the route-back map in `references/channel-order-and-gates.md` governs where any subscribe or asset link may appear with a distinct allowlisted `utm_source` per route. `references/substack-x-and-email.md` already owns the owned list and the weekly-digest rule; one issue lands and is read back before anything is scheduled. Clark's keyword-then-DM drop and every subscriber DM are outward sends under batch-level Gate-D naming recipients and exact text. Creator conversion percentages stay labelled creator claims and never enter a newsletter issue as a fact.
