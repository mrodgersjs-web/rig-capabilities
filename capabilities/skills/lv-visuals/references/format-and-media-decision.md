# Format and media decision: media is the second hook

The two non-negotiables before any format call: Eden generates brand imagery and this stage only
briefs and QA's it, and QA or internal text never enters artwork. Both were learned the hard way: an
agent-built carousel was rejected outright, and one render leaked an internal instruction as visible
footer text [li-visuals].

## Classifier first

One sentence: "This visual is for <named person> struggling with <specific thing>." A category
("founders") fails; a describable person passes. An unclassifiable visual is culled by the feed no
matter how good it looks [li-visuals].

## Moat (choose one before the format)

A real story, proprietary data, or physical proof: a photo, an artifact, a screenshot [szN0DF3Oqik].
The image must carry the post's argument the way a still carries a plot, not decorate it [uYVwSbaztJ4].

## Format matrix

| Payload | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Saveable reference asset: framework, checklist, teardown | document / carousel PDF | reported as the strongest save signal; never quote a multiplier in copy |
| Trust, authority, thesis | real photo of Mike + text post | the unanimous default; a face stops the scroll |
| Demonstration, emotion, story that needs his voice | vertical video short | retention is the game, not views |
| Quick stat, before/after, single insight | single graphic or GIF | at most 8 words on the image |
| Real-time relationship building | LinkedIn Live | high intent engagement, small audience |
| Recurring owned-audience asset | newsletter + issue cover card | notifies subscribers; no subscriber emails owned |

Mixed rule: default is the photo of Mike; escalate to a carousel when the payload is saveable;
escalate to video when the payload demonstrates or performs [li-visuals].

## What Clark's media evidence adds

- Ask of every post whether a photo, graphic or video can carry it [UjDZLhuEpnQ, s6KMjhznXqs].
- Real photos of real people and places win: the office used as a set with someone owning photo and
  B-roll capture, early-days photos, event shots, phone-note screenshots. He reports roughly 27% more
  impressions for IRL photos across a month of client posts, and branded graphics read as ads
  [szN0DF3Oqik, N2b0QVoCEPg, 8VqGXuFxd3Q, O8HeAkNg6L8]. The number is his claim, not ours.
- Formats cycle, so never lock in: short video was boosted in late 2024 and hit-or-miss by late 2025;
  carousels faded in 2024, returned in 2025 with two-to-three-week tails and were "no longer a cheat
  code" by 2026; text-only came back mid-2025 [bjPrW4O4hww, E0uu_GLIkw0, 29hBCF7N7Jg, cWfOlNGZ6eg,
  yBKOrgQS-q8, 8VqGXuFxd3Q].
- Funding and milestone graphics interrupt the scroll [O8HeAkNg6L8, Xt179qkyqQU].
- Featured-link images behave like YouTube thumbnails: bake the value and the proof into the image
  [RVFC8i05e_k, 8VqGXuFxd3Q].
- One creator class claim per source, never a fact: a corpus number never enters artwork or
  client-facing copy without our own receipt [li-visuals].
