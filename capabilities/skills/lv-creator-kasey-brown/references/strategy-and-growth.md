# Strategy and growth (Kasey Brown)

Positioning, profile, outreach and DMs, lead capture, launches, and measurement. All figures are creator claims; the variant ledger at the bottom records where his numbers drift.

## Position before posting

- One sentence, almost entirely about the buyer: if your profile sentence describes your job title, restart it. The goal is being unmissable to a few hundred named buyers, not visible to everyone. [aPa6qCvPFao, rGypgxFz3TM]
- Write for the person you were two levels ago, or for 50 named decision makers; downstream buyers still arrive when the post is aimed at one reader. [M0dAWh95NaQ, -1fJ7U684Hs, bCxD79Wl6ec]
- Narrow positioning pays: the first $50K month followed niching within 90 days (2026-02), and by June 2026 he bans "for everyone" content outright after measuring revenue per post. [EfHijF0P6bo, kuipymgFpjk]
- Three moves over an influencer brand: name one domain you can prove, publish evidence (receipts, named transformations) instead of opinions, and move raised hands into DMs. Being known is not being paid. [rGypgxFz3TM]
- The positioning game beats the content game: a 10-post audit should tell a stranger what you are about in 8 of 10 posts. [iVnkmeVj9ao]

## Profile as the funnel

- Headline is a landing page line (who you help plus what happens), not a job title; it shows everywhere and can add followers on its own. [aPa6qCvPFao, eyfNP4ipgrI]
- About section carries the transformation, the proof and the fit; the profile must be readable in about three seconds. [aPa6qCvPFao]
- Featured section holds the lead capture asset; the chain is post, profile, bio button, featured CTA, offer. [eyfNP4ipgrI, hC7ZCVsG7qM]

## Outreach and DMs

- Warm order: existing engagers first, new connections within 48 hours second, cold third and only with a personal video or voice note. His cold-video DMs get reply rates he puts at 60 to 70 percent. [NjyEXaX8nQU]
- DM principles: open with a simple human question whose only job is a reply; diagnose with a follow-up showing you read them; give one specific tactic; the ask is usually to consume more content. His funnel math: 100 warm conversations to about 50 replies to 20 calls to 8 closes a month at 20 DMs a day. [NjyEXaX8nQU]
- Value ratio of 10 to 1 before any ask, and nurture for weeks (his pitch came about five weeks after first contact). [NjyEXaX8nQU, aPa6qCvPFao]
- Reactivation beats cold: a list of 100 old contacts, gratitude messages with no pitch, virtual coffee asks, and call prep of know, learn, ask. A 20/20/20 daily challenge (connections, DMs, comments) and an ask challenge produced her first revenue. [7vl7hhn_y78, y7i6_S3v640]
- Relationship planting: list dream brands, use their product genuinely, tag and connect their marketing people, stay in their feed for weeks, then reach out as a known supporter, never a stranger. [sb0qucoOSow]
- Micro-invite DM sequences: acknowledge, show interest, invite to an event, never pitch; a guest reports very high response rates from a small, precisely addressed audience. [XNmTFbRmy_o]

## Lead capture and launches

- Rented versus earned audience: social reach is leased by the algorithm, email is owned, so a lead magnet trades real value for the address. Pick one felt ICP problem, drill to the underlying pain, match the format to how the buyer consumes, and make a first version from material you already have in about 30 minutes. [l7l782TiXDk]
- Landing page headline states the transformation, not the asset; he asks subscribers to reply and answers personally (he flags this as not scalable). [l7l782TiXDk]
- Launch mechanics: price up front to filter intent, warm a waitlist before any ask, rolling acceptances, DM the warm buckets personally, publish vulnerability stories through the window, and lead with alumni proof. His waitlist windows opened far above the industry rates he cites. [dI8ZqDwPBNY]
- Trust before selling: he ran a full year of posting with nothing to sell, then taught and sold; he chose reputation over an earlier scaling move. [jYCnMnYF9EE]

## Measurement

- Three weekly numbers only: meaningful DM conversations, email signups, opportunities; deep dive quarterly. [2t-EBcS43Lw, B18w0jPdXiI]
- Ignore analytics until about 100 posts; save inbound business conversations in a named evidence folder as the real scoreboard. [uHgHT0fg3As]
- Grade every post on Stop, Stay, Share and intent; his own biggest post gets an A on attention and a C on what he did with it (no capture path, no positioning, no replies). [hC7ZCVsG7qM]
- Timeline honesty: about six months to momentum and twelve months to consistent business results; consistency with a sustainable cadence beats intensity. [2t-EBcS43Lw, B18w0jPdXiI, jYCnMnYF9EE]
