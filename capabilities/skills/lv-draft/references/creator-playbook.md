# Creator playbook: body writing and formats

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage.

All ten have a `writing.md`. Clark's body rules are in `references/content-system.md`. Nobody is skipped.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. One post, one reader, one idea, in short spoken lines, then a read-aloud pass. Lara wants each line to stand alone [EACWADXrg88]. Diandra wants two or three lines and a read-aloud [ZAqIUNAvXXo]. Will puts spoken habits back in after the draft [TTH-75XOgJE]. Harry treats line breaks as the format [fWhBOPC1eLA]. Kasey cuts lines that do not advance the story [jcxbBuiW2vE]. Mark breaks at spoken pauses [gTkfDjUBXMY]. Pierre keeps one idea and short sentences [i-YDUfx84f8]. RYVO/Louise breaks after two or three lines [KfqDg3sc9Ms]. Shanee spaces for a phone [EJ0QFdEXjqA]. Michelle writes to one persona the way she talks [udtNia_2Onk]. Clark says write as you talk and read it aloud [K2H2KGTbZ08].
2. The body must hold something a model cannot invent: a lived scene, a call, a failure, or a number only the author holds. Lara calls this how-I [Ug5Np7RNX7A]. Diandra dated the same shift to 2026-03-14 [a4uL3wTtjFk]. Will wants one real story in the voice pass [TTH-75XOgJE]. Kasey puts story before instruction [M0dAWh95NaQ]. Mark reversed from templates to stories on 2025-04-07 [-0NvIx7Kjfo]. Pierre's how-I shape runs problem, solution, messy build, end state [YIOMfYkRE-U]. Michelle says a model can edit a lived scene and cannot invent one [1pjFtyAEVcc]. Clark forbids a blank start [8nBIAUSIRnQ].
3. Mine the words from real work, not a blank topic. Diandra uses sales calls and interviews [Z3jHnYT8iJo]. Will keeps a bank of buyer problems from transcripts [EUueg8xudKk]. Harry starts from calls, mail, and chat [uK6p4gAV8zU]. Pierre logs one surprising line after each sales call [9o4BqmpgVT8]. Clark uses a biweekly content interview plus Friday calendar mining [mNR3-MrM5wI, HFCfw6gznRM].
4. Name the job before the sentences. Lara: teach, document, story, or sell [JDZYNJQOee0]. Diandra: growth, authority, conversion, personal [w3_109ygioY]. Will splits top, middle, and bottom [5rLlzb6_vgA]. Kasey picks one type per slot [2t-EBcS43Lw]. Clark: reach, unpitched authority, then proof [K2H2KGTbZ08].
5. Problem before solution, and one ask tied to the post when an ask exists. Lara names the problem, agitates, then solves [6EYqnuncRrg]. Will puts their pain before the fix [EUueg8xudKk]. Pierre adds before-after-bridge [SmuGlt1CDKY]. Michelle mixes that order with insight posts [2JI55PLI9Zw]. Harry bans comment-a-word bait and a link in the body [-BHLc3TIXMc]. Mark wants a question only that story can ask [uEHPPU2k_OM]. Shanee wants one destination, continued from the argument [6f3ZzY5-gUg].
6. A proof post needs a recognizable result and a real artifact. Lara opens documenting posts on a number plus a readable screenshot [JDZYNJQOee0]. Will says a case invisible before the fold is not one [UJvAtV63vk0]. Kasey leads with the result, then the struggle [ycA0D55AEQ8]. Clark tells the customer result as a native story, not an off-site link [K2H2KGTbZ08].

## Conflicts and dated shifts

- Who may draft. Clark, from Sept 2025, forbids a model taking a post from nothing to a draft [8nBIAUSIRnQ]. Will on 2025-12-01 trains a model, then rewrites [TTH-75XOgJE]. Harry from 2026-08-13 lets a trained model draft; a human sends [uK6p4gAV8zU]. Lara seeds a model with a real profile and edits hard [ibfdqyz4KhQ]. RYVO/Louise's later line is original prose [K_sGlIEm_7E]. Newer files do not agree. This stage wins: Eden generates the copy from a real story, transcript, or receipt. A bare topic is refused. The model never sends.
- How often to ask. Pierre closes every post on one next step [3sf8fojVfpM]. Michelle says silence about the offer dries the pipeline [2JI55PLI9Zw]. Lara's May 2026 weekly priced sell is walked back three weeks later toward email [7SKd2cchGd4, 9QiqofqNHq8]. Clark keeps proof posts as milestones, and a keyword asset about monthly [CkoSKSZ6WIU, 8VqGXuFxd3Q]. This stage wins: no CTA unless this is the week's one ask-bearing post.
- Hashtags. Lara drops them for positioning keywords [b_sVHSaQqGA]. Kasey uses none [TcvoXYSHqe4]. Harry allows two or three on 2026-07-31 and none on 2026-09-22 [-BHLc3TIXMc]. Shanee's 3 to 5 cap is a 2024 line [EJ0QFdEXjqA]. Newest Harry side is zero. This stage keeps 2 to 3 maximum.
- Length and grade. Lara cites about 800 characters [ibfdqyz4KhQ]. Michelle cites about a thousand after the hook and teaches no word-count law [Dd42KDThdPY]. Clark's 2026 bands are the newest catalog [QKNc515DSro]. Grades run from fourth (Kasey, Michelle) [TcvoXYSHqe4, udtNia_2Onk] through 5th to 7th (Mark) [uEHPPU2k_OM] to 7th to 8th (Diandra) [ZAqIUNAvXXo]. Use Clark's bands and the floor in `references/body-craft.md`.
- Give the method, or stop at the feeling. Lara and Shanee give the how in full [1wsfrYzwKbk, NtPWMHQfdq8]. Mark's newer line (2026-05-13, restated 2026-09-04) says an explained lesson is the failure mode [FEOAeCCK9e0, 7qDcQrCbIbI]. Lane B still names the mechanism. A past-weakness story is allowed only if it is past and receipted.
- Kasey's intentional typos [jcxbBuiW2vE] and Harry's loud unsourced wins [B1uJ-pdbIFc] lose to the named-source rule.

## What nobody teaches

- No creator teaches the four intake fields as a hard stop, the deliberate valley, or a Jev verdict before a frontier rewrite.
- No creator teaches the first-comment rule (a saveable asset, and no link-drop workaround). A body-link ban is not that rule.
- No creator splits Lane A, Lane B, the client lane, and the job lane. Do not borrow a creator revenue story into any of them.
- Thread architecture is thin. Pierre repurposes one insight. RYVO/Louise puts series in the newsletter. None of that is a feed-thread system. Use `references/formats-and-cta.md`.
- Mark, Michelle, and Harry give no stable word count. Do not invent one.

## How to apply here

Run the stage procedure. Fill the four intake fields and the one-person sentence before any creator frame. Put the frame and the receipt into the Eden brief. Proof still rises weakest to strongest, with the valley named. CTA frequency, hashtag count, the link ban, and one post a day stay with the hard rules in `SKILL.md`. Gate with `post.linkedin` or `post.eden` before the brief leaves. A creator reach multiple is not a RIG expectation.
