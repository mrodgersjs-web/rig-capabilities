# Creator playbook: launch, the posting slot and the first 90 minutes

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 plus Tommy Clark) teach for this stage: posting
windows, what to do after the post goes live, cadence ceilings, and what the feed is believed to reward.
Every algorithm line below is a dated creator claim, unverified. Mike's tracker wins on conflict.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. Reply to commenters inside the early window after publishing. Kasey makes it a named 60 to 90
   minute rule [hC7ZCVsG7qM, 4jnS31Fh5QE]; Clark a 30 to 60 minute checklist step [Fvl_wMtcz24,
   s6KMjhznXqs]; Harry the same hour through 29 Aug 2026 [XbDKE82gRf0, qt_YMXlefXo]; Lara in Dec 2024
   and again in Mar 2026 [6EYqnuncRrg, 6d_oxo1R9gs]; RYVO caps it at 60 minutes [gtwM_m3iZb0]; Diandra
   keeps a two-hour reply habit even after walking her velocity story back [PxjDCR29aKc, R8nrEQx8rEY].
2. Warm the account with comments on other posts just before publishing: Lara 30 minutes (Dec 2024)
   [6EYqnuncRrg], Kasey 15 [uHgHT0fg3As], RYVO three or four [gtwM_m3iZb0, xLhvT0Fmyc8].
3. Never delete a slow post; distribution is spiky and old posts return. Clark reports dead-looking
   posts taking off after 12 hours and a one to four week resurfacing window [s6KMjhznXqs, _tON8MpkmS0];
   Will saw three-week-old posts come back [Ej7WFBI9NS4]; Lara names a roughly three week revival in Jun
   2026 [e3E83C-Xxn0]; Michelle read two to three week returns as design in Jul 2025 [Q6jtTfPADgw,
   FQ5RAfOuAIg]; RYVO reports the same three-week return [ZSS7Dh2WmcE, c9vkQ-wxle4].
4. There is no universal best posting clock; use the audience's own rhythm and the account's own data.
   Clark says no perfect time yet defaults to weekday mid-morning in the reader's zone [DGYZ04X5pk4,
   zMzPmTETIyU]; Firth teaches audience rhythms [eubB4zi3HG4, MnQc73VYbJ0]; Harry names no publish
   clock [XbDKE82gRf0]; RYVO calls clock time a non-lever (Sep 2025) [MVbCMTE5cR8]; Will found 8 versus
   9 a.m. moved nothing [pyPoIYDg_a0]; Shanee finds no universal weekday [yeV4VseTmPQ].
5. Cap daily volume; roughly one post a day is the ceiling and a second same-day post starves the
   first. Clark [TZSADGhY-x4, N2b0QVoCEPg], Harry [XbDKE82gRf0], RYVO (Jan 2025) [qvmIiwBXoXE],
   Michelle (steady beats bursts) [8Yrgj-zOYQ8, mDsy5N6jgHs], Kasey (3 to 4 a week over daily)
   [eyfNP4ipgrI].
6. Dwell time, saves and shares are believed to outrank likes as distribution signals. Clark
   [DGYZ04X5pk4, QVqRt86frJg], Kasey [LvA5pocucUM, 4jnS31Fh5QE], Pierre [i-YDUfx84f8, 2eovlLOHOU4],
   Harry Model B [sUC5UAXni6M], Diandra relaying a vendor figure on saves [R8nrEQx8rEY].

## What nobody teaches

- No creator supplies a controlled measurement of the first hour on anyone's account; every clock
  number here is a claim. Lara says platform staff themselves do not know the ranking (May 2026)
  [7SKd2cchGd4].
- Nobody gives a stop rule for leaving the reply window, or a minute-by-minute script past staying and
  replying.
- Nobody teaches verifying that a posted comment actually landed; the stage's hard-reload check is
  RIG's own.
- No creator covers launching from a client account or a minor's account.
- Nobody closes the loop on paid spend: Clark gives boost mechanics and demographic mining, Michelle a
  negative rule, and no one gives a validation method for what the money bought.

## How to apply here

- The stage's 60 to 90 minute block in `references/golden-hour.md` stands as an RIG rule even though
  four creators retired it or never had it; the reply habit is what every model shift kept.
- Publish only when a human can hold hour one (Kasey's rule), inside the slot picker's Tue-Thu, 7-9am
  or 12-1pm Denver default; every clock figure here is a hypothesis for
  `references/launch-safety-and-measurement.md` to test against Mike's numbers.
- No second post the same day, no external link in the save asset, no deletion in the first hours.
- Boosts stay Gate-D spend items per `references/boosts-and-peaks.md`, after one to two weeks organic,
  which is where Clark and the stage already agree.
- Log one to three learnings at 24 hours; a winner becomes a rerun proposal inside Will's windows, and
  every rerun needs a fresh yes.
