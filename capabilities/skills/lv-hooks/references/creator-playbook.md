# Creator playbook: hooks and openings
What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. The opening carries the post, so it gets the drafting time. Clark treats the first lines like a
   title and thumbnail [P_5mJNksHiE]; Kasey puts most writing effort in lines 1-2 plus the photo
   [4jnS31Fh5QE]; Harry dates the same claim to July 2026 [fWhBOPC1eLA]; Pierre calls the hook the
   biggest reach lever [i-YDUfx84f8]; Will finds the opening is why posts spread [rlbI4ztEBFU]; Mark,
   Michelle, Lara and Diandra make line one the decision line [gTkfDjUBXMY, Dd42KDThdPY,
   6EYqnuncRrg, BPbf3JUm_dw].
2. Specificity beats cleverness: a number, a named buyer, or a concrete scene. Clark's unrounded
   figure lever [-WGCvECeTcU], Lara's concrete-miss rule [qap4n06dqfw], Pierre's numbers-plus-keyword
   rule [2eovlLOHOU4], Diandra's number-plus-pain shape [Zc4E_K48v48], Harry's match-the-proof rule
   [_ddwk79BB3s], and Will's receipts-in-line-one rule [rlbI4ztEBFU] are one instruction.
3. The opening names who the post is for. Pierre wants the reader addressed [i-YDUfx84f8], Harry names
   one role [_ddwk79BB3s], Lara names the reader or the post earns the wrong audience [ibfdqyz4KhQ],
   Shanee conditions every opener on the buyer [gCL9819h1OA], Mark opens on the buyer's live
   frustration [T7G4_owju2A, FEOAeCCK9e0], Michelle opens on their constraint [udtNia_2Onk].
4. The body pays the promise, and the hook is mined from real material. Diandra writes the body first
   and lifts the gap line out of it [ZAqIUNAvXXo, Zc4E_K48v48]; Clark demands exact payoff
   [E5vxr1IZCpI]; Pierre delivers the promised answer at the top of the body [Io7Uq-S8dpk]; Kasey
   forbids a re-hook that teases an absent payoff [yrI3QPIUk0Q].
5. Write like a person; visible formula phrasing reads as machine text. Clark hides the levers under a
   conversational voice [SL8yfxUVFKE]; Kasey dropped borrowed opener formulas by 2026 [iVnkmeVj9ao,
   kuipymgFpjk]; Lara calls template-hacking the trap in July 2026 [9QiqofqNHq8]; Will drops jargon
   and inflated words [TTH-75XOgJE]; Harry writes as he talks [_ddwk79BB3s]; Michelle refuses
   score-flex templates [nC06xaoVWPw].
6. Draft several variants, then a human picks. Clark's three drafts are non-negotiable [TH63A9m1VNA,
   2XeAckxuAKM]; Diandra tests 10 to 15 plain-text lines before design time [Z3jHnYT8iJo]; Harry has
   the model offer about 10 angles and a human choose [uK6p4gAV8zU, -BHLc3TIXMc]; Will drafts five
   interrupts and keeps the one that lands [9CsBaaYOgGM]; Lara validates against past winners
   [Slm6Ek465ow]; Kasey runs a rating loop on his own audience data [Lo7cSO2XH2k].
7. The visual is part of the hook unit. Will's forced-view unit is the graphic plus the visible
   caption lines per format [rlbI4ztEBFU]; Kasey treats the photo as a third hook slot [ycA0D55AEQ8];
   Pierre's two-layer hook pairs the printed image headline with the text lines [mV1Ma2ZVgU8]; Clark
   calls media the second hook [UjDZLhuEpnQ]; Michelle allows the image itself to be the hook
   [Dd42KDThdPY]; Louise makes the video's first frame the hook [tP_IpeaKZqU].
8. A second hook sits right after the first. Lara's line two complicates or proves line one
   [6EYqnuncRrg, ibfdqyz4KhQ]; Kasey reserves line 3 for new tension, never continuation
   [yrI3QPIUk0Q]; Clark's open-loop lever does the same job [P_5mJNksHiE, UjDZLhuEpnQ].
9. Dated templates decay, so re-validate against recent winners before reuse. Lara records the drift
   and calls a format rented [1ph8i-NDz4k, e3E83C-Xxn0]; Diandra says 2023 and 2024 playbooks now
   hurt [Zc4E_K48v48]; Mark retired template reuse for stories and belief-change [N2AOzgp-SIk,
   T7G4_owju2A]; Clark's own lever count drifted across three years (see Conflicts).

## Sharp single-source moves

- Diandra: the mobile fold is laid out in pixels, not characters, so preview in a formatter before
  shipping; and a single-line opener collapses on mobile without two manual line breaks under it
  [Zc4E_K48v48]. Her nervousness test: an opener that leaves you uneasy is probably a real take
  [twBrrKkhpOg].
- Shanee: baseline the topic first. Watch the top pieces on it, note what none of them say, and make
  the off-baseline truth the hook [v3TS92mls0k].
- Mark: sophistication match. Newer buyers get cost framing, experienced buyers get time saved;
  mismatching them is why small accounts stay small [ArYphaJtvmc].
- Michelle: the remove-name test. Hide the author name and a reader should still know who wrote it
  [Dd42KDThdPY].
- Will: a self-disqualifying opener is a win, because a reader who rules themselves out is not a
  buyer [Ql4gXPDhalk].
- Pierre: the image headline sets the angle the reader holds through the whole post, so it must
  match the text hook, not decorate it [CphfSIw7WV8].
- Louise: the first line of a document headline reprints under every comment, so a vague title wastes
  the whole reply thread [fjJqTCWBltk].
- Kasey: pull the buried strongest fact up to line one even when it breaks the story order
  [yrI3QPIUk0Q].
- Clark: on a milestone post lead with the strongest metric class, revenue before growth before
  customers before headcount [CkoSKSZ6WIU]; a lagging fresh post gets a rewritten opening but is not
  deleted early, because delayed spikes are normal [s6KMjhznXqs].
- Lara: reframe an update as an outcome instead of announcing it [6d_oxo1R9gs], and put a lived plot
  in the reader's seat [0ZVUVa4IFTw].

## Conflicts and dated shifts

- Lever count. Clark stacks two or three levers as of 2026 [UjDZLhuEpnQ, s6KMjhznXqs]; Will starts
  with exactly one lever and layers only after it lands (2025-12) [rlbI4ztEBFU]. Clark is newer.
  This stage's procedure already fixes the two-to-three stack; the honest note is that neither count
  is verified on Mike's account, so the analytics tracker decides.
- Visible formula versus hidden formula. Mark's March 2026 re-upload still teaches the 2024
  colon-and-number costume [PVX6FHGp4Uc]; Clark's 2026 rule hides the machinery because formula
  phrasing reads as AI [SL8yfxUVFKE]. Clark wins here, per this stage's hard rules. Mark still
  supplies the subject of the line [FEOAeCCK9e0].
- Word-count specs are dated tests, not laws. Lara taught eight words in 2025-01, called it optional
  weeks later, restored 8 to 10 for video captions in 2025-10, and returned to under eight words in
  2026-03 [ObW3McgGG44, 1wsfrYzwKbk, 71AspHNJxBE, 6d_oxo1R9gs]. Mark moved from 5 to 8 words (2024)
  to a buyer-problem line (2026-05) [gTkfDjUBXMY, FEOAeCCK9e0]. Fold estimates disagree outright:
  Clark 100 to 300 characters [P_5mJNksHiE], Diandra pixels [Zc4E_K48v48], Michelle 150 characters
  [xR6t2llZRKQ], Harry about 12 words [fWhBOPC1eLA]. Newest is Diandra's 2026 pixel read; treat all of
  them as preview instructions.
- Question openers. Will drops rhetorical-question openings entirely [TTH-75XOgJE]; Harry opens on a
  question or a gap [_ddwk79BB3s]; Louise and Firth use a question the post can answer
  [gtwM_m3iZb0, gTkfDjUBXMY]. Harry's rule is newer. RIG reading: a question with a named stake is
  allowed, a generic rhetorical opener is on the kill list.
- Negative openers. Clark's negativity-bias lever rewards mistakes and warnings [cWfOlNGZ6eg]; Mark
  dropped "this is dead" openings for cold audiences in 2026-01 and divisive-figure bait in 2026-04
  [T7G4_owju2A, iubQmgPG3ZM]. Both dated 2026 and both can hold: a warning with a receipt for a named
  audience; no rage bait, no borrowed villain.
- Effect sizes drift inside one creator. Harry's study of viral posts was 10,000+ in 2026-07 and
  1,000 by 2026-08 [_ddwk79BB3s, fWhBOPC1eLA]; Louise's comment multiplier appears as 9x and as 30
  to 75 times [yXk5rjO0EWg]. Cite the later, smaller figure and label it a creator claim.
- Format cycles do not hold. Clark's media ledger has short video, carousels and text-only each
  boosted and faded on different schedules [bjPrW4O4hww, E0uu_GLIkw0, 8VqGXuFxd3Q]. No format is a
  standing rule.

## What nobody teaches

- No creator publishes a measured hook benchmark on Mike's account, and no rate in this set is more
  than a creator claim.
- No one teaches a calibrated hook ranking rubric with a confidence floor. Michelle has no A/B
  protocol [nC06xaoVWPw]; Shanee runs no variant routine [v3TS92mls0k]. Jev ranking is RIG's
  addition.
- No verified, platform-published fold spec exists here; every number above is one person's read.
- Nobody teaches hooks for a minor's lane, a recruiter-facing job-search claim, or a client account
  where the poster is not the author.
- Nobody teaches recovery beyond rewriting the opening or reposting.

## How to apply here

- Consensus rules are drafting constraints for procedure steps 4 and 6; single-source moves are
  optional lenses, and nothing here is copy to reuse.
- Ranked judgment stays with Jev per step 8; classifier down keeps the variant set a draft.
- Where a dated rule fights a stage rule (one hook plus one rehook, no placeholder numbers, no bait,
  no em dashes), the stage rule wins and the conflict is logged above.
- Lane rules bind: Lane B carries Mike's receipts, Lane A keeps the cast limits, and anything about a
  minor stops and goes back to Mike.
