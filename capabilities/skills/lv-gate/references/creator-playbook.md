# Creator playbook: pre-publish quality bars
What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage.

Inputs here are the hooks.md quality bars from all 11 doctrines plus each creator's writing.md
"what a post must contain" bar. These are human tests the creators run themselves; none of them is a
gate with a calibrated verdict.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. The post delivers exactly what the opening promised. Clark pays off the opening [E5vxr1IZCpI];
   Pierre delivers the promised answer first in the body [Io7Uq-S8dpk, SmuGlt1CDKY]; Kasey forbids a
   re-hook that teases an absent payoff [yrI3QPIUk0Q]; Lara's teaching posts give the whole method
   [1wsfrYzwKbk]; Louise rejects a hook that gates a file behind a keyword [KfqDg3sc9Ms].
2. Short lines, plain words, scannable layout. Diandra wants two or three line paragraphs at about a
   7th to 8th grade level with a read-aloud pass [ZAqIUNAvXXo]; Kasey measures a 4th grade level,
   short sentences, active voice, digits over words [TcvoXYSHqe4]; Firth writes to pause, 5th to 7th
   grade [uEHPPU2k_OM]; Michelle says a level-four read is fine even for a CEO [udtNia_2Onk]; Shanee
   keeps the read simple [EJ0QFdEXjqA]; Harry keeps one point per line [fWhBOPC1eLA]; Pierre caps
   rules at two lines [SmuGlt1CDKY]; Lara makes every line stand alone for a scanner [EACWADXrg88].
3. One post, one idea, one reader, one next step. Pierre: one idea per post [i-YDUfx84f8]; Michelle:
   one persona, one problem, one differentiator, one next step [xR6t2llZRKQ]; Shanee: one goal, one
   person, one destination [6f3ZzY5-gUg, DitOkxbdSvo]; Harry: one niche, one buyer, one point
   [qt_YMXlefXo]; Firth: one post, one turn [eubB4zi3HG4]; Diandra: one topic lane for 90 days
   [PxjDCR29aKc].
4. The body carries material a model cannot invent: a lived scene, proprietary numbers, real client
   problems in their words. Lara's how-I bar [Ug5Np7RNX7A]; Kasey's receipts over borrowed frameworks
   [kuipymgFpjk]; Will's problem bank built from real call transcripts [EUueg8xudKk]; Firth's lived
   moment told from the resolved side [FEOAeCCK9e0]; Michelle's story a model cannot invent
   [1pjFtyAEVcc]; Diandra's body-first numbers and mechanism [ZAqIUNAvXXo].
5. A human edits before anything ships. Diandra never publishes the model draft as-is [KlawrDJuqYc];
   Lara keeps part of machine output and adds her own insight [wYIzZqpHs3Q]; Harry runs a second pass
   that strips machine tells [-BHLc3TIXMc]; Will ends with a voice pass read aloud [TTH-75XOgJE];
   Michelle writes the thinking before the tool [cuM8ySMSxHc]; Louise's later line is original prose
   for one reader [K_sGlIEm_7E].
6. One soft ask, no bait, no hard pitch from the body. Kasey's soft CTA extends the value
   [M0dAWh95NaQ]; Pierre grades asks and never disguises a pitch as content [3sf8fojVfpM]; Firth's
   durable CTA is a question tied to the post [gTkfDjUBXMY]; Michelle wants one next step and calls a
   no-ask post a broadcast [xR6t2llZRKQ]; Shanee requires the CTA to continue the content's own logic
   [v3TS92mls0k]; Harry bans comment-a-word bait [_ddwk79BB3s]; Louise bans the keyword-to-unlock-file
   move [KfqDg3sc9Ms].
7. Story and lived experience outrank generic how-to. Firth's 2026 arc ends at belief-change with
   prescriptive content out [7qDcQrCbIbI]; Diandra dates the how-I shift to 2026-03-14 [a4uL3wTtjFk];

8. A named human quality test runs before publish. Kasey asks what a buyer would pay four figures for

## Conflicts and dated shifts

  [2XeAckxuAKM]; Harry trains a model on calls and winners, has it draft about 10 hooks, and a second
  chat cut the tells [uK6p4gAV8zU, -BHLc3TIXMc]; Will trains an AI to draft, then rewrites into voice
  [TTH-75XOgJE]; Michelle allows a draft only after human thought [cuM8ySMSxHc]; Louise's 2026 line
  is do not hand the content to AI at all [K_sGlIEm_7E]. Newest strict line is Louise's 2026 speech
  and Clark's standing ban. RIG wins anyway: Eden drafts from a real story, transcript or receipt,
  never a bare topic, and the model never sends.
- Hashtags. Shanee allowed 3 to 5 in 2024 [EJ0QFdEXjqA]; Harry went from two or three (2026-07-31)
  to none (2026-09-22) [XbDKE82gRf0, -BHLc3TIXMc]; Kasey and Lara are already at none
  [TcvoXYSHqe4, b_sVHSaQqGA]. Newest and current: none.
- Comment-gated delivery. Kasey still teaches comment a keyword and move to DM [jcxbBuiW2vE];
  Louise bans the keyword-unlock-file hook [KfqDg3sc9Ms]; Clark-era RIG rules refuse engagement bait
  outright. RIG rules override Kasey here: that pattern is a send, so it waits for Mike's item-level
  yes and the bait form is refused.
- Length. Will cites long posts reaching further [Ej7WFBI9NS4]; Pierre writes long on purpose
  [Io7Uq-S8dpk]; Lara cites about 800 characters for a short post [ibfdqyz4KhQ]; Harry warns a dense
  text block gets limited distribution, unverified [-BHLc3TIXMc]; Michelle teaches no length spec at
  all [xR6t2llZRKQ]. The survivor across all five is the readability consensus above: length is
  allowed when every line earns it.
- Sell posts. Lara taught a weekly priced sell post in 2026-05 and put most revenue in email three
  weeks later [7SKd2cchGd4, 9QiqofqNHq8]; Michelle holds sell posts to be required [2JI55PLI9Zw];
  Shanee routes high-ticket to a call [bm6_uMvLagQ]. RIG's boundary: a post naming a price or a
  commitment is a Gate-D item and stays a draft until Mike approves it.
- Wins versus losses. Harry argued loud wins beat modest copy (2026-08-26) and then that loss posts
  carry equal weight (2026-09-11) [B1uJ-pdbIFc, R1E5wPV-h6U]; Firth dates failure stories replacing
  success stories to 2026-03 [r2LsebGinxs]. Both can hold: a sourced win and a sourced loss are
  allowed, unsourced boasting is not.
- Voice texture. Kasey's deliberate imperfection play (PS asides, occasional intentional typos as an
  authorship signal) [jcxbBuiW2vE] collides with this stage's anti-slop and quality floors. The stage
  rules win: typos are defects here.

## What nobody teaches

- No creator in the set teaches a fail-closed gate with a confidence floor, a decision id, or an
  escalation vocabulary. Their bars are human judgments; the Jev-first mandate and the verdict ladder
  are RIG's.
- Nobody teaches claim hygiene for regulated or YMYL material, disclosure labels, or a risk register
  (see `references/claim-hygiene-and-hard-blocks.md`).
- No creator gives a verified platform distribution rule; every algorithm statement is a creator
  claim.
- Nobody covers gating content about a minor, a client account where the author is not the poster, or
  recruiter-facing job-search claims.
- Nobody teaches what to do when the human test and a scored criterion disagree; RIG escalates after
  two rewrites.

## How to apply here

- Feed the consensus bars into steps 5 and 6: the critic and the data-backed check absorb the human
  tests (Kasey's value question, Michelle's RATE and remove-name tests, the read-aloud passes) as
  named sub-checks, not as new gates.
- Claim-level bars are already enforced by `references/claim-hygiene-and-hard-blocks.md`; where a
  creator is stricter it becomes a checklist item, where the stage is stricter the stage wins.
- Every conflict resolution above is logged, and none of them authorizes publishing. The verdict
  stays PASS, ESCALATE, BLOCK, or DEFERRED whenever Jev could not run.
