# Creator playbook: media is the second hook

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage.

All ten have a `visuals.md`. Clark's media rules are section 5 of `references/hooks-and-media.md`. Nobody is skipped. Several teach no carousel build and no pixel spec. That gap is logged below, not filled in.

1. A real photo of a real person or place beats a branded graphic, stock, or a generated face. 2. The asset has to match the copy or the job. Clark states the match rule [uYVwSbaztJ4]. Lara pairs personal photos with stories and screenshots with teaching [6d_oxo1R9gs]. Diandra adds a visual only when it helps the bucket [ZAqIUNAvXXo]. Will picks the layout before the caption [rlbI4ztEBFU]. Kasey optimizes the photo with the first two lines [4jnS31Fh5QE].

## How to apply here

Pin the lane before any creator format. Classify the asset in one sentence. Choose the moat in `references/format-and-media-decision.md`: real photo first, carousel when the payload is saveable, video when it needs his voice. Write the Eden brief from `references/eden-briefs.md`. If the cast appears, `references/cast-and-generated-imagery.md` wins over Shanee's likeness render and Will's caricature. Profile photo, banner, and Featured edits are Gate-D. Do not schedule from here.
