# Lucy resume guidance

The following are paraphrases of the cited resume sources. They are guidance, not facts about the candidate.

- Select one target role and compare its actual requirements with the candidate's evidence. Read minimum qualifications before assuming fit. Sources: [rTAyTfEmxxs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rTAyTfEmxxs), [qCWJnjG97Jo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qCWJnjG97Jo), [5xiBqNiJQt0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xiBqNiJQt0).
- Make the top third and summary relevant to the employer's need. Use specific supported capabilities and proof instead of generic claims. Sources: [rTAyTfEmxxs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rTAyTfEmxxs), [rHUcY_EFI68](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHUcY_EFI68), [kHaVSZBVhQA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kHaVSZBVhQA).
- Describe achievements and impact instead of duty lists. Preserve who did what and how the result arose. Sources: [aNkYHiJ30y0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aNkYHiJ30y0), [ENFzdAutBHE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ENFzdAutBHE), [rTAyTfEmxxs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rTAyTfEmxxs).
- Use figures only when the candidate can defend them. Unknown metrics stay pending; they are never replaced with plausible estimates. Sources for defensibility: [5xiBqNiJQt0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xiBqNiJQt0), [9HFYE6a5Q8I](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9HFYE6a5Q8I). The placeholder and confirmation controls are this package's adaptation.
- Keep formatting restrained, readable, and easy to scan. Inspect the actual rendered document. Sources: [nUcrq7JjZnE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nUcrq7JjZnE), [rHUcY_EFI68](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHUcY_EFI68), [EHtHqo33i7s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EHtHqo33i7s).

The full working-draft-first sequence, source ledger, exact hash review, image gate, and first-five submission pilot are workflow adaptations. Do not attribute them as Lucy quotations or endorsements.
