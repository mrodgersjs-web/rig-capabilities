# Positioning, pages, advocacy, newsletters and measurement (Michelle J Raymond)

This is her home ground: the company page as a system, employee advocacy as its multiplier, and the positioning sentence that ties them together. Her one-line positioning is company-page-led growth scaled with employee advocacy, and she wants yours equally repeatable [mDsy5N6jgHs, c7GPLlwC0UM].

## Positioning

- **One sentence a stranger can repeat.** Name who you help and the problem; if you cannot say it, ask someone else to say it for you and adopt their version. Hers took six years to write [mDsy5N6jgHs].
- **One niche makes you the referral.** She dropped company pages because the niche felt small, then returned because that narrow niche is exactly what made her the referral; she regrets leaving her earlier beauty-industry selling niche [mOguk9ZaZPk, 1pjFtyAEVcc].
- **Narrow the who.** A year-stuck client moved after narrowing their message; a too-broad feed sells less (her cited 2025 research) [rW1CGbmLvzA].
- **Three conversations set the content map (2026-08):** sales (live buyer questions and objections), customers directly (including the buying committee: trigger, near-miss, why you, their words), and global plus regional together, ongoing. Only themes that repeat across all three earn a place [wZLL1QHLbvc].
- **Strategy before tools.** Customer research comes first; roughly 15,000 martech tools will not substitute for knowing how the buyer names their problem [R0OE2jitOo0].

## Company page doctrine (her specialty)

- **The Four C's (her name):** clarity, content, community, cadence. One owner, an audience a stranger can see, two or three posts a week for that audience, one CTA, and reporting on saves, reshares, visitors, new followers and demographics [K0D6kDr-WAY].
- **A page fails as a system, not from too few posts.** Her four-area diagnosis: missing strategy, change-chasing, inconsistency, thin capacity; outsourcing the page is allowed while the profile is not [vxmq9UDBs6s].
- **Three failing archetypes (2026-01):** ghost town (fix settings, tagline, about, banner), internal newsletter (return to strategy and decline one-off internal requests), product dump (lead with why the buyer cares; she uses a three-products, one-benefit constraint) [m8FkFDZJi-M].
- **Service mode over broadcast.** Marketers killed page reach with bulletin-board content and no point of view; benefit language, not features and heritage [jAzJ0hWhtqw, _yf76tCe-zY, _HK3z6D0SRY].
- **Comment as the page more than you broadcast,** and hold a point of view without picking fights; if playful is not your register, be the most precise expert instead [_yf76tCe-zY, jAzJ0hWhtqw].
- **Reverse-engineer a working page (her audit):** watch active pages with real comments, not just the biggest; check about keywords, featured recency and formats; filter their posts by media type and note comment-driving openings and CTAs; scan the ads library; use the admin feed and the built-in competitors report (nine pages, a 90-day window, reviewed monthly). Find the gap and differentiate; copying flattens results [x3d6HUw2T6s].
- **Page invites (creator claims):** under 100,000 followers, 250 follow-invite credits a month, first-degree only, expiring unused, accepted about 40 to 60 percent when targeted; spend them early in the month so new followers see that month's content. Her workaround when an admin's network is small is to grant a colleague temporary admin rights to spend credits [n_jsi6uAALE]. A guest agency adds 30 employee invites each and reports targeted acceptance around 20 to 30 percent versus about 5 percent untargeted [0nz_nzgaJrY].
- **Dated platform change 2025-11:** the free employee-advocacy tabs were removed, pushing programs to manual process or paid tools [ByOGGcK0bi8].

## Employee advocacy ("power of two")

- **The page brings consistency, clarity and infrastructure; people bring humanity, expertise, trust and reach** (her paired model) [-ES-EKvATMs, c7GPLlwC0UM].
- **Three tiers (her guest's model):** executives get draft, publish and report; experts get prompts; general staff get three to five options with blanks to fill. Do not force non-believers [LKUcEKAMmLo].
- **About nine in ten people asked to post do not want to be creators** (creator claim); enablement beats enforcement, guidelines beat policing, and early wins are the retention lever [MKsOihMy68M, -ES-EKvATMs].
- **Alignment first:** hacks fail while sales, marketing, the page, advocacy and leaders run separate plans; one room for goals and hot accounts, one plan with shared metrics, a named page owner [c7GPLlwC0UM, wZLL1QHLbvc].
- **Don't clone the corporate post onto people.** The page educates; people carry clicks. A personal post can out-draw two million page followers [LKUcEKAMmLo].

## Selling and pipeline

- **Value, then relationship, then the clear offer** (her three-step). Refusing to sell hurts B2B; spray-and-pray is the failure mode, not the offer [dNDNxyoD4qE].
- **Posting alone stopped producing inbound** (her 2025 update): the mix is posts for credibility, comments for discovery, DMs for deals [kXBXV1bQKes].
- **Soft and hard CTA slots** for sell posts, and a 15-minute intro call as the standard ask [dNDNxyoD4qE, K0D6kDr-WAY, hlAoLfF7t2A].
- **Warm signals over cold lists:** poll voters, newsletter subscribers and commenters get a question, not a pitch [k3dCMwu8Nmk, TtJFDeUqUNM, xR6t2llZRKQ].

## Newsletters and owned audience

- **Grow on LinkedIn, own on email.** The LinkedIn newsletter is the discovery engine; the owned list is the asset and the insurance if the platform moves; the Substack option is community plus paid subscriptions. Master one platform first [TtJFDeUqUNM, kyFnmN5Gu78].
- **The first-edition effect (creator claim):** the first issue reaches every connection and follower, the only send that does, and about 30 percent of them tend to subscribe [kyFnmN5Gu78, SjhzoOD6Vmk].
- **Write a promise before a name (slot):** this newsletter helps one audience do one thing so they gain one outcome [SjhzoOD6Vmk].
- **Editorial standard:** one clear idea per issue, strong titles, real point of view, sustainable cadence, no approval bottleneck; expanding an idea is fine, recycling alone is not [SjhzoOD6Vmk].
- **The AI-citation bet (2026-03):** LinkedIn.com is cited more for professional queries, and headed newsletters and articles are the surface she expects to be cited, not viral posts; posting more is not proven to buy AI visibility. By 2026-06 she warns that launching a newsletter only for AI citations mostly damages brands [vMfQ3tewli8, SjhzoOD6Vmk].
- **Her own numbers drift** (8,500 versus 82,000 subscribers in one episode), so treat every figure here as a creator claim [kyFnmN5Gu78].

## Measurement

- **Score saves, sends, visits and follows, not impressions.** The June 2025 shift ended impression-counting as the goal; new analytics surface saves, reposts, link clicks and followers generated [lYqfrZZA64o, Nj8yZyzXi1E].
- **Report pages in four parts:** visibility, resonance, credibility and opportunity (guest's split), so results are not blamed on the algorithm [0nz_nzgaJrY].
- **Follower count, dated reversal:** she once said it does not matter; by early 2026 she calls it social proof that compounds credibility [0nz_nzgaJrY].
- **Follower count is still a bad success metric for a show or a niche**; her best clients arrived through the podcast, six or seven of ten in her telling [1pjFtyAEVcc].
- **Qualitative wins count:** trade-show conversations started by a six-month-old post, analyst coverage, in-person recognition (guest's measurement style) [rQFfWJrQkKo].

## Growth shortcuts she refuses

Pods, bought followers (she cites about 350 dollars for 10,000), rented accounts and rule-breaking automation destroy reputation and accounts; honest path is slow curation [THXjj1yM6Kw, 1pjFtyAEVcc]. She also flags automated-comment vendors and job-ad follower gaming as traps [lYqfrZZA64o, 0nz_nzgaJrY].
