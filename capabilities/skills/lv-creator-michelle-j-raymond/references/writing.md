# Body craft, formats and CTAs (Michelle J Raymond)

Her writing doctrine is buyer-first and anti-polish: one person, one problem, one next step, said in the reader's language, with the offer named out loud [xR6t2llZRKQ, udtNia_2Onk, 2JI55PLI9Zw]. She treats "professional" tone as a conversion killer for senior buyers and blames cloned AI beige for most invisible content [nC06xaoVWPw, cuM8ySMSxHc, hlAoLfF7t2A].

## The one-post contract

- **One persona, one problem, one differentiator, one next step.** Pick a business outcome first, not impressions; a post that names several buyers converts none of them [xR6t2llZRKQ, 8Yrgj-zOYQ8].
- **Write to their KPI, not the internal ask.** Map the buyer's day, then write to the metric they own; jargon and company-news framing are what gets scrolled past [udtNia_2Onk, _HK3z6D0SRY].
- **The RATE filter (hers):** Relevant, Authentic, Thought leadership, Empathy. She aims for about 80 percent of posts passing all four, with the rest free to be for fun (creator claim) [udtNia_2Onk].
- **A level-four reading level is fine, even for a CEO.** Simple language is not a downgrade [udtNia_2Onk].

## Voice

- **Write the way you talk face to face** (her "digital twin" test). Humor and emotion are allowed; a LinkedIn-version voice is not [Dd42KDThdPY].
- **Pass the remove-name test** with word choice and a consistent signature detail [Dd42KDThdPY].
- **Too much polish kills results.** Unpolished can beat over-polished, and a guarded brand voice strips the opinions that make thought leadership work [1pjFtyAEVcc, xR6t2llZRKQ, Dd42KDThdPY].
- **A model averages the internet.** AI copy reads as beige clone; the writing pass is where the thinking happens, so a model may draft or edit only after a human thought [cuM8ySMSxHc, nC06xaoVWPw, OCpDoffJO_I].

## Body structure and length

- **The body after the hook can run long** (she cites about a thousand characters as a working space) but only earns its length when the reader tapped through [Dd42KDThdPY].
- **No length spec is taught.** Staying on the post is a writing goal (holding attention), not a word count [xR6t2llZRKQ].
- **Repeat what you do.** A drumbeat of the name and the promise beats novelty; repeating the offer is required even at a large following [xR6t2llZRKQ, rW1CGbmLvzA].
- **Narrow copy builds a group people want to join.** Drifting toward "everyone" is the failure she admits in her own 2025 feed [rW1CGbmLvzA].

## Post types she teaches

- **Sell posts are required, not optional.** Silence about the offer dries up pipeline; problem-agitation-solution mixed with insight posts is her non-cringe frame, and volume without an offer creates no leads (five problems, five posts) [2JI55PLI9Zw, dNDNxyoD4qE].
- **Insight posts make the offer learnable.** Teach the decision first, then offer; trust is value, then relationship, then a clear ask [dNDNxyoD4qE].
- **Story posts:** a lived scene is the 2026 defense against copies; a model can edit your story but cannot invent it [1pjFtyAEVcc, nC06xaoVWPw].
- **Polls:** an easy question with two obvious answers plus a third option that routes "it depends" people to the comments; run one week; keep answers short (she cites about a 30-character limit) (creator claims) [k3dCMwu8Nmk, nC06xaoVWPw].
- **Newsletters and articles:** long-form explanation with headings, one clear idea per edition; they are the surface she expects AI engines to cite [SjhzoOD6Vmk, vMfQ3tewli8].
- **Bullet-point beats a generic announcement** when handing a story to whoever writes the post [rQFfWJrQkKo].

## CTAs

- **One next step per post.** A free intro call is a legitimate soft CTA; a hard CTA is a named number of spots (slots only) [xR6t2llZRKQ, dNDNxyoD4qE].
- **A post with no ask is a broadcast.** On pages she holds one CTA per post; a question is the easy reply format [_HK3z6D0SRY, K0D6kDr-WAY].
- **The offer appears after the conversation, not instead of it.** Her soft slot: if you are stuck on the named problem, reply and something useful comes back to you [dNDNxyoD4qE].

## Applying it (RIG)

lv-draft owns the Eden brief. Feed Eden a real story, transcript or receipt; never a bare topic and never a blank-to-draft request. Every claim in the body carries its receipt, and the CTA is a slot filled with Mike's real offer. Sell posts are a Gate-D item only when they name a price or a commitment; a drafted sell post that stays a draft is free. Jev gates the draft (`post.linkedin`, `post.eden`) before any frontier model weighs in; DEFERRED when the classifier is down. Her RATE filter is a pre-gate checklist, not a substitute for the gate.
