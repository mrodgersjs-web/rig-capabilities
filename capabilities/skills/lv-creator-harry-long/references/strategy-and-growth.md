2. **26 July.** Small yeses: blank connect, a 15-second voice note, permission, then a screen video on their page. Opener shape: an apology plus one noticed fact. Four or five videos a day, only after a yes. Non-buyers may return in 6 months. This replaces a letter menu [0kLvQW0aPJE].
3. **6 Aug.** After 85,000 DMs, he says, voice plus a one-letter menu. Four buckets: ready, later, not interested, rude opt-out. Automate the note rather than record 25 a day (he says that would take 2 hours). Formal industries get text, not voice. Do not chase hard nos. He claims 24 percent replies versus 1 to 2 percent, and that the four-bucket menu tripled replies with no base shown. This, not the screen video, "works every time." [smWHLEKYucQ]
4. **20 Aug.** Views do not pay. Attract, convert, retain. A DM within 5 minutes of a like, comment, or connect, written from their profile. He claims 70 to 80 percent replies versus 1 to 2 percent, and closes at 5 times, with no shared test against the 24 percent. He had said do not ask a stranger for a call; this video drops that [bGPALjhoXYA].
5. **5 to 16 Sept.** Browser-driven model reads the open tab, drafts a researched DM or a voice script from three recent facts, sorts the inbox hot, later, or dead. He claims about 10 seconds a message, 10 times replies and closes with no base, and 40 to 50 messages a day. "Love your content" and "love what you do at [company]" fail. The old "not now" bucket is now "tell me more." Later waits about 6 months. Each letter gets an email path. One pain, one letter. The opt-out is the disruption. A 15-minute call is the meeting, not the first ask [rGWmeMul31I].
