This doctrine governs the creation of music using SUNO AI with the explicit goal of producing Billboard-charting songs that stay on charts for 52+ weeks. Every song must pass anti-generic gates. A song that sounds like it could have been made by any AI music tool is not a RIG song — it is background noise. --- ## Core Belief The average of all popular songs is a forgettable song. Hit songs are statistical outliers — they deviate from the median in specific, measurable ways. RIG OS applies the same deviation engine to music that it applies to websites, emails, and strategy. The goal is not to make music that is "good" — it is to make music that is impossible to forget. --- ## The Generic Baseline (What 0 Sounds Like) A 0-deviation song has: - Predictable chord progression (I-V-vi-IV or similar) - Generic lyrical themes ("love," "party," "hustle") with no wound precision - Standard verse-chorus-verse-chorus-bridge-chorus structure with no rupture - BPM in the safe zone (100–130) with no rhythmic surprise

Not "I'm sad" but "I keep checking your location even though I deleted the app." - WoundPrecision ≥ 0.6 — the listener recognizes their own experience - Concrete imagery over abstract language. Specific nouns, specific verbs, specific places. - Repetition is strategic — the hook repeats enough for mere exposure effect but not so much it becomes wallpaper **Structure:** - Time-to-first-hook: under 15 seconds. The listener must hear the melodic hook before they decide to skip.
