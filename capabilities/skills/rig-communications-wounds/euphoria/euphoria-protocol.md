## Overview The Euphoria Protocol is the counterpart to the Wound Protocol. Where the Wound Protocol maps the 8 core wounds (exile-level pain that drives human behavior), the Euphoria Protocol maps the 8 core euphoria states — the neurochemical and psychological peaks that make someone feel alive, seen, powerful, and bonded. The Emotional Symphony Framework then fuses both into a single orchestration system that scores every piece of content as a **composed emotional experience** with deliberate highs, lows, pauses, and crescendos. Core principle: **The high only works because of the low that preceded it.** Constant positivity becomes invisible (hedonic adaptation). Only contrast is felt. The wound makes the win feel like salvation. The win makes the next wound feel like betrayal. The oscillation is what keeps attention locked. --- ## The 8 Core Euphoria States ### E1 — "I Am Seen" (Mirror Neuron Activation + Oxytocin)

### Key Scientific Principles - **Kahneman's Peak-End Rule:** People don't remember the average of an experience. They remember the peak (most intense moment) and the end (how it concluded). Duration is irrelevant. - **Intermittent Reinforcement (Variable Ratio Schedule):** Unpredictable rewards produce the highest response rates and most resistance to extinction. Content cadence should be variable — not every post is a high, not every email is warm. The unpredictability is the mechanism. - **Hedonic Adaptation:** Humans adapt to any constant emotional state. Constant positivity becomes invisible. Constant negativity becomes tolerable. Only contrast is felt. --- ## Scoring Formulas ### Emotional Amplitude Score (EAS) ``` EAS = (Peak_Intensity × End_Intensity) / Emotional_Flatline_Duration ``` - Peak_Intensity (1-10): How intense is the single most emotionally charged moment? - End_Intensity (1-10): How does it end? Resolved but resonant = 10. Flat = 1.
