## 3. The asset types he builds

- **Niche infographic or list visual:** niche title, a center icon or figure, many short truths, keep the ones that hit you. He reports this pattern at 10,000+ followers early on and cites later list visuals at 160,000 to 500,000 views when great, versus 25,000 to 50,000 when weak (creator claims) [3UcZFurI_yw, D_MY33bviuQ].
- **Side-by-side contrast:** stereotype of the role versus the real job, or struggling state versus better state; light background, dark text, center figure, red and green accents [O1LeyZsrnls, D_MY33bviuQ, 3UcZFurI_yw].
- **Whiteboard style:** hand-drawn comparison layouts, which he calls trending because still uncommon; he cites one at 185,000 views and another past 200,000 (creator claims) [D_MY33bviuQ, 5rLlzb6_vgA].
- **Signature caricature:** run a real photo through an image model, vary the expression, keep one recognizable marker (his example: a consistent hoodie) so the asset becomes brand memory; he uses it on broad carousels and cites a caricature post around 300,000 to 400,000 views (creator claim) [wVWXbYuoXYg].
- **Proof visuals:** community screenshots, message screenshots, before-and-after numbers rendered as an image. A case study without a proof visual is not believed [TTH-75XOgJE, 0_8TCrdBPVM].
- **Scripted testimonial visual:** capture the client saying the decision-relevant things, not general praise [0_8TCrdBPVM].
- **News-clip curiosity visual:** a news-shaped layout that creates a gap the caption fills [pyPoIYDg_a0].
- **Spectacle asset:** an oversized check, an offline billboard, or the stunt itself becomes the content; he describes a billboard campaign where the decode game was the content [F8IGilhJItA].

## 4. Video

Under 60 seconds, captions burned in, the hook inside 2 seconds, shot in varied real settings because strangers recognize physical details and comment on them (his account) [5rLlzb6_vgA, wVWXbYuoXYg]. Tag the client when fair [5rLlzb6_vgA]. He keeps the video habit for trust rather than reach [Ej7WFBI9NS4, 5rLlzb6_vgA].

## 5. System rules

- **One color system for a year.** Profile and content share colors so the feed asset is recognizable before the name is read [Ql4gXPDhalk, UJvAtV63vk0]. A big brand-colored number draws the eye [5rLlzb6_vgA].
- **Jump early on a new visual format,** including when smaller creators are the ones trying it; early niche infographics beat later copies in his account [3UcZFurI_yw, D_MY33bviuQ].
- **Cheap beats absent.** He says an ugly asset outperforms no asset when the message needs one [UJvAtV63vk0].
- **Tools he names:** Canva and Figma for layouts, image models (he names ChatGPT image generation and Gemini) for caricatures and photo cleanup, remove.bg for cutouts, a pro shoot or headshot service for the profile photo [Ql4gXPDhalk, wVWXbYuoXYg, D_MY33bviuQ].
- **Profile specs (his numbers):** headshot at least 400 by 400, banner 1584 by 386, banner holds one offer, one CTA and proof with the left side clear; photo is a close-up with a solid background, not a selfie and not a group shot [Ql4gXPDhalk].
