# Substack doctrine

## JEV-first mandate (read before applying the doctrine)

Judgments this doctrine drives (positioning fit, title choice, note quality, peer fit, offer soundness, flop cause) go to Jev through `rig-s1gate` BEFORE any frontier LLM call. DEFERRED when the classifier is down; never a guessed verdict. An advisory PASS never authorizes a public action.

## What this is

- **Corpus**: `data/corpora/substack/` (local; transcripts are not republished). 914 transcripts (808 growth wave + 106 bestseller wave under `substack-bestseller/`), 0 duplicate ids, 3 videos with no English captions logged in `errors.jsonl`. Bestseller-wave selection: Jev `corpus.source` with a bestseller seed, decisions 121978-121985, planted off-subject control 121986 (0.02-0.08); scores in `discovery/jev-bestseller/_scores.json`, plan in `discovery/scrape_plan_bestseller.json`, dossier cut by `build_bestseller_dossier.py`.
- **Channels** (selection evidence and Jev decision ids in `data/corpora/substack/TARGETS.md`): Sinem Günel, Matt Giaro, Substack Growth, Lori Ballen, Claire Venus (@creativelyconsciousUK), Mark Thompson, Amy Suto (@sutoscience), Nicolas Cole, Solo Shannon, The Coach's Alchemist; plus @substackinc as platform ground truth only.
- **Honest limits**: Jev scored no candidate as a true fit (best same_domain 0.82); these are the closest available peers. Most are creator-economy writers, not B2B operators, so their monetization advice leans toward paid subscriptions and courses; the RIG application sections correct for that.
- **Fidelity**: 12 random transcripts re-fetched by a second method matched at bigram precision and recall 1.000; the comparator was proven non-vacuous (a different video scores 0.29 recall, a half-truncated one 0.50).

## How to use a law

1. Find it in `references/law-index.md` by stage.
2. `consensus` (2+ channels) can drive a plan; `single-source` is a hypothesis to test and must be labelled as such when it drives a recommendation.
3. Read the verbatim quotes and the context in `data/corpora/substack/notes/<stage>.md`, or open the transcript by id from the manifest.
4. Creator numbers ("I grew to 10K in 6 months") are UNVERIFIED. Never present them to Mike's buyers as facts.
5. Platform mechanics come only from @substackinc windows and Substack's help center; re-check a feature in the live product before relying on it (the corpus itself records Notes scheduling changing in March 2026).

## Cross-stage laws that carry the most weight

Laws with the widest channel support, across stages (see the index for the full wording and ids):
- Consistency on a fixed day beats volume; one issue per week is the sustainable baseline (ss-plan).
- The About page and the welcome email are the two sales documents and are usually left on defaults (ss-publish, ss-strategy).
- Subscriptions are the audience engine; the money is usually in what is sold on the back end (ss-convert).
- Every post needs one next step (ss-convert).
