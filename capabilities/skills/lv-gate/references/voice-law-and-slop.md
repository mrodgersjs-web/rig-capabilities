# Voice law and de-slop: the human-shipping pass

## The anti-slop loop

Run the loop on any AI-assisted copy before it reaches the critic: lint for AI tells, rewrite to remove
them, cleanse the rewrite through a rival model pass, then lint again. Lint-clean is the exit condition.
anti-slop and slopmonster are the same engine; use one, not both. The point is copy a human would ship,
not copy that merely passes a filter [anti-slop, slopmonster].

## Voice law (the Lane A standard the Eden checker enforces)

- Positive first, contrarian second. The reader should feel encouraged to think, not corrected for not
  knowing. The warm move says the hard thing kindly and includes the writer in the diagnosis. Never
  cynical, never dunking, never scolding.
- Scarcity is specificity: every post earns attention with at least one of a specific number, a named
  study, a dated prediction or a firsthand observation. Vague posts fail.
- One idea per post. Depth reads as expertise; breadth reads as content.
- Short sentences after the turn: long for setup, short for landing.

## Banned elements (a hit fails the draft)

Em dashes (the AI tell punctuation). The stock vocabulary: delve, tapestry, unleash, unlock, leverage,
robust, journey, landscape, navigate, foster, harness, vibrant, dynamic, holistic, paradigm. The
"not X, it's Y" reframe. Reflex tricolons. Throat-clearing openers ("let me explain", "here's the
thing", "it's worth noting"). A sign-off that is not the doctrine sign-off for that day.

## Sign-off and cast by day

- Tuesday posts close with the Birch sign-off. Required.
- Never use the Birch sign-off outside Tuesday.
- Wednesday through Saturday are Coach posts: they close warm and helpful (the Lasso close) and never
  carry the Birch sign-off.
- Cast assignment: Birch and Unit 7 carry the Monday, Tuesday and Sunday story posts; The Coach carries
  Wednesday through Saturday. A wrong cast on a wrong day fails the draft. Birch is never rendered
  outside its days; The Coach is never face-on and never illustrated.

## Pop-culture hook position (Lane A)

The hook (song line, movie line, TV or historical reference) must be the FIRST line, not the second,
not the closing thought. The reference earns the scroll, then the post bridges to the thesis. The
four-beat shape: hook, bridge, unique angle, warm close.

## Editing rule

When fitting an existing draft to the doctrine, make light adjustments: move the reference to the first
line, add the correct close, swap in the right sign-off. Preserve the substance; do not rewrite the
voice or replace the argument.

## Checker output contract

The audit returns PASS or FAIL with five sections: banned words found, sign-off rule, cast assignment,
hook position, and a 0-10 score across specificity, positive framing, the warm move, the four-beat
shape and absence of banned words. Anything below 7 needs another pass. PASS only when all four hard
checks succeed and the score is 7 or higher [voice-law-checker].
