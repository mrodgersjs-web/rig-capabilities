# Evidence repair examples

An ideal pattern can read: "Reduced [confirmed cost measure] by [confirmed result] through [actual intervention], across [scope] during [period]." Keep it in the labeled ideal-pattern section until the candidate assigns a supported actual employer and example.

If a source says an inventory balance fell, do not rewrite it as recurring annual operating savings. Keep the inventory measure, amount, and known period. If no measurement period exists, do not invent one from the employment dates.

If an example describes a team result, preserve the candidate's personal contribution separately. "Helped secure financing" cannot become "raised all financing" without new factual evidence.

An interview proposal such as "I would pilot this with one team" remains proposed. Approval of that phrasing does not establish a completed pilot. A working resume may use it only as a labeled bracketed achievement pattern, and a final resume needs an actual confirmed example.

After confirmation, show exact before/after text and the source/evidence ID. If the candidate cannot substantiate a pattern, omit it or use a narrower truthful statement. Do not fill the missing result with a typical industry benchmark.
