# Writing (Escobar, 2026)

She writes for qualified pipeline, not for a viral score. A post that cannot name its bucket is not ready. [ZAqIUNAvXXo, R8nrEQx8rEY]

## Four buckets, and the mix shift

Each bucket has one job. Growth gets seen. Authority makes them stay. Conversion asks. Personal makes you memorable. A point of view runs through all four. Growth-only audiences never buy. Authority-only audiences never see you. [w3_109ygioY, R8nrEQx8rEY, _ZFncQNrOhw]

The ratio is dated. Do not quote a single mix as if it never moved.

- 2026-03-05: 35 growth / 35 authority / 20 conversion / 10 personal. [ZAqIUNAvXXo]
- 2026-04-03: 40 / 30 / 20 / 10. More reach, less pure authority, conversion and personal unchanged. [R8nrEQx8rEY]
- 2026-07-21: 40 / 30 / 20 / 10 restated. That is the current mix in this corpus. [_ZFncQNrOhw]

CTAs match the bucket. Growth can ask a question or ask for nothing. Authority can offer a comment or a DM for a template. Conversion can point at a link or a keyword. Personal often has no ask. [ZAqIUNAvXXo]

She also wants one topic lane for 90 days. A feed that jumps niches is, in her reading, unmatchable by retrieval. Creator claim, unverified. [PxjDCR29aKc]

## Body craft

Short paragraphs, about two or three lines, white space, language around a 7th to 8th grade reading level, and a read-aloud pass before you ship. [ZAqIUNAvXXo]

Write the body before the hook. Put numbers and a named framework in the body, then lift the hook from that body. [ZAqIUNAvXXo, _ZFncQNrOhw]

Earn saves, not just likes. Frameworks, data, playbooks, and checklists get saved. Motivational lines do not, in her reading. She cites a vendor claim that one save is about five times the reach impact of one like. Creator claim, unverified, and not a Mike result. [PxjDCR29aKc]

The one-sentence-per-line dramatic pause is the layer she mocks. Specific frameworks, real tools, and a human voice. No corporate fog. [u66RUn_rTbc]

## How-I replaced how-to as the differentiator

On 2026-03-14 she softened her own teaching format. Generic how-to now competes with free AI answers. The durable move is how-I: what you did, why, the outcome, the failure, and the actual artifact (a screen, a doc, a number). Instructional posts can still exist. They no longer differentiate alone. [a4uL3wTtjFk]

Five types she says people still seek: long writing that connects dots, work that shows effort, a process demonstrated, an interview with checkable experience, and a warm presence. [a4uL3wTtjFk]

For AI-search citation she wants three writing moves on every post: one real technical detail (a number, a tool, a method), plain-text names of companies, people, and tools (an at-tag is not required), and one narrow topic instead of a category. She claims technical detail lifted citations about 77% with no reaction effect, named entities about 33% on citations, and topic specificity about 18%. Unicode-styled letters cut ChatGPT citations about 58% in the study she cites. Perplexity and Google AI mode were fine in that same claim. All of these are creator claims, unverified. [D9DKTj85wWc]

Publish one article of 500 to 2,000 words in the month, often by converting a post you already wrote (she says that conversion is about 20 minutes). She claims articles are 50% to 66% of cited LinkedIn content in the studies she read, and that a feed post plus an article hits two indexing paths. Creator claims. [D9DKTj85wWc]

## Where the words come from

Do not sit down to invent a topic. Collect from sales calls, internal docs (the thinking behind them, not just the deck), and interviews with the people who already do the work. She claims experts volunteered the useful material nine times out of ten. Creator claim. [Z3jHnYT8iJo, w3_109ygioY]

Every objection is a post. Every prospect question is a hook line. Steal working formats, not someone else's idea, then transform them with experience you actually have. [Z3jHnYT8iJo]

A text post that hits can move up a ladder: text, then carousel, then video. Do not start at video. [Z3jHnYT8iJo]

News posts must say what the event means in one sentence. If you cannot, you are not ready to publish it. The window she cites is 24 to 48 hours. [twBrrKkhpOg, KlawrDJuqYc]

## AI in the writing room

Her writing stack, as of 2026-03-05: a kanban for ideas, a drive of transcripts and decks, and a model project with three docs (positioning, north-star lines plus tone rules, and a source map). The project also holds role, identity, contrarian beliefs, a banned-word list, best posts, and a folder of posts she hates. She names Claude for that project. [ZAqIUNAvXXo]

Her banned-word examples overlap Mike's own ban list (hype verbs such as leverage and unlock, plus empty intensifiers). Keep both lists. Do not treat her list as permission to add new hype.

On 2026-03-27 the news workflow emits about 10 drafts a morning. She is explicit: never publish the model draft as-is. A human spends about 5 to 10 minutes sharpening voice and the take. [KlawrDJuqYc]

## Applying it (RIG)

Eden drafts. The brief is a real story, a call transcript, or a receipt, plus the bucket and the CTA. A bare topic, or a news scrape with no Mike mechanism, is refused. That is stricter than her overnight agent and matches Clark's ban on blank-to-draft.

Lane B uses the 40/30/20/10 mix only as a planning hint, dated to her 2026-04-03 shift, not as a quota that forces a hot take. Conversion posts use numbers Mike can source. Personal posts do not import her firing story or her client revenue claims.

Articles are allowed when they are a longer receipt, 500 to 2,000 words, plain text, no stylized Unicode. They are not a second brand voice. Job-search writing stays in the job lane. Anything about a minor is out of this file.
