Sub-skill of `lv-creator-harry-long`. Synthesis in RIG's words. Bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims. This is the richest golden-hour source in the set, and it is also the source that demoted the golden hour. Date both.

On 4 Aug 2026 commenting becomes step one. Frame: rent reach with posts, borrow it in comments, own the relationship on an email list. A comment is a mini post and needs a point, not praise [q_EkkPZ1vX4]. He cites other people's volumes as class claims, not his own results: one creator at 100 to 300 comments a day during a growth spurt, Lara Acosta at 4 to 5 hours of commenting, another creator's email list in the hundreds of thousands. Do not copy those volumes onto Mike's account [q_EkkPZ1vX4].

On 25 Sept his account of her comment block drops to about an hour a day, done by her, not a team. He also cites, as her medians, hundreds of comments on a typical post and about 6 comments per 10 likes. Those are his report of her account, unverified here [kK5H2Uu1L6Y]. Working volume if this doctrine is used: about an hour of real comments, not 4 to 5 hours, and not a quota that trips `linkedin-site-mechanics`.

Company pages: comment as the company, tag the page, reshare both ways. Invite credits are capped and, he says, refunded on accept. Creator claim, no method shown for the refund [JAta-iCI5gk].

## The first hour (pre-2 Sept model)

On 31 July the first hour decides viral or dead. Reply in that hour, including DMs. No named clock time for when to publish [XbDKE82gRf0]. On 22 July, reply fast; comments can out-impress the post; 30 comments beat 300 likes; 50 readers beat 100 skim likes. All creator claims, unverified [fWhBOPC1eLA]. On 29 Aug he names the first 60 minutes the golden hour: self-comment, park the link there, reply at once. Comments, he claims, reach 2 to 3 times further, and likes are about 1 percent of distribution while dwell is over 15 percent. Unverified. Papers unnamed [qt_YMXlefXo].

On 23 Aug the cross-app version is: first viewers already know you, reply fast, one negative can cancel about four positives, never buy engagement. No 60-minute rule in that video [QKM9Uy8_1Lw].

## The 2 Sept demotion (do not delete the reply habit)

On 2 Sept 2026 he said an AI reader replaced the engagement test. Buyers may never comment. Likes are the wrong score. The old few-minute test is not replaced with a new minute count; the golden hour is demoted, not given a successor clock [sUC5UAXni6M]. The reply habit is not retired. On 5 Sept inbox triage (hot, later, dead) exists so a human can reply faster, still with no minute count [NqHd-89RycA].

Practical split: if you are executing his July-August model, stay the first 60 minutes, self-comment, park the link, reply. If you are executing his 2 Sept model, judge the post by whether the right people viewed the profile, and still reply to anyone who did comment. Do not run both scoreboards as if they were one.

## Pods, bait, and automation

No pods, no comment bait, no bought engagement. He calls pods the old rig on 8 Sept [fWhBOPC1eLA, XbDKE82gRf0, QKM9Uy8_1Lw, hWhYdDbcVCg]. Early videos auto-visit a profile and leave one like as part of a connect sequence. That is outbound theater, not a comment practice, and it is not allowed as automation on Mike's account [voRG26IXYS8, aWw3kS2hOBI].

A like, comment, or connect triggering a DM within 5 minutes (20 Aug) is an outreach rule, not an engagement rule. It conflicts with "do not ask a stranger for a call," which he had said earlier and then dropped. See `references/strategy-and-growth.md` [bGPALjhoXYA].
