# AI-era engineering: what survives code generation

Purpose: state the author's positioning for front-end engineers in the AI era: fundamentals
underneath, architecture direction on top, and the value areas that are not being automated.

When to load: when asked how AI changes front-end career value, when choosing what to study next,
when positioning a résumé or profile for the AI era, and when someone frames vibe coding as a
replacement for engineering judgment.
