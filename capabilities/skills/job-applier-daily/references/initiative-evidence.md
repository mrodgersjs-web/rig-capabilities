# Employer initiatives and personal contribution

Research both the target employer's relevant current priorities and source-backed initiatives at Mike's actual former employers that may explain role fit. Prefer dated first-party sources. Record source ID, employer, initiative title, source URL, publication/event dates, captured excerpt/hash, relevance, and uncertainty. Do not assume current activity from an old announcement.

For former-employer initiatives, compare confirmed employment dates and role before proposing participation questions. Temporal overlap is a reason to ask, never proof. For target-employer initiatives, describe a prospective application of supported experience unless Mike independently confirms he worked on them.

Create a claim record containing initiative_id, requirement_ids, participation_state, participation_response_ref, employer/role/period, personal_action, result, metric_scope, inclusion_state, inclusion_response_ref, and exact draft hash. Participation states are unknown, confirmed, corrected, or denied. Inclusion states are pending, include, or omit. Confirmation never silently changes inclusion. Denied participation bars first-person attribution. Confirmed plus omit remains excluded. Pending may appear only as a red explicitly unconfirmed proposal or worksheet question, never a clean claim.

Ask concise questions after presenting useful provisional drafts. One question establishes participation and scope. A second asks whether to include the supported contribution in this role's resume and/or cover letter. Preserve separate responses. A combined reply is acceptable only if it answers both explicitly. Approval of prose alone supplies neither missing fact nor missing result.
