# layout: looks like / does not

Owning main skill: ds-layout. Every row paraphrases a good-versus-bad contrast a creator showed, cited to the moment. Use as calibration before judging a screen; a card is only reusable as a visual reference after Jev ui.design scores the good side above the bad side.

| # | looks like (good) | does not (bad) | why | citation |
|---|-------------------|----------------|-----|----------|
| 1 | A quiet field, a small corner mark, type that still reads with room at the lower right | Type scaled up and a texture poured in to kill the empty half | Empty space is what lets the viewer sort the information | CLAIM(EFf9jBs2yfU@00:43, EFf9jBs2yfU@01:27) |
| 2 | Headline with body, the button alone, icons in a third cluster | A button jammed against body copy, or related cards spaced so far they no longer group | Proximity is the cue; too much gap kills it as surely as too little | CLAIM(EFf9jBs2yfU@03:34, cf95Z7Ngg8k@02:52) |
| 3 | Most edges on shared axes, one price badge deliberately off-grid | Every element nudged by eye | A single break reads as emphasis only if the rest is aligned | CLAIM(EFf9jBs2yfU@08:32, EFf9jBs2yfU@08:47) |
| 4 | Largest contrasted image, medium headline, small supporting type | A field of medium-sized, medium-contrast objects | Without a size jump there is no entry point | CLAIM(9eJYjNGeIOM@03:08, 9eJYjNGeIOM@03:19) |
| 5 | Front and back sharing type and layout, one side dark and one light | Two sides that do not look like the same brand | A card is one object seen twice | CLAIM(5zS-5XDo4WQ@01:07, 5zS-5XDo4WQ@01:15) |
| 6 | Important type held inside a margin | Type running into the cut | Edge-hugging type looks unfinished after the cutter moves | CLAIM(5zS-5XDo4WQ@03:00, 5zS-5XDo4WQ@03:10) |
| 7 | Filled high-contrast button, order set by size and weight | An outline-only button on a page built to an F diagram | Scanners do not follow one diagram, and ghost buttons disappear | CLAIM(1NTKwpAVcHg@01:05, 1NTKwpAVcHg@01:53) |
| 8 | A 50 to 75 character measure, then bullets with icons | One solid paragraph block | Scanners leave at the wall of copy, and lists give an entry point | CLAIM(TeMtV8BaZt0@01:14, TeMtV8BaZt0@01:51) |
| 9 | 16 px inside a group, 32 px between groups, the same large gap on both sides of a hero | Random gaps, or the same gap used for every relationship | Rhythm is what people mean by clean | CLAIM(TeMtV8BaZt0@04:45, TeMtV8BaZt0@05:26, cf95Z7Ngg8k@04:37) |
| 10 | Heading line-height equal to size, body near 1.5 | Body leading packed at 1:1 | Body needs air; a display line should stay one unit | CLAIM(TeMtV8BaZt0@06:05, TeMtV8BaZt0@06:50) |
| 11 | Display tracking tightened, body at 0, CTA tracking opened | Default tracking on a huge headline, or body tracked open | Tight display scans; open body hurts reading; open CTA slows the click label | CLAIM(TeMtV8BaZt0@09:28, TeMtV8BaZt0@09:35) |
| 12 | A soft texture with a wash behind the subhead, content still winning a squint | Dots, gradients, and shadows competing with the subhead, form field gone in the blur | Ornaments are subordinate; the squint is the check | CLAIM(TeMtV8BaZt0@16:06, TeMtV8BaZt0@19:32, TeMtV8BaZt0@20:10) |
| 13 | At least 6 px between an icon and its label; 24 px screen inset; 16 px inside the card | A 1 px gap that only looks fine at thumbnail size | Tight pairs fall apart when the screen scales | CLAIM(cf95Z7Ngg8k@02:08, cf95Z7Ngg8k@07:14) |
| 14 | Heading closer to its cards than to the previous section label | Equal gaps so every block looks equally related | The smaller gap is the group boundary | CLAIM(cf95Z7Ngg8k@08:09) |
| 15 | A feature image crossing three columns, a best-seller cell spanning two modules | Every block forced to one width and one cell size | A small intentional break, or a larger cell, is how importance is spatial | CLAIM(gUzkRue28VI@01:05, gUzkRue28VI@01:47) |
| 16 | Cropped, graded, high-resolution photo whose colors match the site | Random smiling stock, a bad cutout, or a photo whose palette fights the brand | An image without a job, or with the wrong colors, reads as unfinished | CLAIM(9eJYjNGeIOM@02:22, ityZtG3FMgw@04:41, ityZtG3FMgw@04:51) |
| 17 | A row whose children fill the parent and reflow when the frame shrinks | Fixed-width cells that stay put while the frame changes size | Pixel padding alone is not a responsive layout | CLAIM(guYN5LaAcS8@03:23, guYN5LaAcS8@04:29, 2UbxHkiPwxg@20:19) |
| 18 | Columns that line up because each cell is its own frame set to fill, or one long field set to a fixed width | A row of hugging text blocks whose edges drift when the copy changes | Alignment across rows is a frame setting, not a nudge | CLAIM(2UbxHkiPwxg@13:45, 2UbxHkiPwxg@16:10) |
| 19 | Bottom margin about double the top, guides fit inside that margin box | All four margins equal, guides run to the page edge | Equal margins look default and the block feels like it falls off; page-fit guides make the first cell the wrong size | CLAIM(wIabVp0Hh8U@09:56, wIabVp0Hh8U@10:19, wIabVp0Hh8U@12:45) |
| 20 | Three image rows beside two, plus one image bled off the light side of the spread | A symmetrical three-and-three grid, or a photo across the fold with type in the gutter | Uneven counts add interest; a gutter keeps type readable; a bleed rebalances weight | CLAIM(wIabVp0Hh8U@22:00, wIabVp0Hh8U@22:16, wIabVp0Hh8U@20:45) |
| 21 | Picture left and copy right, then the next band flipped, with a different field | The same side and the same field on every band | A known pattern is fine; an unvaried stamp is not | CLAIM(rRFD6tENX2U@03:14, rRFD6tENX2U@03:42) |
| 22 | A face aimed at the headline | A face aimed off the offer | Gaze is a free pointer | CLAIM(rRFD6tENX2U@08:06, rRFD6tENX2U@08:15) |
| 23 | Mark pinned top-left, menu pinned top-right, cards stacked once the frame is phone-narrow | A desktop row squeezed until type overlaps, or a photo stretched as the frame shrinks | Constraints and a direction change beat a manual nudge | CLAIM(QjK56pv9Qe4@01:50, QjK56pv9Qe4@05:09, QjK56pv9Qe4@08:13) |
| 24 | A grid counted from the smallest card, then cells merged to fit the real cards | Finished cards dropped on a three-column frame that cannot hold a half-span | The small cell is what makes the span possible | CLAIM(k5AZoQC-OZk@10:25, k5AZoQC-OZk@12:03) |
