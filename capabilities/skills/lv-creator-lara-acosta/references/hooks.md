# Hooks and openings (Lara Acosta)

Her hook advice is the most volatile part of the doctrine: the length spec changed four times in two years while the structural rule stayed put. Load this file for openings, re-hooks and the dated ledger.

## What has not changed (2024 to 2026)

- Line one earns the read by naming an outcome or a specific problem, not by announcing who the author is. [6EYqnuncRrg, ObW3McgGG44, ibfdqyz4KhQ]
- Line two re-hooks: it complicates, challenges or adds proof to line one so the reader opens the body. [6EYqnuncRrg, ibfdqyz4KhQ, 6d_oxo1R9gs]
- Specificity beats cleverness. A concrete miss, a real number or a named buyer outperforms a professional abstraction. [qap4n06dqfw, b_sVHSaQqGA]
- The opening must say who the post is for; a scroll-stopper without that line earns the wrong audience. [ibfdqyz4KhQ]
- First two lines carry the positioning keywords, because she believes the platform reads them to classify the account. Creator claim, unverified. [b_sVHSaQqGA]
- Validate an opener against posts that already won, then repeat the winner until it stops working. A proven skeleton is a container for your own material, never text to reuse. [Slm6Ek465ow, qUmkSnXzW5A, 6d_oxo1R9gs]

## Testing and reuse

- She banks outlier ideas from other niches and platforms, transplants the proven hook and structure, then rewrites for her lane. [wYIzZqpHs3Q]
- She clusters past winners, feeds each to an AI writer as a template, and generates ideas from fresh milestones so the content is not a rerun. [6d_oxo1R9gs]
- Her framing on repetition: a format is rented, and reusing last quarter's winner without a new angle is exactly what stalls an account. [1ph8i-NDz4k, e3E83C-Xxn0]
- Hook testing in her workflow is human: a final edit pass always follows the machine draft, and she keeps only part of what a long-form model produces. [wYIzZqpHs3Q]

All counts in this file (eight words, ten words, two keywords) are creator claims about her own tests, not measured platform rules.
