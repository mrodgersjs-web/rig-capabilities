# Strategy and growth (Shanee Moret)

Positioning, category, profile, offers, lead generation, repurposing and measurement. Synthesis in RIG's words; numbers are creator claims; every claim carries a video ID.

## Position before anything

- One 90-day business goal first, then the buyer, then the assets. Examples of the goal shape she uses: 3 to 5 high-ticket clients in a quarter, filling a program, selling a course, or growing a list before a launch [6f3ZzY5-gUg, DitOkxbdSvo, bm6_uMvLagQ].
- Define the buyer from your top 20% of payers: what they bought first, why, how they found you, what their values are, and the trigger moment that made them call. Then write only to that moment [6f3ZzY5-gUg, bm6_uMvLagQ].
- How tightly to curate connections follows from five business factors: how your best clients actually arrived, how you want the next ones to arrive, who matches the offer you are moving toward, what sales cycle you can sustain, and how ready a buyer must be for fast monetization [ScjYcHsuPvM].
- Sell the first problem in the buyer's own words, not the full service menu; she reports buyers almost always arrive asking for the first-step problem [DdJYKsAfWqE].
- Beginner content attracts beginners and non-buyers; a market of one beats a message for everyone [0cQdGNRvatM, ScjYcHsuPvM, bm6_uMvLagQ].
- Strategic planning comes before profile optimization; the profile is styled for the named buyer afterwards [bm6_uMvLagQ, 6f3ZzY5-gUg].

## Category and mind share (2026 layer)

Her 2026 video reframes the whole playbook around one narrow category plus machine-readable proof: prospects will ask AI agents for experts, agents look for proof, and the profile's headshot, banner and about must all signal the same category [bCRYL6aL3ec].

- Mind share is her test: when someone sees you, the category names itself. Narrow from a broad field (marketing) to the smallest category your evidence supports (her own example ladder ends at live-video marketing) [bCRYL6aL3ec].
- One category, one ICP, one repeated CTA; about 8 of 10 posts on the category; the CTA matches the deal size (a six-figure engagement does not open with an ebook download) [bCRYL6aL3ec].
- She estimates most tech-savvy prospects run an agent within about 24 months (creator claim); treat as a hypothesis dated 2026 [bCRYL6aL3ec].

## Profile as landing page

- Order of blocks: photo, banner, headline, name clip or badge, buyer-facing about, featured proof, and the goal pinned. The about answers what, who, results and next step; it is not a biography [EejwRLkhHOc, toTLWp4aOtI].
- Goal first, profile second: by Feb 2025 she has the owner name the goal and the person before the about section, and a tool may draft the two about variants for you to edit [toTLWp4aOtI].
- Her 3-second profile test: can a stranger tell who you serve and what to do next [6f3ZzY5-gUg].
- Profile mechanics she cites: a short voice note, a verification badge that arrived within a day or two in 2024 and was demoted by Oct 2024, headline keywords feeding LinkedIn and search engines (unverified) [EejwRLkhHOc, zpi5MCB3Wzs].
- Pin the newsletter at the top of the activity section so visitors meet the article library first [JwnyrcJAdaQ].

## The three-channel machine

Feed for reach, newsletter for algorithm-independent contact, live events for booked revenue; the 2026 version is feed plus live video plus newsletters under one category and one CTA [ZohBpiAEvus, bCRYL6aL3ec, 6f3ZzY5-gUg].

- The 4,000-connection threshold: below a few thousand first-degree connections she points at feed content; past roughly 4,000 the invite engine works. She claims about 60 days of serious posting to reach 4,000, or 30 with one exceptional post (creator claims) [ZohBpiAEvus, YBYkNwplEmE].
- Supply-side argument she repeats: only about 1 to 3 percent of a billion members post original content weekly, 4 of 5 members are decision makers, and buying power is double other consumer platforms (all creator claims, unverified) [YntxonmZyyI, 6f3ZzY5-gUg, ZohBpiAEvus].
- Live events substitute for paid media: free invite distribution, a saved warm list of attend-clickers, a replay that becomes newsletter content, and chat that surfaces who is watching [Ec4xbXY8sjs, ZohBpiAEvus, JwnyrcJAdaQ].
- Her live funnel math (creator claim): about 4,000 invites, roughly 400 attend-clicks saved as warm contacts, about 100 live viewers, at least 5 calls, at least 2 clients; she reports live converting 10 to 30 percent against 1 to 3 percent for uploaded video [Ec4xbXY8sjs, bCRYL6aL3ec].
- Event fill: spend 7 to 10 days publishing only the event topic, DM the people who engaged with it, state the disqualifiers openly, run most lives solo, and set one success metric per event (even a single inbound message counts) [ebVmLIPyri8, g6zmV-Y90rs].
- Webinars off-platform are a separate machine: a LinkedIn event never routes into a Zoom registration; the landing page collects the signup and a reminder sequence converts registrants to attendees. She reports 60 to 65 weekly registrations for one midweek webinar and show rates around a third (creator claims) [1E75Q3jF7w4, Cv7QGjxA974].

## Lists, offers and revenue motion

- Rent the platform, own the list: the badge left, the inbox is still LinkedIn's, and her own private list started two years late. The LinkedIn newsletter is front-facing reach and trust; the exportable list (Substack or similar) is the owned backend she calls financial security [zpi5MCB3Wzs, X8Mz2tpvN-8, 93Wgv8WAHw4].
- Launch yields she reports: 10 to 20 percent of followers subscribing within 48 to 72 hours (up to 50 percent when very visible), open rates of 40 to 50 percent, and 5,000 followers pulling 500-plus subscribers in a day (creator claims) [wWrbyr6_9-s, BDW169gFDy0, 93Wgv8WAHw4, 1kqCOtDeLRA].
- The subscriber list is a prospect and referral map: she reports contracts landing within 4 editions and silent readers of years converting [BDW169gFDy0, pSncKW0JBFs].
- Offer discipline: say the offer out loud in standalone posts, keep the offer competitive because AI now gives away what was gated (dated 2025), practice the pitch until the discomfort is gone, and audit what competitors deliver before, during and after the consultation [DitOkxbdSvo].
- High-ticket buyers want a call; the CTA is a consultation with stated contents, and no landing page is required when a DM or a booking link works [bm6_uMvLagQ, 6f3ZzY5-gUg].
- Referral partnerships with respected peers when your motion is referral-led; direct content-led conversations when it is not [ScjYcHsuPvM, ebVmLIPyri8].
- Her origin method (2018 cold DMs at volume) is explicitly disavowed on 2024-02-04; her current motion is inbound positioning with trust earned at scale [i05h7QYsQIc, WCWDDPFnoKU].

## Repurposing and growth loops

- The chain: one video transcript feeds newsletter editions (she says up to 6), the best newsletters become live outlines (top 4 to 8 by what closed leads), lives recut into YouTube long-form and shorts, and the replay becomes a newsletter edition [62b9R_HMLAY, rI4ZkkauCcU, RSyNiEh-EPU, JwnyrcJAdaQ].
- YouTube as an inbound surface: she reports about half of inbound arriving via YouTube after she repurposed lives (creator claim), having dismissed the channel for years first; give it about 90 days before judging, and search intent sits closer to buying than feed attention [6iTHB7Ox1yo, rI4ZkkauCcU].
- Momentum: a spike gets followed by more output, not less; layer text, images, short and long video once clients stabilize [i05h7QYsQIc].

## Measurement

- One metric per stage: views for brand goals, calls or emails for revenue goals. Her own stated metric is emails received [sqv-zZLDGAA, ajCBOx3C-oI].
- 4 lives in a row with no consults or emails is a messaging problem in her words, every time; 12 weeks of newsletters with zero conversions is the same problem, not a channel failure [qSt2LtOCNy8, rI4ZkkauCcU, JwnyrcJAdaQ].
- Month-one analytics decide month-two content: mine the top outlier posts, pre-write them as the first newsletter editions, and double down on what moved the goal [5Y_tekIdDmI, ajCBOx3C-oI].
- Judge on emails, replies and calls booked; a prospect with huge impressions and zero clients is her standing counterexample [Ave2bYi7XRg, 6f3ZzY5-gUg].
- Her worked client stories (2,000 to 100,000 followers in months, $24K a month after 30 days, 900-plus likes on a first AI-image post) are creator claims and stay labeled as such [toTLWp4aOtI, qPHqqOfZSEo, bm6_uMvLagQ].
