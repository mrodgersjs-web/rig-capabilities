# Classifier sync: the machine's half of the profile

If it is not clear who the content is for, the feed serves the wrong people and culls reach; get it right and the platform runs the prospecting process on Mike's behalf [classifier-sync, creator claim]. The profile is one of three inputs to one classification.

## Three inputs, one classification

Content, messaging and profile must sync under the same messaging framework. A profile that says governance while the posts say productivity and the banner says growth gives the classifier three hypotheses and therefore none [classifier-sync].

Why it matters more now: the feed is no longer primarily a relationship graph. Only about a third of the timeline comes from connections; the rest is inferred from topic interest, which is also how a small, clearly-classified account out-converts a large vague one (500 clear followers can beat 500,000 unfocused ones) [classifier-sync, creator claim; cf. distribution-and-algorithm].

## The 60-second audit, made executable

1. Look at who viewed the profile. If titles and industries look like buyers, the classifier is working. If they are random, the wrong content made the account hard to classify; that is a content and messaging fault, not an audience fault [classifier-sync].
2. Cheaper reads when the main region is degraded: the right-rail "People you may know, from your industry" cluster is the platform's own classification of the account and survives a degraded main surface [classifier-sync].
3. Record the verdict as a ratio with a denominator: "7 of the last 20 visitor titles sit in the ICP" is a measurement; "visitors look roughly right" is a vibe [classifier-sync].

## Re-check cadence

A few weeks, maybe a month [classifier-sync, creator claim]. The classifier needs history to re-read. A weekly check shows noise and invites a change that resets the signal again. Fix the inputs, then wait. Headline churn resets what the classifier is learning, so one change set per month unless the audit says otherwise [step-8-profile].

## The failure this prevents

Reach falls, nobody notices for three weeks, the response is to post more, and the extra posts confirm the wrong hypothesis. Check classification before checking volume, and check who engaged, not how many [classifier-sync].

## Ordering rule

Classifier sync runs before any copy edit in this stage: decide who the profile must be about first, then write [lg-profile]. The change list carries the classifier verdict so the reviewer sees why the copy points where it does [step-8-profile].
