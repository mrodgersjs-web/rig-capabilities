# Cadence, timing and algorithm claims (Michelle J Raymond)

Two dated reads anchor this file, both creator claims and both unverified: the June 2025 feed-model shift from reach to relevancy, and her July 2025 conclusion that the first hour after posting no longer decides anything [Q6jtTfPADgw, VUkmBUjOy_c, FQ5RAfOuAIg]. Everything mechanical below is her reading of the platform, often second-hand from a report or blog she cites [Q6jtTfPADgw, 8Yrgj-zOYQ8, Nj8yZyzXi1E].

## Dated algorithm claims (record, do not merge)

- **2025-06: reach to relevancy.** The June change ranks posts on relevance to the reader rather than recency; her label is reach versus relevancy [VUkmBUjOy_c, Q6jtTfPADgw]. Old posts surfacing two to three weeks later was still visible to her in late July and she reads it as design, not a bug [Q6jtTfPADgw, FQ5RAfOuAIg].
- **2025-07: the first hour is retired.** Her July 2025 episode says the first hour, maybe the first few hours, no longer matter when posts surface weeks later. This is the only episode in the 52 that addresses the golden window, and it retires it [FQ5RAfOuAIg]. She never teaches a golden-hour routine anywhere in this corpus, and no later episode restores one (the nearest thing is a 2025-11 instruction to step away after posting so you do not delete the post, which is psychology, not an engagement window) [ZbylvpCOCzA].
- **2025-10: live, personalized ranking.** After June the ranking adapts to each action and differs per user; passive scrolling starves it while profile, posts and comments train it. She contrasts a pre-June static map with a post-June live GPS [Q8rO8FCy72w].
- **2025-11 to 2025-12: comments outrank posts.** She reports trusted sources saying comments can now out-impress posts, and reads per-comment impression analytics as the platform valuing comment work (creator claim) [lYqfrZZA64o, kXBXV1bQKes].
- **2026-03: topics over connections.** Distribution moves toward what you are known for rather than who you know; her cited engineering blog says the feed can re-topic within minutes [8Yrgj-zOYQ8].
- **2026-02 to 2026-06: the model layer, and a name retired.** In February she describes 360Brew as one model reading all behavior together, impossible to game [Nj8yZyzXi1E]. In June she retires the "360 Brew" name (she says it was a test label, not the internal name), reports generative recommenders as one signal family, and repeats that they update within minutes [OCpDoffJO_I].
- **Her own numbers as reference points (creator claims):** a years-long baseline around 2,500 impressions per post fell to about 1,000 to 1,200 after June 2025, with a helpful post still reaching near 6,000; her mid-2025 drop was 30 to 50 percent like everyone else, and her best clients kept arriving anyway [FQ5RAfOuAIg, Nj8yZyzXi1E, 1pjFtyAEVcc].

## Cadence (no post-publish window)

- **Two or three posts a week for small and mid-size businesses**, calendared, with old posts repurposed from about six months back; solopreneurs are exempted from that rule [vxmq9UDBs6s, K0D6kDr-WAY].
- **A ramp ladder:** one post weekly for four weeks, then two for four weeks, then three to four, with commenting and DMs alongside; a post costs her about an hour (15 minutes for experienced creators) (creator claims) [kXBXV1bQKes].
- **Volume is not the recovery move.** Posting more is the wrong reflex when reach drops; steady clear posts beat bursts, and a large page she studied cut from six-plus posts a day to two [8Yrgj-zOYQ8, mDsy5N6jgHs, p_jnafHMun4].
- **Poll cadence:** weekly or every second week, each running a full week [VUkmBUjOy_c, k3dCMwu8Nmk, nC06xaoVWPw].
- **Newsletter:** monthly for most, weekly is hers; first edition reaches every connection and follower, the one time the platform does that (creator claim) [VUkmBUjOy_c, SjhzoOD6Vmk, kyFnmN5Gu78].
- **Live:** not weekly for most; schedule the event weeks ahead and cut clips from it [VUkmBUjOy_c].
- **Daily floor:** 15 calendared minutes covering post, comment and message, forever [Ldms-a_C04Y].

- **Premium, page:** worth paying for a staffed brand in her 2026 read (she believes it favors SMB); page invites remain the cheaper lever [lYqfrZZA64o, n_jsi6uAALE, 0nz_nzgaJrY].
