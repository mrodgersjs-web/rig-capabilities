# Hooks and openings (Shanee Moret)

Her opening doctrine is idea selection over production: the hook wins by saying the thing the established voices on the topic leave out, then moving fast. Synthesis in RIG's words; numbers are creator claims; every claim carries a video ID.

## The baseline method (her core hook machine)

Before writing an opening, baseline the topic: spend about 30 minutes watching 2 to 3 top videos per platform on it and note what they all say and, more importantly, what none of them say. The hook is the off-baseline truth, told as your own belief rather than as a rebuttal [v3TS92mls0k, 62b9R_HMLAY].

- Slot skeleton: first step in [topic] has nothing to do with [topic]. Name the checklist the viewer expects first, then put the real first step ahead of it [v3TS92mls0k, 62b9R_HMLAY].
- Slot skeleton: [counter-intuitive claim], and here is why [v3TS92mls0k].
- Slot skeleton: what I learned as [experience] who has [result count]. The credibility line must land in about 4 seconds or be cut entirely [v3TS92mls0k].
- For a live or a talk, promise three points and make the first one break the baseline expectation [rI4ZkkauCcU].
- For a trend or news video, open on a stance about the bigger shift; do not open by announcing a list of updates [cY6L5HMglEQ].
- Never argue against a belief the buyer already holds; write to the belief and show a better route [v3TS92mls0k, ScjYcHsuPvM].

## Claim first, no greeting

Short video opens on the specific or contrarian claim, so curiosity drives the profile click. No intro, no greeting, no filler before the main point; cut the first seconds of hesitation on edit [YggvR0eUj6E, EJ0QFdEXjqA, WbWSMLOeGW0].

- Slot skeleton: [specific or contrarian claim] + [how] + [logical next step] [YggvR0eUj6E].
- Slot skeleton: [experience] + [lesson] + [what they should do] [xwCLAdZTJgE].
- Curiosity tease: state the action and withhold the result [EJ0QFdEXjqA].
- Honest opener for an early live: name your inexperience out loud and ask the audience to teach you [C_oq55RbYaU].
- Because autoplay is silent, put the topic on screen in the first frame [toTLWp4aOtI].
- Post text carries the opening too: the first three lines are all most readers see, so the substance goes there [EJ0QFdEXjqA].

## Newsletter titles are hooks

The launch title is both hook and permanent filter: it decides who subscribes, it becomes the email subject line, and renames only affect future subscribers. Specific beats cute; name who it is for and what they get, in the field's short limit (she instructs around 30 characters) [pSncKW0JBFs, vNNVBp_mM0g, bm6_uMvLagQ, 93Wgv8WAHw4].

- Slot skeleton: [result or topic] for [person] [gCL9819h1OA].
- The launch article's own structure is a hook: why now, what it is, what to expect, then a full-sentence call to action [1kqCOtDeLRA, wWrbyr6_9-s].
- Titles that recall one friend of yours are the test she applies to a topic [RDEugFeexyI].
- Live chat prompts are hooks too: give a keyword and a belief the room already holds, never an open-ended question [WbWSMLOeGW0, Ec4xbXY8sjs].

## What she does not teach

She runs no A/B hook testing regime and no three-variant drafting routine; her testing is the 30-minute baseline read, the comment sections of what already worked (top trend entries and their comments are demand research), and the post-hoc check of which pieces produced emails and calls [v3TS92mls0k, cY6L5HMglEQ, sqv-zZLDGAA]. Do not attribute a testing framework to her that she does not run.

## Dated shifts

- March and May 2024 teach daily video volume with client-question openings [ajCBOx3C-oI, t5xc4-NE_OE]; by Oct 2024 the length tightens to roughly 45 seconds to 2 minutes and a short that travels carries one new action for one buyer against one 90-day goal [0cQdGNRvatM].
- By 2025-05-06 quality is the entry fee and loose posting earns nothing, so an off-baseline claim is not optional garnish but the entry requirement [bm6_uMvLagQ].
- 2026 reframes the opening around category ownership and proof signals an agent can read: the hook names the category you want recalled [bCRYL6aL3ec].
