## The doctrine in 10 lines (current as of Sept 2026)

1. **Fix the system before posting more.** A stale profile or a confused page costs distribution and search, so hold the next post until the positioning is matched across headline, banner, about and proof. [mDsy5N6jgHs, K0D6kDr-WAY, m8FkFDZJi-M]
2. **One audience, one problem, one next step.** Write to the buyer's day and the buyer's words, not the internal ask, and end on a single next move. [xR6t2llZRKQ, udtNia_2Onk, wZLL1QHLbvc]
3. **The page is the anchor, people carry the clicks.** Organic page reach is nearly gone, so the page educates and comments outward while employees and leaders carry distribution. [LKUcEKAMmLo, lYqfrZZA64o, _yf76tCe-zY, p_jnafHMun4]
4. **Commenting is the discovery engine.** Fifteen to thirty minutes a day on buyer-matched posts, or five comments across five days if you already post, with no quota and no pods. [LG_sBQB2xAc, kXBXV1bQKes, THXjj1yM6Kw]
5. **Relevance beat reach from June 2025.** The ranking is live and personal, trained by your profile, posts and comment choices, and old posts surfacing weeks later is the design, not a bug. [Q6jtTfPADgw, VUkmBUjOy_c, Q8rO8FCy72w, OCpDoffJO_I]
6. **She never teaches a golden-hour routine.** Her one episode on the first hour retires it (July 2025), and nothing later restores it; post-publish timing is a habit and a calendar, not a window. [FQ5RAfOuAIg, ZbylvpCOCzA, Ldms-a_C04Y]
7. **Say what you sell after the conversation.** Sales posts are required, volume without an offer creates no leads, and repeating the offer is not beneath a grown following. [2JI55PLI9Zw, dNDNxyoD4qE, xR6t2llZRKQ]
8. **Newsletters and articles are the citable surface.** Grow on LinkedIn, own the list on email, and do not launch a newsletter just to chase AI citations. [vMfQ3tewli8, TtJFDeUqUNM, SjhzoOD6Vmk]
9. **Faces over stock, opinion over polish, no format is magic.** Employee faces move page metrics fastest, and short video earns a slot only when it serves the buyer, never as the year's bet. [K0D6kDr-WAY, Dd42KDThdPY, zSfu7p5LjXM, lYqfrZZA64o]
10. **Alignment and mindset gate everything.** Name the fear, then get sales, marketing, page and advocacy on one plan before adding volume; the internal fight outranks the algorithm. [_7hbfgS9lTw, c7GPLlwC0UM, MKsOihMy68M]

## Binding overlays for Mike (RIG rules win on conflict)

- **Lane first.** Enter through `linkedin-viral` for the lane guard. Her page and advocacy doctrine maps most directly to Lane B (RIG B2B); Lane A keeps its own rhythm and cast rules; the Ideawake client lane runs on Coby's account and never inherits her or Mike's voice; the job-search lane has its own profile rules. Anything about a minor stops. Her tactics never travel to the minor's lane.
- **Eden is the publisher of record and the assigned generator.** Feed Eden a real story, transcript or receipt, never a bare topic. Agents source facts, rank options, gate and measure.
- **Gate-D on the outward steps she recommends.** Publishing or scheduling a live post, DMs and connection requests in volume, paid boosts, premium purchases, and profile or page-admin changes each need Mike's explicit yes for that item.
- **Conflicts with her advice, named.** She teaches proactive outbound: messaging poll voters by how they voted, messaging second and third degree newsletter subscribers, and five to ten DMs a day [k3dCMwu8Nmk, TtJFDeUqUNM, kXBXV1bQKes]. On Mike's account nothing is sent automatically without his yes, and all outreach stays inside `linkedin-site-mechanics` throttles. Her monthly page-invite credit push includes temporarily granting a colleague admin rights to spend credits [n_jsi6uAALE]; that is an account-permission change and a Gate-D item. Premium seats, boosts and thought-leader ads are spend [FQ5RAfOuAIg, OCpDoffJO_I]. Against Tommy Clark's 2026 rules: he boosts under-delivered posts at about 30 dollars a day after a week of organic run, while she calls boost buttons donations and would rather pay for targeted thought-leader ads (2026-06-17) [OCpDoffJO_I, ZbylvpCOCzA]; where they conflict, believe the dated claim and Mike's own analytics.
- **Comments stay positive.** Mike's rule for other people's posts: validate the point, add one real insight, optionally link his own related post, never AI-written. Her contrarian and opinion dial applies to his own posts only.
- **Every number is a sourced receipt.** Her skeletons are slots to fill with Mike's verifiable facts, never with her figures.
