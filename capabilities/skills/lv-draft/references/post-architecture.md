# Post architecture: the arc, the structures, the line mechanics

## The belief-shift arc (default for a thesis post)

A LinkedIn post is a belief-change artifact: it fails if the reader ends at the belief they started
with. [/rig-ae-linkedin-run]

| Beat | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (inciting incident) | 1-3 lines | interrupt the scroll; make the ideal reader feel "that is exactly my situation". Generic openers ("here is what I learned") are anti-patterns |
| Rupture (the valley) | 2-4 lines | name the strongest skeptic objection by content and confront it here, not buried at the end. No valley means rewrite |
| Reframe (new lens) | 2-4 lines | the frame that makes the new belief make sense, not just its opposite |
| Proof (rising) | 3-6 lines | evidence ordered weakest to strongest; the last element is the most specific and most surprising; every element traceable |
| CTA | 1-2 lines | one real next step tied to belief_to. No "what do you think", no "follow me for more" |

Anti-fake checks: a rupture that only restates the hook (deletable with no loss) is a fake valley; three candidates that are the same post with swapped synonyms are one post; an asserted quality score with no test applied is not a gate. [/rig-ae-linkedin-run]

## The four named body structures (corpus)

1. **Problem, agitate, solution** (top of funnel): name the Monday-morning pain specifically, agitate
   the felt disparity, then give numbered fixes. Failure mode: agitation nobody feels. Test: would the
   target wince? [creator: Will McTighe, TTH-75XOgJE]
2. **Story, lesson, application** (middle, trust): a real story, the takeaway, then how to apply it.
   Application is optional; story and lesson never are. [creator: McTighe]
3. **Case study in six parts** (bottom, conversion): transformation shown in text and visual, before,
   after with receiptable numbers, what changed, an authentic-urgency CTA, and visual proof.
   Without screenshots of real messages or metrics it is a claim, not a case study. [creator: McTighe]
4. **Before, after, bridge** (journey posts): state the transformation, then how. Use when the journey
   itself is the point. [creator: Pierre Herubel, i-YDUfx84f8]
5. **Hand-raiser / opt-in:** pain-focused contrarian take, "game changed and it is not your fault",
   the new way, a curiosity loop, one visual model, a proof line, an invitation, then the opt-in. Note
   the house tension: comment-word delivery brushes the engagement-bait kill list, so prefer a
   saveable first-comment asset until that contradiction is resolved. [creator: Taki Moore,
   Eq0nlMdmaFc]

## Line mechanics (apply inside every structure)

- One idea per post. One point per line. Real whitespace so the post occupies vertical space and is
  read slower.
- People skim structure first and read second: deliver in skimmable layers. [creator: Herubel]
- Simple sentences, no complex words. The body delivers the hook's promise. [creator: Herubel]
- One consistent through-line; cut tangents and fluff. [creator: McTighe]
- End on a question or a moment of disagreement to seed threads.
- Give four, take one. No CTA in every post. [creator: Harry Long, 27IqjaAYSa0]
- Title the image: a titled visual is a second hook. [creator: Herubel]

## Retention mechanics (inside any structure)

- Therefore/but rule: consecutive story beats must connect with "therefore" or "but". Beats connected
  by "and then" mean the story is broken. [creator: Kallaway, t5Z-Q1bg1tU; language-flagged caption]
- The dance: alternate context and conflict; each resolved conflict meets the next one; open loops
  hold the reader. [creator: Kallaway, t5Z-Q1bg1tU]
- Dopamine is a prediction chemical: stakes, a sustained big question, then the head fake. The named
  fourth step was not captured in the doctrine file: re-read the source video before citing beyond
  step three. [creator: Kallaway, KyC8r-zitVE]
- Boring-topic rescue: make an existing thing feel new by revealing what changed about it; relevance
  comes from calling out the viewer. [creator: Kallaway, LvuoNlYRs7g]
- Re-hook inside the body ("then this happened") reopens the gap the hook opened; the body pays it and
  the CTA routes. [creator: Lucy (Career Growth With Lucy), WH_2PNwkT3E]
- Sell while teach: every moment teaches and sells at once, so the closing ask is an invitation
  rather than a pitch. [creator: Taki Moore, iB6Zq2o7JrY]

## First comment (designed at draft time)

The first comment is a director's call: it names the one conversation the post wants and asks for it.
No external link in the body; the comment-link workaround is penalised too, so the reference comment
carries a saveable artifact (checklist, playbook, framework) instead. [/rig-ae-linkedin-run, body-craft]
