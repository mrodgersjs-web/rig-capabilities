# Match employer initiatives to truthful role-fit evidence

## Enter when
Dated employer research and confirmed employment history are available.

## Route
Match relevant initiatives to requirements. Treat date/employer overlap only as a reason to ask. Use contribution questions to establish participation before any personal attribution.

Load these concrete steps or subskills when their named work starts. Preserve the inherited eight-leaf order; a router does not skip unmet upstream gates or treat configuration as execution.

1. [employer-initiative-research](../../steps/employer-initiative-research/SKILL.md).
2. [company-due-diligence](../../steps/company-due-diligence/SKILL.md).
3. [contribution-questions](../../subskills/contribution-questions/SKILL.md).

## Exit evidence
Source-bound initiative matches and separate unanswered contribution questions.

## Failure and recovery
Stop the affected transition on missing evidence, malformed receipts or unavailable required adapters. Save the exact job/revision/checkpoint and unresolved hold. Resume the same operation after reconciliation. No local or JEV PASS grants submission, messaging, schedule activation or credential-change authority.
