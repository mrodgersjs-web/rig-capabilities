# Hooks and media

Sub-skill of `tommy-clark-doctrine`. Synthesis; bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims.

## 1. Why the hook gets the effort

The first one to three lines (roughly 100-300 characters before "see more") decide whether the rest is read. He treats the opening like a YouTube title and thumbnail and argues a modestly better hook can multiply the result several times over. [P_5mJNksHiE, cWfOlNGZ6eg, pk_SUAVD3mA, sDuWUBoHsRI]

## 2. The levers (stack 2-3, four at most)

| Lever | Why it works | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Specific numbers | precision reads as truth; money moves B2B readers | unrounded beats rounded; he argues a precise figure out-pulls a round one [-WGCvECeTcU, h3751DcSeQw] |
| Narrative | a story promise: stakes, a turn, "how I" | the anchor lever for 2025-26 [nbXzXfthKl0, 0EA0n1Ka41k] |
| Negativity bias | mistakes, warnings, what is dying | warning, not rage bait [cWfOlNGZ6eg, P_5mJNksHiE] |
| Social proof | lead with the result or record that earns attention | he lists proof first [P_5mJNksHiE] |
| Borrowed credibility | a well-known company or person in the opening, repaid with your own insight; tag them when fair | he calls the evergreen version parasite marketing [_YPmpy4fVGw, cWfOlNGZ6eg] |
| Trend | a live news story in line one, your read after the fold | [nXY6Tl6-uh0, 2wE-6op6BeM] |
| Packaging | counts, steps, playbooks, timelines | save-signal words: checklist, blueprint, playbook, framework [cWfOlNGZ6eg, z7YQx8Qj2f0] |
| Open loop | finish the opening on a colon, an ellipsis, or an unresolved turn | [P_5mJNksHiE, UjDZLhuEpnQ] |

Lever count drifted from 2-5 (2024) to 2-4 (2025) to 2-3 (2026). [P_5mJNksHiE, 2wE-6op6BeM, UjDZLhuEpnQ, s6KMjhznXqs]

## 3. Hook process

1. Draft three versions of every hook and merge the best parts. His team treats this as non-negotiable. [TH63A9m1VNA, z7YQx8Qj2f0, 2XeAckxuAKM]
2. Write for a stranger. Assume the reader has never heard of you; callbacks and inside references belong in the body. [2Yv8osegsKY, Y8ewcq03zxc]
3. Pay off exactly what the opening promises. [E5vxr1IZCpI]
4. Keep the levers invisible under a conversational voice. Recognizable formula openings now read as AI output. [SL8yfxUVFKE, gZwWBtiZ7qE]
5. Since May 2025 he prefers a fuller opening that uses all three visible lines over choppy one-liners. [sDuWUBoHsRI]
6. For milestone posts, lead with the strongest metric class: revenue, then growth rate, then customers, then headcount. [CkoSKSZ6WIU]
7. A clearly lagging fresh post can get a rewritten opening (the image cannot be swapped after upload), but by 2026 he says not to delete slow posts in the first hours because delayed spikes are normal. [Fvl_wMtcz24, s6KMjhznXqs]

## 4. Hook skeletons (RIG-written; fill every slot with a sourced receipt)

- Result plus constraint plus loop: "[specific result] in [time], with [surprising constraint]. What we changed:"
- Boundary: "I won't [common practice] unless [condition]. Here's the reason."
- Buyer conversation: "Last week a [role] at a [company size] told me [problem]. [turn]"
- Mistake count: "[number] mistakes [buyer] make with [topic]. The [ordinal] one cost [client type] [amount]."
- How-I reframe: "How I [outcome], not how you should."
- Borrowed name: "[Known company] just [event]. What it changes for [buyer]:"
- Hypothetical: "If I ran [function] at a [stage] company and needed [outcome] by [date], my order of moves:"
- Data: "We reviewed [scope]. [Metric] moved [precise figure], and [surprise]."
- Origin: "[Time] ago I [left / almost lost] [thing]. [Stakes]. How we got here:"

Never publish a skeleton with a placeholder number or an invented client. Rank variants through Jev before drafting the body.

## 5. Media is the second hook

- Ask of every post whether a photo, graphic or video can carry it. [UjDZLhuEpnQ, s6KMjhznXqs]
- Real photos of real people and places win: the office used as a set with someone owning photo and B-roll capture, early-days photos, event shots, phone-note screenshots. He cites about 27% more impressions for IRL photos over a month of client posts; branded graphics read as ads. [szN0DF3Oqik, N2b0QVoCEPg, 8VqGXuFxd3Q, O8HeAkNg6L8]
- The image must match the copy. [uYVwSbaztJ4]
- Formats cycle, so do not lock in: short video was boosted in late 2024 and hit-or-miss by late 2025 (about once a week for trust); carousels faded in 2024, returned in 2025 with two-to-three-week tails, and were "no longer a cheat code" by 2026; text-only came back in mid 2025. [bjPrW4O4hww, E0uu_GLIkw0, 29hBCF7N7Jg, cWfOlNGZ6eg, yBKOrgQS-q8, 8VqGXuFxd3Q]
- Funding and milestone graphics interrupt the scroll. [O8HeAkNg6L8, Xt179qkyqQU]
- Featured-link images behave like YouTube thumbnails; bake the value and proof into the image. [RVFC8i05e_k, 8VqGXuFxd3Q]
