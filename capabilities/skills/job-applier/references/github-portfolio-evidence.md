## Separate the three claims

1. **Repository capability:** what the inspected code or artifact supports at the cited revision.
2. **Personal contribution:** what Mike actually designed, built, led or changed. Use corroborated history or his explicit confirmation; account ownership alone does not answer this.
3. **Business outcome:** actual deployment, adoption, savings, revenue, reliability or performance. Require attributable evidence with units, period and scope. Proposed benefits, benchmark targets and README assertions stay unconfirmed.

Use capability evidence to improve the role-specific summary, capabilities and experience framing without inventing ownership or outcomes. Ask Mike only the remaining contribution, employer/period, attribution or result questions that the source audit cannot resolve. Keep any proposed experience claim visibly red and UNCONFIRMED until factual review resolves it. Do not demand a full questionnaire before the useful draft.
