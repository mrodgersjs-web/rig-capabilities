# Calendar and cadence: the week, the mix, the numbers

Sub-skill of lv-plan. The output is a dated slate on the board Mike already uses, each slot carrying
a job, a format, a funnel stage and an evidence label. Nothing here publishes.

## Weekly procedure

1. Read last week's actuals first (measurement skill). Never plan against a feeling.
2. State the week's target as a range with its arithmetic basis. If the request is unreachable, say
   so and name the reachable band; never quietly widen it.
3. Slot 3-5 posts. Never two in one day: the second post of a day is cannibalised.
4. Give each slot one job: `classification` (narrow ICP relevance), `save-asset` (framework or
   checklist a buyer forwards), `tail-ticket` (highest-variance contrarian take), `register-break`
   (day-in-the-life), or `second-wave` (a re-cut of the week's best first hour).
5. Force difference across the week: different format, different material, different energy. Three
   posts of the same shape trigger content fatigue.
6. Enforce give 4 : take 1 on calls to action. At most one ask-bearing post in five.
7. Assign formats from the format rules below. Default timing: Tue-Thu business hours in the ICP's
   timezone; weekday 9-11am local is the neutral default when nothing better is known.
8. Write the week to the board Mike already uses. No parallel plan file.

## Format rules

| format | evidence | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| document or carousel | highest average engagement among post types; most activity is mobile | save-asset slots; mobile-legible 1080x1350 |
| text plus a real photo of Mike | unanimous across the corpus | the default; never stock, never an AI infographic |
| video | top-tier claim only; mechanics not covered by our corpus | do not invent a video strategy; about once a week for trust, photos win |
| poll | not covered at all | unproven, not banned |

Contested, carried not resolved: one creator claims visual formats pull multiples of reach, then
elsewhere says there should always be a photo of you. Both can hold on different axes: carousels win
saves, a face wins trust. Carry the contradiction, drop the multiplier.

Length bands rotate: under 500 characters, 500-1500, 1500-3000 (the platform limit).

## Cadence rules (the 2026 bar and the lane exception)

- **Default bar: three better posts a week.** The quality bar rose; volume alone no longer buys reach.
  Per-account ceiling is one or two posts a day and more volume means more accounts, not more posts.
- **Lane exception:** a lane keeps its existing rhythm only while it beats the account's own
  baseline. The weekly review decides, not the habit. Conflict log: daily posting (Taki), a 7-day
  Lane A themed rhythm, and 4-5/week Lane B all predate or diverge from the three-post bar; the bar
  is the test, the rhythm is provisional.
- Volume still buys data: one post a week is about 4 data points a month, five a week about 20.
- Plan about a week ahead. Month-deep queues go stale.
- Erratic cadence underperforms steady cadence; 3-3-3-3-3 beats 2-then-5.
- One planned peak a month: a launch, an event, a stunt or a big data post.

## Funnel mix

- Top (reach), middle (authority for the buyer, unpitched), bottom (proof). The 2026 split is
  20 / 60 / 20; at three posts that is one per stage.
- Alternative barbell slate: one moonshot (heavy research or original data), one scrappy fast post
  (a rant, a screenshot), one experiment. Avoid the polished middle that machine output imitates.
- Bottom-of-funnel posts read as milestones and team pride, never as pitch.
- Easy wins protect cadence when a deep post will not ship: a milestone, a hire, a small result.

## Monthly and quarterly

- One planned peak per month, mapped to a real moment in the lane.
- Bank winners; rerun a proven post after about three months, and the origin story quarterly using a
  different angle each time.
- Weekly review (about 30 minutes): outlier review of the last 5-6 posts against baseline, rank
  formats, set next week's slate, list boost candidates for Mike with a dollar amount (Gate-D). Each
  metric maps to one action: rerun, re-cut the hook, change the media, retire the format, or boost.

## Production support (how the slate gets filled)

- A biweekly content interview (30-45 minutes) or Mike's voice notes produce the transcript; the
  transcript goes to Eden as source material; Eden drafts; Mike edits for voice. This satisfies both
  the no-blank-machine-draft rule and the Eden-as-generator rule.
- Friday calendar mining (20-30 minutes) reviews the week's meetings for moments with emotion and a
  lesson. Idea capture (pinned notes, screenshots, a swipe file, a winners playbook) means no draft
  starts blank.
- One approver. Committees make mush; delegating is fine, abdicating is not.

## Checks before a week ships

- Slots: 3-5, never two in one day, one job each, evidence label each, at most one experiment.
- Formats differ across the week; length bands rotate.
- Give-to-take is 4:1 or better.
- Target is a range with arithmetic; cadence choice names which rule it follows.
- The slate lives on the board Mike already uses and every outward step is marked for Mike's yes.
