# Ideal achievement bank

## Inputs
Current verified JD/persona and requirement IDs.

## Actions
Execute inherited ideal-achievement-bank. Produce distinct requirement-linked examples and explicit gaps. Mark all ideal achievements hypothetical until supported by candidate evidence.

## Outputs
Complete ideal example bank with hypothetical status and source bindings.

## Gates
No ideal metrics, credentials or outcomes enter clean candidate documents as facts.

## Recovery
Correct missing coverage and invalidate alignment/drafts derived from changed examples.
