# Draft workflow: intake, source material, candidates, gates, the Eden brief

## Intake (fill before any prose)

- belief_from: the specific belief the ideal reader holds now (not "they do not understand X" but a
  falsifiable position).
- belief_to: the specific, falsifiable belief after reading.
- ideal_reader: title, context, moment in their day.
- platform_moment: how they are consuming the feed (scrolling before standup, in a meeting break),
  which sets pacing and hook style.

Any field missing or vague is a stop. A post aimed at "people interested in marketing" has no target
and produces no belief change. [/rig-ae-linkedin-run]

## Source material (the Eden rule)

Eden generates the copy and brand assets. The agent's job is the brief, and a brief is built from
real material, never a bare topic:

- Content interview: every two weeks, 30-45 minutes, prompts mapped to funnel stages and worked
  backward from the posts you intend to ship. The transcript is raw stone; the writer carves; Mike
  edits for voice. [clark:content-system mNR3-MrM5wI]
- Calendar mining: 20-30 minutes reviewing the week's meetings for moments with emotion and a lesson.
  [HFCfw6gznRM]
- Idea capture: pinned notes, screenshots, a running doc, a swipe file and a winners playbook so no
  draft starts blank. [E0uu_GLIkw0]
- Receipts: named sources for every number, or the number is out. A creator-reported number is
  context, never content.

That satisfies both the no-blank-to-draft rule and the Eden-as-generator rule. [clark:content-system,
li-hooks]

## Proof table and the valley

Before writing: two to three specific, citable evidence items ordered weakest to strongest, each with
source and year. Name the strongest counter-argument to belief_to and confront it in the rupture:
that valley is what earns trust. Flag every unsourced factual claim; unsourced means blocked. A
figure that cannot survive one search is blocked as unsourced. [/rig-ae-linkedin-run]

## Candidates and scoring

For consequential posts, write three complete candidates. They must differ in hook style (scene,
number, counter-claim), proof ordering, and CTA specificity. Score each on four axes, honestly:
justified credibility, engagement probability, proof density, audience fit. State the winner, its
score, and keep the runners-up visible. Three near-identical candidates with clustered scores are one
post disguised as a comparison. [/rig-ae-linkedin-run]

## Gate scorecard (each gate is a true/false observable)

- Belief shift named: both positions present and falsifiable.
- Arc has the valley: the skeptic objection is confronted in-body by content, not by a transition
  phrase.
- Proof rises: weakest to strongest, strictly.
- All claims sourced: every factual claim names a source.
- First comment engineers the conversation: names the one conversation and asks for it.
- Anti-generic force: at least one detail only Mike could write; a brand find-and-replace must break
  the post.
- Not a zero-substance post: at least one claim that could be proven wrong.

Blocked means fix the gate first. Do not ship a winner without the candidates that justify it.
[/rig-ae-linkedin-run]

## The hand-off: brief to Eden, then gate

1. Mechanical filters pass: no link in body, 2-3 hashtags maximum, no engagement bait, no em dashes,
   one post per day, no CTA unless this week's one ask-bearing post.
2. Jev gate run (`post.linkedin` for Lane B, `post.viral`, `post.eden` for Eden copy under review)
   with a decision id recorded, or explicitly DEFERRED.
3. Hook validator prompts run (from lv-hooks) and any BLOCK fixed or killed.
4. Brief handed to Eden: objective, audience, lane, classification, receipts table, chosen format and
   architecture, hook line and rehook from lv-hooks, CTA and first comment, constraints.
5. Draft goes to critique next (lv-gate). A draft failing the bar returns here with the named failing
   criteria.

## Tools are called, not copied

The RIG write engine (`~/Developer/rig-capabilities/capabilities/skills/rig-write-linkedin/SKILL.md`)
assembles the write packet and gate-scores the artifact; the master comms engine
(`~/Developer/rig-capabilities/capabilities/skills/rig-communications-core/references/SKILL.md`) and
the writer agents in the tool table supply vertical expertise, feed-rhythm writing, craft passes and
deliberated treatments. None of them replaces the source-material rule: any of them still needs a
brief built from a real story, transcript or receipt.

## Conflict log (kept and dropped)

- Generic framework formatting (fixed word count and line budget, a closing repost ask on every post)
  versus the house rules (rotating length bands, no CTA in most posts): the house rules govern; the
  named-framework format is used only when Mike asks for it.
- Fixed self-promotion split versus the house give-four-take-one ratio: the house ratio governs until
  this account measures otherwise; the contradiction stays open.
- Comment-word opt-in delivery versus the engagement-bait kill list: saveable first-comment asset
  wins until resolved.
