# Great example - web-conversion-copy-proof

The proof stack for the Northwind Billing page, ordered by strength and placed against the
claim each element supports.

What makes it excellent:

1. The proof stack is ranked and the strongest available rung is used. Order on the page:
   a measured result with a number and a timeframe (three named customers, close time
   before and after), then two attributed testimonials with name, role, company and a
   face, then a single case study, then a grayscale row of six logos. No unattributed quote
   appears anywhere on the page.

2. Placement answers the doubt at the moment it forms. The first proof element sits one
   screen height below the hero claim it supports, not at the bottom of the page. Each of
   the three feature claims carries its own supporting number within one screen height.

3. Testimonials are cut for quality, not padded for count. Three are shown and each runs
   under thirty words while still containing a before-state, an after-state and a reason.
   Four more are held behind a "read all seven" link. Two that were just praise with no
   before-state were dropped rather than shortened into decoration.

4. The case study follows situation, decision, mechanism, result, next step. Its headline
   carries one number and a timeframe: "Four days to one: how Corley Foods cut close by
   78% in one quarter." The decision section names what they rejected and why, which is
   what makes the story credible rather than promotional.

5. Pricing and objections are structured for the buyer. Tiers are named for who buys
   (Solo operator, Finance team, Multi-entity), the middle tier is the one the seller wants
   to move and is marked "most chosen", each card states what is NOT included, and the full
   comparison table sits below the cards. The top three objections from sales calls are
   answered inline beside the CTA, each leading with the concession, and the risk reversal
   sits with the button rather than in the footer.

The result to beat: a sceptical reader reaches the CTA having already had their top three
objections answered in their own words, next to the thing they are being asked to click.
