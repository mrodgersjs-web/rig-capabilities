# Golden hour and the first 24 hours

Reach is decided mostly in the first 60-90 minutes, and each post is tested against roughly 2-5% of its network before any expansion. Treat the golden hour as a scheduled block, not an intention.

## Read the clock first

Establish post age before proposing anything. Past about two hours the expansion window is closed; the honest move is to deepen the existing thread and plan a second wave as a new post, not to pump the old one. Reach under about 5% of followers with single-digit comments means the distribution test already stopped expanding.

## The window and the slot

- Post when the ICP is awake: Denver business hours, Tue-Thu preferred, 7-9am or 12-1pm local to the audience. Launches run Monday to Thursday; evenings are weak.
- Do not add a second post the same day; the second post of a day is heavily cannibalised.
- The first 20 minutes of reception matter a lot, but by 2026 a post that starts slow can recover; there is no universal best time, the audience's own rhythm decides.

## Signal weights, strongest first

1. Saves, roughly twice a comment. The strongest measured single signal.
2. Threaded replies, up to about 2.4x the reach of a plain comment.
3. Multi-person back-and-forth threads, far more amplification than isolated pairs.
4. Comments from in-topic experts, several times the weight of generic engagement. Reply to those first.
5. Comments, roughly twice a like.
6. Dwell time, tracked separately and heavily weighted; long structured posts help.
7. Likes, weakest. Do not chase them.

## The four writes, in this order

1. Reply substantively to the highest-status in-topic comment first: validate their specific point, add one thing they did not say, end on a question that needs expertise to answer. The second reply from them is the point.
2. Reply to the remaining comments the same way. Never "thanks, great point".
3. Post the save asset on your own post (`references/save-assets.md`).
4. Only then consider any outreach, and read the outreach rules before doing it.
5. A second save asset, then the repost. A graded answer key is a distinct asset people save for a different reason and is the only write that raises the strongest signal again after publish. An "Add your thoughts" repost is the only action that creates a new distribution event on an already-published post: frame it around one commenter's story, credited by name, and point it at the save asset in its first comment rather than at a link. Reposting is public: explicit yes first.

## Presence rules

- Reply instantly and in depth, as himself. This is the highest-weight item in the runbook and it is human work. An agent can prepare and stage every reply; an agent cannot hold the hour.
- Log first-comment and last-reply timestamps. If replies were not within minutes, the block was not held; record that rather than reporting it as done.
- At +90 minutes, read the numbers once and stop. Hourly re-reading produces decisions on noise; a rising curve is not a straight line.
- Ask two or three real people for real comments only on big moments (a launch, a fundraise). Mobilizing the wider network for everything is what pods do, and pods are banned.

## The first 24 hours

- Do not delete a slow post early. Distribution is spiky and late: a post can look dead for 12 hours and then take off, and the feed now resurfaces posts one to four weeks old, so a live post must stay clean. [s6KMjhznXqs]
- The early window is not the whole story: the 2024-25 view that the first 30-60 minutes decide everything is superseded by "still matters, but posts recover". [s6KMjhznXqs]
- At 24 hours write down one to three learnings, in the scoreboard file the measurement routines already read, and bank the winner for a rerun in about three months (origin story quarterly). [Fvl_wMtcz24, 0EA0n1Ka41k, fHha-KIZeMg]
