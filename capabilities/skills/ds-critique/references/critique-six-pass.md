# Critique Six Pass

Purpose: the ordered edit (cut, read, color, space, unify, say), including the 4.5 contrast floor and the wide-screen container. Owns 4, 8, 14, 15, 20, 39-43, 82, 84, 94, 95.

Load when: the task touches this part of critique and quality gates, or a ds-critique critique names it. Part of `ds-critique`.
