# Layout Space And Grouping

Purpose: empty space, proximity, one focal point, edit-before-decorate. Owns 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 11, 12.

Load when: the task touches this part of layout and composition, or a ds-layout critique names it. Part of `ds-layout`.

## Checklist
1. Work through every law below against the current artifact before calling the sub-topic done.

## Anti-patterns for this sub-topic
- Filling negative space by scaling type or laying a texture across the empty half. CLAIM(EFf9jBs2yfU@00:43)
- Decorative type in paragraphs, and client copy pasted as a wall. CLAIM(1NTKwpAVcHg@04:22, TeMtV8BaZt0@00:58)
- Less than 6 px between an icon and its label. CLAIM(cf95Z7Ngg8k@02:08)
- Ornaments that outrank the content, and a form field that disappears in a squint. CLAIM(TeMtV8BaZt0@16:06, TeMtV8BaZt0@20:10)
