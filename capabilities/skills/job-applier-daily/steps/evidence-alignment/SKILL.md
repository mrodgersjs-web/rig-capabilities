# Evidence alignment

## Inputs
Persona/bank, confirmed career evidence, initiative research and accessible portfolio sources.

## Actions
Execute inherited evidence-alignment including its authenticated portfolio audit. Distinguish inspected code, personally performed work and commercial results. Map supported, adjacent and unknown claims. Carry participation/inclusion state without inference.

## Outputs
Claim ledger, portfolio audit, requirement map, supported source pointers and residual questions.

## Gates
Missing portfolio access remains an explicit partial-coverage hold under inherited rules. Company results and ideal examples cannot supply personal ownership.

## Recovery
Preserve source SHAs and raw receipts; refresh only changed evidence and invalidate dependent claims.
