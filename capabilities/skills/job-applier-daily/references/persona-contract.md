# Full persona for every posting

The thirteen required sections are mandate, decision_rights, operating_environment, industry_economics, outcomes_90_days, outcomes_year_one, capability_depth, leadership_behaviors, ideal_career_proof_metrics, hard_criteria, tradeoffs_failure_modes, hiring_questions, and adjacent_vs_direct_fit.

Use [the inherited schema](../../job-applier/references/family-packet.md). Bind every section to the current JD and requirement IDs. Label JD statements, public research, and inference separately; cite captured sources. Never invent private hiring-manager beliefs. Ideal proof and metrics describe an ideal candidate, not Mike. Every substantive qualification and responsibility needs result/action/strategy evidence alignment or an explicit gap.

Create the full persona for each new posting. A resumed identical revision reloads and validates the complete existing artifact and real receipt; it does not replace it with a summary. Changed source or persona content invalidates dependent artifacts and approvals. A real current JEV receipt is required at the persona gate. Field presence alone does not establish content quality.
