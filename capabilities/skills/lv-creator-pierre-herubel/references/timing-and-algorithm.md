## The timing advice he actually gives

- Publish at the same clock time every day so the audience forms a habit. He compares it to a TV show airing on schedule; he names no clock hours [DB-P9AUogXU].
- Cadence: daily, or at a minimum 3 times per week (creator claim on the minimum), and he ran daily posting for years himself. One passing line in the same video says three times a day; his own recap says three times a week [DB-P9AUogXU, IAn0_pfzero].
- For teams: stagger the week so the brand surfaces several times instead of in one burst. His example is 3 posts per person per week on offset days (Mon/Wed/Fri style), producing about 7 feed touches a week (creator claim) [mc7nK3PYgjw].
- Results land 2 to 3 months after publishing, and flat stretches run for months; his own six-month low-engagement stretch came while posting daily (creator claims) [vzQrN8BVE9c, c_2g0KM7fqY].

## Algorithm claims (his reading, unverified)

- Dwell time and re-reads raise a post's retention and therefore its impressions; time on post is the metric he says platforms optimize for [i-YDUfx84f8, SmuGlt1CDKY, mV1Ma2ZVgU8].
- Expanding the post registers as interest, but bouncing right after the expansion limits the gain [Io7Uq-S8dpk, SmuGlt1CDKY].
- Scrolling past a post sends a negative signal [SmuGlt1CDKY].
- Saves are a strong quality signal; he claims actionable posts earn saves at close to the rate of reactions [2eovlLOHOU4].
- Expert content is currently prioritized over personal stories (his claim, stated in both the 2024 and 2026 versions of his strategy framework) [zVC1HEH9060, 2HSavr17yq0].
- Generic AI-written content is currently punished by distribution (his claim, July 2026) [9o4BqmpgVT8].
- Carousel scrolling raises dwell time and reshare signals; opinion posts draw reactions, both agreement and disagreement, that the ranking rewards [9o4BqmpgVT8].
- Consistent frequency is favored while excessive irrelevant posting gets punished [6RJb6qYq1gA].
- General reach on LinkedIn has decreased, and reposts underperform originals partly for that reason (his claim, Jan 2025) [CphfSIw7WV8].
- Repeated multi-voice exposure makes a brand read as the dominant player in its niche (his claim, June 2026) [mc7nK3PYgjw].
- The algorithm, the market and the audience all change, so content must be continually reinvented; he states this as the reason nothing here is permanent [vzQrN8BVE9c].

## Applying it (RIG)
