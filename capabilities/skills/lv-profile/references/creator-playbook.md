# Creator playbook: profile (lv-profile)

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage.
Synthesis in RIG's words; bracketed IDs are the source videos; every number below is a creator
claim. Coverage note: Michelle teaches no personal-profile topic in her strategy file; her surface
is the company page, which `references/company-page.md` already owns, so she is skipped here.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. The profile is a landing page built around one next step. Clark sets it once in 5-10 minutes and
   makes every block point at the same next step [RVFC8i05e_k, MXBaHCO4WI8, E5vxr1IZCpI]; Diandra
   runs a stranger test and treats the page as a landing page plus a prompt [w3_109ygioY,
   R8nrEQx8rEY]; Harry [27IqjaAYSa0, aWw3kS2hOBI], Lara [UiKT7lxE4bo], Pierre [7NTNAIErphE,
   3sf8fojVfpM], Shanee [EejwRLkhHOc] and Kasey [aPa6qCvPFao] all say the same thing.
2. The headline names the buyer and the outcome; a bare job title is the failure case. Clark wants
   role, company and one line of value for a named audience [RVFC8i05e_k, MXBaHCO4WI8]; Harry role
   plus who you help [27IqjaAYSa0]; Kasey who you help plus what happens [aPa6qCvPFao,
   eyfNP4ipgrI]; Lara what, for whom, how, then proof [UiKT7lxE4bo, LBa8SdmItjU]; Mark one person
   type and one outcome in the first 8 to 10 words [wplSBnjBxt0]; Will role, audience, outcome,
   relevant proof [Ql4gXPDhalk]; Pierre never a bare job title [3sf8fojVfpM].
3. The About is a short sales page: the reader's problem, the wanted result, proof, one ask. Clark
   keeps it to a handful of sentences with one call to action [RVFC8i05e_k, MXBaHCO4WI8]; Diandra
   orders it problem, solution, proof, ask [w3_109ygioY, _ZFncQNrOhw]; Lara hooks, problem, result,
   proof, one off-platform ask [LBa8SdmItjU]; Pierre problem-agitate-solution [7NTNAIErphE];
   Shanee what, who, results, next step, and no biography [EejwRLkhHOc, toTLWp4aOtI]; Will wants a
   fifth-grade reading level and no life story [Ql4gXPDhalk]; Kasey wants transformation, proof and
   fit [aPa6qCvPFao].
4. Featured and the button are a conversion chain, not decoration. Clark one or two links, three at
   most, each to a next step [RVFC8i05e_k, MXBaHCO4WI8]; Kasey the chain post, profile, bio button,
   featured CTA, offer [eyfNP4ipgrI, hC7ZCVsG7qM]; Harry one next step and pinned proof
   [27IqjaAYSa0]; Lara the custom link into a funnel or magnet [UiKT7lxE4bo]; Pierre one landing
   page, one offer [7NTNAIErphE]; Will a magnet, a booking link or a case study, never a top post
   [Ql4gXPDhalk]; Shanee featured proof plus the pinned goal [EejwRLkhHOc].
5. A stranger must comprehend the page in seconds. Kasey and Shanee test at about three seconds
   [aPa6qCvPFao, 6f3ZzY5-gUg]; Will and RYVO at five [Ql4gXPDhalk, rgILPN6j31A]; Diandra at ten
   [w3_109ygioY, _ZFncQNrOhw]. The clock differs, the rule does not.
6. Proof in the profile beats adjectives. Harry pins proof [27IqjaAYSa0]; Lara puts proof in the
   headline, banner, Featured and posts at once, and with no logos yet puts a testimonial on the
   banner [hCzcM6jV_zY]; Mark keeps a best-proof link and authority markers [i4lHm-CGiwU,
   8BidcQ5viLs]; Pierre wants visual testimonials in the Services slot [7NTNAIErphE]; Will features
   a case study [Ql4gXPDhalk].
7. Every field points the same way. Diandra writes profile copy a searcher can match and front-loads
   the headline in buyer language [w3_109ygioY]; Lara says every field points at the same thing and
   the profile is read like a prompt [e3E83C-Xxn0, b_sVHSaQqGA]; Shanee makes headshot, banner and
   about signal one category [bCRYL6aL3ec]; Harry realigns content, message and profile together
   when the viewers are not buyers [sUC5UAXni6M].

## What nobody teaches

- Nobody teaches how to verify that a profile edit actually persisted. The executor/verifier pair
  and cold-reload probes in `references/profile-audit-and-probes.md` cover that gap alone.
- No creator covers a profile serving three audiences at once (influencer, job, clients). The
  single-buyer pages above assume one game; the two-headline split in `references/job-search-profile.md`
  is the stage's answer.
- Nobody gives a numeric threshold for reverting a profile change. Will gives a rollout order and
  Pierre an audit cadence, but no failure criterion.
- Nobody teaches governed, claim-audited profile copy where each figure needs a measured source;
  their own numbers are creator claims, and the claim rules in `references/headline-and-keywords.md`
  are stricter than anything in this set.
