## Core laws

1.  **Revenue Proximity Principle**: Systems touching the front end (lead gen, sales) command ~350% higher project value than back-end systems because revenue-justified spend beats savings-justified spend.CLAIM(T5-RXi-Zk_g@1m)[consensus]
2.  **Digital-First Targeting**: Target digital businesses over local brick-and-mortar to ensure easier lead sourcing, higher tech literacy, and faster sales cycles, as local owners often lack budget and technical understanding.CLAIM(TxMAUMUn-is@0m),CLAIM(OLwEtOEF36A@1m),CLAIM(8HL0IwuuqMQ@0m)[consensus]
3.  **The No-Brainer Offer Structure**: A compelling offer must be delivered in 1-2 weeks, cost little to fulfill, minimize client risk via guarantees, and naturally lead to a recurring retainer.CLAIM(ArNmQRpctIc@3m),CLAIM(eLqveVYFWc4@10m)[consensus]
4.  **Guarantees Drive Conversion**: Offering specific performance guarantees (e.g., X leads in Y days) removes logical barriers to trust and can increase conversion rates by ~300%, outweighing the cost of occasional refunds.CLAIM(Gz8tbgZTdpI@1m),CLAIM(miYIg4F9aE4@9m),CLAIM(kChb6HrYOeE@10m)[consensus]
5.  **Retainer Economics**: Recurring revenue multiplies business value; a $2,500/month client out-earns a $5,000 one-off within the year, and retained clients yield higher margins due to workflow efficiency.CLAIM(T5-RXi-Zk_g@4m),CLAIM(RmYTguzXrVM@4m),CLAIM(kChb6HrYOeE@2m)[consensus]
6.  **Sell Outcomes, Not Tech**: Pitch business outcomes like revenue and risk reduction, not technical specifications or model names, as clients pay for problem resolution, not access to AI tools.CLAIM(Fw8NGGnEchs@0m),CLAIM(Y54kp53gi_M@2m),CLAIM(LQMAnCIIPgM@111m)[consensus]
7.  **The 90% Outreach Rule**: Allocate 90% of working time to outreach and lead generation rather than building systems or learning tools, as no leads equals no business regardless of skill.CLAIM(mE4gqfLBAYs@7m),CLAIM(-vOVyjmT7FQ@7m)[consensus]
8.  **Speed of Delivery**: Sending a customized proposal within 30 minutes of a sales call significantly increases close rates by reducing the gap between interest and commitment.CLAIM(whXs1RaQIDA@1m),CLAIM(T9diMzLIpqQ@1m)[consensus]
9.  **Personalization at Scale**: Use AI to generate personalized icebreakers based on web research or profile data to increase cold email reply rates from ~2% to 10%, avoiding generic templates.CLAIM(OLwEtOEF36A@18m),CLAIM(whXs1RaQIDA@45m),CLAIM(jBF48jNWPJE@5m)[consensus]
10. **Value-Based Pricing**: Charge a percentage of the money saved (10-25%) or revenue generated (10-30%) rather than hourly rates, which punish efficiency and misalign incentives.CLAIM(owdN96DwdT4@7m),CLAIM(NsQWWnu7vzI@6m),CLAIM(Y54kp53gi_M@15m)[consensus]
11. **Bottleneck Identification**: Identify the single tightest bottleneck in a business pipeline (usually lead gen) and sell a system that specifically alleviates that constraint to justify higher pricing.CLAIM(Gz8tbgZTdpI@4m),CLAIM(OLwEtOEF36A@29m)[consensus]
12. **Simple Over Complex**: Sell simple, linear automations that solve specific problems rather than complex AI agents, as they are easier to explain, deliver, and maintain, leading to higher perceived value.CLAIM(jBF48jNWPJE@0m),CLAIM(cZuvJxmjdxU@2m)[consensus]
13. **The Double-Tap Strategy**: Use multiple channels (email, LinkedIn, Twitter) to reach prospects, as this maximizes the chance of the message being seen and cuts through the noise of single-channel outreach.CLAIM(bao3ogOcpiw@3m),CLAIM(miYIg4F9aE4@4m)[consensus]
14. **Pre-Sell Before Build**: Validate demand by selling the concept and gathering feedback before building the product, using live market data to define features and avoid wasting time on unvalidated ideas.CLAIM(eLqveVYFWc4@0m),CLAIM(LQMAnCIIPgM@11m)[consensus]
15. **Onboarding as Sales**: Treat onboarding as a retention tool by sending immediate payment receipts, personalized thank-yous, and clear next steps to reduce buyer's remorse and build trust.CLAIM(hRBL6ISvB3s@12m),CLAIM(hRBL6ISvB3s@3m)[consensus]
16. **Avoid Regulated Industries**: Steer clear of heavily regulated sectors like healthcare or legal unless necessary, as compliance requirements can increase implementation effort by 5-10x without proportional pay.CLAIM(OLwEtOEF36A@6m),CLAIM(LQMAnCIIPgM@6m)[consensus]
17. **The 15-Message Minimum**: Aim for a daily minimum of 15 personalized outreach messages to maintain momentum and pipeline activity without burning out, focusing on consistency over perfection.CLAIM(bao3ogOcpiw@25m),CLAIM(mE4gqfLBAYs@9m)[consensus]
18. **Pricing Ladder**: Progress from free/low-paid work to hourly rates, then to fixed-price projects, and finally to monthly retainers to build trust and increase margins over time.CLAIM(bao3ogOcpiw@46m),CLAIM(owdN96DwdT4@3m)[consensus]
19. **Borrow Credibility**: When lacking social proof, join platform partner programs, buy press releases, or complete certifications to transfer brand authority to your profile and increase trust.CLAIM(breoPPRHFfo@1m),CLAIM(breoPPRHFfo@3m)[consensus]
20. **Human-in-the-Loop**: Include a human review step for AI-generated content to fix errors and ensure brand voice alignment, as fully automated publishing often degrades quality and trust.CLAIM(jBF48jNWPJE@18m),CLAIM(XlYQa0GgfaU@32m)[consensus]
21. **Niche Commitment**: Commit to a specific niche for at least 90 days without switching to avoid resetting progress, as consistency allows for measurable optimization and deeper market understanding.CLAIM(mE4gqfLBAYs@24m),CLAIM(z1KP_Urfz5Q@1m)[consensus]
22. **Upsell Logic**: Design introductory offers that logically necessitate a follow-up service (e.g., lead gen requiring a CRM) to create a natural path to higher-priced retainers.CLAIM(ArNmQRpctIc@22m),CLAIM(RmYTguzXrVM@9m)[consensus]
23. **Avoid "All-in-One" Agents**: Do not sell complex AI agents that attempt to manage entire operations, as they lack reliability; focus on rigid, deterministic tasks like data enrichment or ticket routing.CLAIM(whXs1RaQIDA@26m),CLAIM(cZuvJxmjdxU@12m)[consensus]
24. **Response Time Matters**: Respond to inbound leads within 60 seconds to 5 minutes to maximize conversion, as speed signals competence and captures interest before it fades.CLAIM(whXs1RaQIDA@4m),CLAIM(wLNw-rpklfE@89m)[consensus]
25. **Scope Creep Prevention**: Limit client involvement to a single initial form or brief check-in and define success criteria upfront to prevent scope creep and project delays.CLAIM(ArNmQRpctIc@9m),CLAIM(hRBL6ISvB3s@29m)[consensus]
