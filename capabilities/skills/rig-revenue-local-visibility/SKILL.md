# Local visibility install

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

Governing contracts: rig-revenue-meta. Compliance is the product here: fake hours, keyword-stuffed
names, and review keyword-stuffing are excluded tactics and get flagged if a client asks for them.

## Inputs

- Verified business facts: legal name (and DBA if any), address, phone, real hours, service list,
  service areas, categories, credentials/licences held
- Access: Google Business Profile owner access, website CMS, top competitor profile(s)

## Outputs

- Optimized GBP (every field completed truthfully)
- NAP consistency report with exact fixes per surface
- Review engine: request script, compliant reply templates, velocity plan
- Service-by-city page plan with unique content per page + build queue
- Compliance exceptions log (anything refused and why)

## Step 1. GBP protocol (rig-revenue-gbp-protocol)

1. Business name must match the legal/registered name. A keyword-bearing name is only acceptable
   via a real DBA/rename — never stuffed, never fake.
2. Primary category = the money service in customer language ("emergency plumber", not the trade
   jargon customers don't type). Secondary categories cover the rest truthfully.
3. Load every real service (20-30 where true) organised under categories. Do not list services
   the firm does not offer.
4. Complete every field: description, hours (real), services, products, attributes, accessibility,
   payment methods, Q&A. Real photos only — no stock, no untagged AI images.
5. Stage edits over days, not one suspicious burst.

## Step 2. NAP consistency (rig-revenue-nap-consistency)

1. Inventory every listing: GBP, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, industry directories (10-15 real
   ones), site footer/contact.
2. Diff name/address/phone/hours character-by-character ("suite" vs "ste" counts). Fix to one
   canonical form.
3. Remove or dispute stale duplicate listings. Report anything that cannot be fixed (owner
   unavailable) instead of silently skipping.

## Step 3. Review engine (rig-revenue-review-engine)

1. Review ask: short, personal, after the job — QR/link direct to the review form.
2. Reply to every review restating the service naturally. Compliance rules are mandatory:
   never confirm a client relationship in a reply (HIPAA/confidentiality), never name a reviewer
   negatively, never argue in public, move complaints offline ("Please reach out so we can make it
   right." is corpus phrasing — CLAIM: wO_8Gcw2kr8).
3. Velocity over size: steady new reviews beat a stale pile. Never buy or seed reviews.

## Step 4. Service-by-city pages

1. One page per service per city the firm genuinely serves — but every page must carry unique,
   true substance (case detail approved for use, local specifics, named expert). A swapped city
   name is a doorway page and is refused (rig-revenue-meta risk register).
2. Location pages link to service pages and back; they answer the query in the first lines
   (rig-revenue-answer-first).
3. Build order: money service × primary city first.

## Step 5. Report and decline

Deliver the NAP report, the review velocity plan, and the page queue. Log compliance exceptions
(refused fake-hours request, protected-title question → escalate per rig-revenue-meta).
