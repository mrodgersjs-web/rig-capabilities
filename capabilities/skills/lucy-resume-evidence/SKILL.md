# Lucy resume evidence

Write the strongest role-specific executive working draft first. Mike owns the evidence-mapping stage: he attaches the real examples and confirms the facts. This is a Mike-specific workflow; do not claim Lucy authored or approved the complete method.

Read [the shared evidence contract](../lucy-executive-evidence/references/evidence-contract.md). Read [before-and-after examples](references/before-after.md) when a metric or causal claim needs repair. For Lucy's resume-specific advice, use the installed sibling `../lucy-skills/SKILL.md` and its relevant resume leaf; cite that leaf's actual SRC IDs. The interview source `c_o9EVzBiVE` alone does not establish a Lucy resume rule.

## Default: ideal-executive working draft

1. Read the actual JD and any supplied resume. Preserve the original text. Identify the business outcomes and strongest achievements an ideal executive would need to demonstrate. Map each drafted achievement to the requirement it addresses.
2. Produce a polished **IDEAL-ACHIEVEMENT WORKING DRAFT — PENDING MIKE'S EVIDENCE REVIEW** immediately. Do not require Mike to answer a fact questionnaire before generating the draft. Use already-sourced facts where available; leave unconfirmed facts as bracketed fields.
3. Write executive resume syntax: **outcome → personal action → mechanism**, with scale where relevant. Example: “Reduced [inventory carrying cost] by [confirmed amount] through [actual intervention], across [scope] during [period].” Put the draft status outside the bullet. A working bullet may use past-tense resume phrasing because its placeholders and section label make clear that it is an achievement pattern awaiting evidence, not an assertion that the event happened.
4. For an unsourced achievement, preserve fields for [actual employer], [role or ownership], [result and units], [actual intervention], [scope], and [period] in the bullet or its mapping row. Do not invent plausible figures, clients, causality, tenure, or completed projects. Do not put an unsourced pattern under a real employer as though it happened there.
5. Use the JD's terminology to define the target achievement. Mark its actual evidence match pending. Once Mike supplies history, retain exact JD wording only where it describes what actually happened. Give the before/after text and requirement map beside the draft.

## Human evidence review → application-ready resume

Mike supplies or confirms the actual example for each pattern. Populate the ledger with employer, title, dates/duration, personal action, mechanism, result/units, scope, and source limits. He may replace an ideal pattern with a different real achievement. “I've done everything” establishes no particular number, employer, timeframe, ownership, or causal mechanism; keep those fields pending without withholding the working draft.

Approval of wording is not confirmation an event occurred. Distinguish a specific factual confirmation from “that sounds right.” Keep source-confirmed/candidate-reported history distinct from independently verified evidence. Do not claim the agent completed Mike's evidence review.

After factual confirmation, produce the application-ready version using only supported claims. Remove all working labels and unresolved placeholders from that version; omit or safely narrow unsupported achievements. Keep a separate gap note if a material requirement remains unestablished. If Mike explicitly asks to edit only confirmed history, use this mode directly.

Preserve actual employers and supported titles, units, scope, periods, and individual/team ownership. Employment duration is not the time it took to achieve a result. Check the summary against the same evidence ledger so it cannot reintroduce an unsupported bullet claim.

An old AI-generated answer is a derivative reference, not independent event confirmation.

## Hard boundaries

- An interview scenario can inspire a clearly labeled ideal-achievement working pattern with bracketed fact fields. It cannot become completed resume history through approval of the wording.
- Preserve the metric's meaning: cost versus revenue, inventory stock versus recurring savings, business size versus purchase price, prototypes versus scaled businesses.
- Do not reattribute an achievement to another employer, invent a technology mechanism, or turn a team result into sole personal execution.
- Avoid “perfect fit” and guaranteed ATS/interview/hiring outcomes. Describe supported capabilities and leave material gaps visible.

## Deliver and stop

Return the requested working draft or application-ready version, exact before/after table, requirement-to-achievement map, and evidence-review fields. In working mode, generate the useful draft first and give Mike a concise place to attach his real examples. Do not present a pending mapping as a completed verification.

Before calling a version application-ready, check every factual clause against the confirmed ledger and verify that no placeholders remain. This skill stages text only; it does not publish, submit, send, change a profile, or replace an installed resume registry entry. Those steps follow the ordinary scoped authorization workflow after human evidence confirmation. For interview answers or spoken industry applications, use [lucy-executive-evidence](../lucy-executive-evidence/SKILL.md).
