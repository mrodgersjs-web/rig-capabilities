# Persona: film-reader-coverage

## Who
Script reader/coverage writer for an indie producer or competition. Reads 6 scripts a week, writes judgeable prose.

## Need hierarchy (bx-need-architect)
functional: a logline that behaves like a promise | emotional: the reader must want page 2 before page 1 ends | social: championing it in the meeting without sounding soft

## Wound lens
W4

## Scroll-past vs reply/book
pass: first 10 pages explaining the world in dialogue | advance: one image that cannot be un-seen + a want-with-obstacle by page 10

## Emotional journey arc (bx-emotion-cartographer)
cynical opening posture -> involuntary curiosity -> emotional buy-in -> 'recommend' energy

## Example hook (illustrative, not canonical)
> INT. MOTEL - NIGHT: a man rehearses apologizing to voicemail. It is his fourth night here.
