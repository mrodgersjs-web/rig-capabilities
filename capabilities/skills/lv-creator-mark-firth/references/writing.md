# Writing

Sub-skill of `lv-creator-mark-firth`. Synthesis in RIG's words. Bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims.

## 1. What a post is for now

The catalog moves from templates to stories to belief-change, and the dates matter.

- Through early 2024 he preferred reusable templates over experience stories, reposted winners after 6 weeks to 3 months, and told people to ignore grammar. [N2AOzgp-SIk, gTkfDjUBXMY]
- On 2025-04-07 he reversed the template preference: stories beat commands, and he claims stories tripled response. Vanity metrics were called worthless the same day. [ -0NvIx7Kjfo ]
- On 2025-05-20 the working post is a three-part transformation, short lines, a question that only that story can ask, and a remix of hits rather than new topics. Readability target: 5th to 7th grade (creator claim). Attention window he cites: 8 seconds (creator claim, not a lab result). [uEHPPU2k_OM]
- On 2026-01-27 teaching posts lose to belief-change posts. Write the closer first. The body is a character arc, not a tip list. He claims money and time beliefs cover about 80 percent of non-buying (creator claim). Density of feeling-words is something he prompts for, citing a book count of 20,000 (creator claim, not a method to copy). [T7G4_owju2A]
- On 2026-03-13 failure stories replace success stories. A tacked-on moral killed reach in his telling. Take no side on a news post. [r2LsebGinxs]
- On 2026-05-13 the story is a brief moment, told from the resolved side of a past struggle, ending at the realization. An explained lesson is the failure mode. He claims reach can fall while DMs rise, and that this is the point. [FEOAeCCK9e0]
- On 2026-09-04 prescriptive content is out. Experiential stories are in. Offer wording comes from what buyers already want, not from a feature list. [7qDcQrCbIbI]

Do not teach the 2024 template rule as current. Do not teach the 2025 how-to list as current if the task is a 2026 post.

## 2. Body craft

Whitespace at spoken pauses. He wants copy that looks like talk, including a dictate-then-edit pass. Grammar is secondary to pause. [gTkfDjUBXMY, eubB4zi3HG4, lzOWlqfHz-s, PVX6FHGp4Uc]

Short lines. Two or three lines per story beat in the video version of the same doctrine. Two or three bullets per story in the belief-change version. [HMHGfcaMFlg, T7G4_owju2A]

One post, one turn. The 2025 story arc he repeats is time frame, a scene the reader recognizes, a short action, and a felt outcome. He times the action beat at 30 to 40 seconds when the post is spoken (creator claim). [eubB4zi3HG4, MnQc73VYbJ0]

Voice constraints from 2026, which override earlier "just tell them what to do" copy:

- Stop commanding. He explicitly rejects lines that tell the reader they should or they must. [lzOWlqfHz-s, k7xn5Rn6_Sw]
- One owned weakness, and it is in the past. A current weakness is not the asset. [iubQmgPG3ZM]
- Do not trauma-dump. A brief moment lets the reader project. An explained joke is a dead joke, in his analogy. [FEOAeCCK9e0]
- Personality over credentials once the January 2026 distribution claim is in force. Credentials still sit in the headline. They do not carry the post. [MJFCR1EJdrw, rKwbCjanWCo]
- If a model helps, it interviews him out of his own history and keeps his voice. A tone-flattening chat draft is the failure. This does not authorize a blank-page draft. Eden still needs a real story or receipt. [FEOAeCCK9e0]

The March 2026 re-upload still teaches dictate-then-edit, a question at the end, and no-ask DMs from the 2024 playbook. Use it for those mechanics only if they do not contradict a later video. It is not a license to revive template posts. [PVX6FHGp4Uc]

## 4. CTAs

The durable CTA is a question tied to the post, duplicated in the first comment. Generic engagement bait is what he later calls machine slop. [gTkfDjUBXMY, lzOWlqfHz-s, uEHPPU2k_OM, 0WBbXeM5pJM]

Slot shapes, not his lines:

- "Which of these [steps in this post] fits [buyer constraint]?"
- "Where did this break for you: [step A] or [step B]?"

He also uses a no-ask period. One video claims six months of an empty calendar before any CTA (creator claim, his own story, not a rule for Mike). [ -0NvIx7Kjfo ]

Profile-visitor messages are a separate CTA: curiosity, not a pitch, aimed at people whose profile matches the buyer. [uEHPPU2k_OM, HMHGfcaMFlg]

January 2026 adds an indirect ask with an easy no, after rapport, not as the first line of a post. [rXs_4EsUB4w]

## 5. Voice and offers inside the post

Pitch the identity outcome, not the feature list. He dates this to a buyer shift: people self-educate before the call, so the post is not a brochure. [g2MSqSUOX6c]

Sell done-for-you outcomes to established owners. Courses are the offer he told that market to stop leading with, from 2025-04-04. [ArYphaJtvmc, 8BidcQ5viLs]

Chat-style outreach copy, as opposed to post copy, is short spaced lines, closer to a message app than a letter. That is a January 2026 messaging rule, not a post layout. [rXs_4EsUB4w]
