# Commenting, replies and DMs (Michelle J Raymond)

Commenting is her strongest teaching area after company pages. Her claim: comments are the discovery engine, because comment impressions reach mostly people beyond your first degree, and by late 2025 a good comment can out-travel a post [kXBXV1bQKes, lYqfrZZA64o, LG_sBQB2xAc]. She refuses fixed comment quotas and everything automated [LG_sBQB2xAc, THXjj1yM6Kw].

## How much, and when

- **No universal quota (her explicit refusal, restated against her own earlier cadence episode).** If you are not posting: 15 to 30 minutes a day on ideal-audience posts. If you post two or three times a week: five comments across five days [LG_sBQB2xAc].
- **A working ladder for growth:** about ten thoughtful comments a day on target-audience posts, plus five to ten genuine DMs a day, alongside a posting cadence that ramps from one to three or four a week [kXBXV1bQKes].
- **In a slow quarter: 15 calendared minutes a day** covering post, comment and message keeps you on the buyer's short list (she cites buyers shortlisting about three firms) [Ldms-a_C04Y]. A one-week burst is not a habit.
- **Fifteen to twenty minutes of genuine connection a day** is the anti-growth-hack version of the same routine (guest Scott Aaron) [THXjj1yM6Kw].

## What to write

- **Three comment shapes (hers):** a mini thought-leadership comment that stands alone as an idea; a tag-and-engage comment that brings a peer into the room; and a call-to-action comment that can hold a link (she doubts the platform likes links in comments, creator claim). A deep comment is raw material for your own post [LG_sBQB2xAc].
- **Useful outruns short.** A comment that adds an idea cannot be copied from elsewhere; a page bot restating the post looks terrible [LG_sBQB2xAc].
- **RATE applies to comments too** (Relevant, Authentic, Thought leadership, Empathy) [udtNia_2Onk].
- **Invite the reader into a conversation you are prepared to lead,** and accept that a good comment warms stranger-to-familiar before any DM [Dd42KDThdPY, kXBXV1bQKes].

## Commenting as the page (her 2026 page play)

- **Page advocacy:** the page should comment outward more than it broadcasts, joining conversations it was never tagged in [jAzJ0hWhtqw, _yf76tCe-zY].
- **A comment cap exists.** Her ManyChat case commented so much that LinkedIn blocked further comments; she does not know the number and says it later reset (creator claim) [_yf76tCe-zY].
- **Identity switching:** a page, book or podcast account can comment as itself through the profile switcher [LG_sBQB2xAc].
- **Page comment impressions were still rolling out** as she understood it in mid-2026 (creator claim, unverified) [p_jnafHMun4, J3hMUDIk7qE].

## Who to comment on

- **A halo of about twenty people** you want a reciprocal tie with, plus the buyers and the creators they read [Q8rO8FCy72w].
- **Comment targets train the feed** in her reading: every comment is a signal about what you want to be shown (creator claim, unverified) [LG_sBQB2xAc, 8Yrgj-zOYQ8, OCpDoffJO_I].
- **Comment discovery is measurable now:** profile analytics can show when someone found you through a comment (creator claim) [J3hMUDIk7qE].

## DMs and warm follow-up

- **Answer your comments and DMs** before adding volume; selling is value, then relationship, then the offer [dNDNxyoD4qE].
- **Poll voters are a warm list.** You can see who chose what; message each segment with something matching their vote [k3dCMwu8Nmk].
- **Newsletter subscribers are a warmer list still:** mine second and third degree subscribers, thank them, and open a conversation [TtJFDeUqUNM].
- **Outreach is a question, not a pitch slap.** Her guest sent no DMs for six months while rebuilding a profile [xR6t2llZRKQ].

## Hard no's

- **No engagement pods.** They never converted to calls, DMs or hires in her guest's account; pod tells are uniform engagement, the same small commenter set, and polls with more comments than votes [THXjj1yM6Kw, 1pjFtyAEVcc].
- **No bought followers, rented accounts or scraping tools.** She cites account loss and reputational cost, and both she and Michaela Alexis refuse pods outright [THXjj1yM6Kw, ZbylvpCOCzA].
- **No automated blasts.** Automation wrecks the relationship the comment was supposed to build [2JI55PLI9Zw, LG_sBQB2xAc].
