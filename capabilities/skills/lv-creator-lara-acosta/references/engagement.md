# Comments, replies and the engagement engine (Lara Acosta)

Load this file for commenting on other people's posts, replies on your own, self-comments, and target lists. This is the part of her doctrine that most directly collides with RIG rules, so the collision is spelled out at the bottom.

## The comment as content

- Her standing claim: a well-made comment reaches further than a weak post, and comment impressions count toward the account. Creator claim, unverified. [6EYqnuncRrg, r7nnmWBTHUk, ueIjOIWS4fY]
- Comments are mini-posts. She bans empty praise outright and gives three working types: a personal take on the point, a tone-matching quotation or reference, and humour. [ZK-CBIFDR9w, r7nnmWBTHUk]
- Never a one-word agreement line. Her stronger version of a comment shows the work, serves the same audience the post serves, and pulls a click on merit. [MLXZ_aa2ZKw, OSzrHwHLT6M]
- Never AI-written comments: she says readers spot them instantly. [ozhwN9ndetM, ZK-CBIFDR9w]
- A comment should be a mini case, a respectful challenge, or a tip in her expert-era advice; she also teaches commenting where the buyer's problem is being described, including questions the original poster ignored. [OSzrHwHLT6M, MLXZ_aa2ZKw]

## Targeting

- Build a named list rather than scrolling. Her earlier term is a Dream 100 of targets plus adjacent people; the 2024 version is 20 to 30 active accounts a day, and a 2024-12 version is about 10 accounts of roughly 10K followers each. Creator claim. [ZK-CBIFDR9w, 6EYqnuncRrg]
- Filter the commenters under a target's post by their headline so you engage the buyers, not the crowd. [Slm6Ek465ow, 6EYqnuncRrg]
- Study mode: track about 20 creators for a week or a month and study the openings that already work instead of scrolling for entertainment. [3gbPTfHEWNI]
- Her timing trick from 2024: arrive at a target's publish minute and leave something good enough to start a thread. She admits a bland version of this fails. [_6lKN2fZgIs]
- Stay in the bubble. Comments outside the niche or on people who never reply teach the wrong lesson. [1wsfrYzwKbk]

## Replies and self-comments on your own post

- Three self-comments per post, then reply to everyone: the 2025 shape is three self-comments followed by a reply round for 30 to 60 days of habit, and the 2026 teardown shape is the same three plus a reply round inside 30 minutes. [ozhwN9ndetM, 6d_oxo1R9gs]
- What the three self-comments carry: something behind the scenes, one tip, and a question that starts soft conversation. [6d_oxo1R9gs]
- Reply to every comment in the early window, and if engagement is thin keep commenting there rather than waiting. [6EYqnuncRrg, 6d_oxo1R9gs]
- If engagement dies mid-flight, her 2024 restart move is a short thank-you to a handful of recent commenters. [_6lKN2fZgIs]
- Her claimed effect for a call-to-engage closing question is a large multiple on likes; creator claim, and she gives no controlled test. [6d_oxo1R9gs]

## Warm-up, pods and what she rejected

- 2025-07: 30 minutes of commenting before publishing to earn reciprocity, then self-comments and replies after. She calls it a warm-up you earn rather than a forced pod. [ozhwN9ndetM]
- 2026-06: the first-hour friend rush and the perfect posting clock are the old system, and a comment pod is the wrong comment. This retires the warm-up she taught a year earlier. [e3E83C-Xxn0]
- 2026-05: she says nobody inside the platform, staff included, knows the ranking mechanism, and that an engagement paper cited as proof was withdrawn; her co-panelist calls the algorithm aggregated behaviour. User inference on both counts. [7SKd2cchGd4, e3E83C-Xxn0]
- 2026-09: the one-plus-three engine on 20 creators returns with a denial that it is a pod. The contradiction stands in the record: she bans pods and runs engineered reciprocity in the same year. [0ZVUVa4IFTw, e3E83C-Xxn0]

## Comments and DMs together

- Commenters, profile viewers and new connections are the DM list; the message opens with thanks plus a light question, never a pitch. [Slm6Ek465ow, 9QiqofqNHq8]
- After a magnet comment, her clock is a DM within 12 to 24 hours and a call only once they ask. [MLXZ_aa2ZKw]
- She warns that pitching a stranger in a DM is how a warm account goes cold. [EACWADXrg88]

## Applying it (RIG)

RIG takes the targeting logic (named accounts where the buyer reads, comments that carry a real insight, replies as conversation) and drops the quota, the reciprocity choreography and anything that coordinates engagement. Mike's rule governs: comments on other people's posts are positive-only, one real insight, optionally pointing at his own related post, and never AI-written. Her 20-per-day adds and cold outreach live inside `linkedin-site-mechanics` throttles with nothing sent automatically without Mike's explicit yes. The comment draft itself goes through `rig-s1gate` with the `comment.linkedin` rubric.
