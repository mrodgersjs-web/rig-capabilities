# Lucy skills, portable resume and interview edition

This edition contains reusable authored guidance for resumes and executive interview evidence. It does not include a full video corpus or private career examples.

For a resume, read [Lucy resume evidence](../lucy-resume-evidence/SKILL.md), then [the resume guidance](references/resume-guidance.md) for the source-specific tactics. For executive interviews, read [Lucy executive evidence](../lucy-executive-evidence/SKILL.md). For an application, use [Job Applier](../job-applier/SKILL.md). The [lucyskills alias](../lucyskills/SKILL.md) reaches the same leaves.

Truth outranks presentation tactics. A target-role heading describes positioning; it cannot rename an employment title or invent industry experience. Quantification is useful only when the figure and meaning are supported. Do not force a numerical result when the evidence supports a qualitative one.

Every attributed Lucy rule uses its actual source video ID. Wording written for the candidate is an adaptation. Exact anchors are limited to the cited source map. No advice guarantees an ATS score, interview, offer, or hiring outcome.

If asked for a broader Lucy workflow not present in this edition, state its scope. Do not imply that this package includes salary negotiation, LinkedIn automation, or the full historical corpus.
