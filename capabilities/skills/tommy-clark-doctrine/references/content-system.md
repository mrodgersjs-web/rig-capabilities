## 2. Funnel mix

- **Top:** reach. Founder stories, broad takes, milestones.
- **Middle:** authority for the buyer, unpitched. Opinions, frameworks, data.
- **Bottom:** proof. Case studies, shipped features, launches, offers.
- Split moved from 10-20 / 60-70 / 10-20 (2024) to 20 / 60 / 20 (Sept 2025). At five posts that is 1 / 3 / 1; at three posts, one per stage. [K2H2KGTbZ08, mJ1c1PHBoo4, E0uu_GLIkw0, yBKOrgQS-q8]
- Bottom-of-funnel posts read as milestones and team pride, never as a pitch. [CkoSKSZ6WIU]
- 2026 alternative, the barbell slate: one moonshot (heavy research or original data), one scrappy fast post (a rant, a screenshot), one experiment. Avoid the polished middle that AI can imitate. [s6KMjhznXqs]
- Easy wins protect cadence when a deep post will not ship: a new-hire highlight or a milestone takes minutes. [2Yv8osegsKY, gZwWBtiZ7qE]

## 4. Production

- **Content interview:** every two weeks, 30-45 minutes, 5-10 prompts mapped to funnel stages and worked backward from posts you intend to ship. A domain-literate interviewer keeps the founder on track; the transcript is raw stone and the writer carves the posts; the founder edits for voice (5-10 minutes a post). He claims this cuts founder time from 10-20 hours a week to about 2. [mNR3-MrM5wI, UKEUZHJUOCM, SL8yfxUVFKE, yBKOrgQS-q8]
- **Calendar mining:** 20-30 minutes on Friday reviewing the week's meetings for moments with emotion and a lesson. [HFCfw6gznRM, uYVwSbaztJ4]
- **Idea capture:** pinned notes, screenshots, a running doc and a swipe file so no draft starts blank; a winners playbook of hooks, stories and visuals. [E0uu_GLIkw0, TZSADGhY-x4, pk_SUAVD3mA]
- **Writing habit:** 30-60 minutes a day; write the way you talk and read it aloud; improve one deliberate thing per post. [K2H2KGTbZ08, DGYZ04X5pk4, _YPmpy4fVGw]
- **One approver.** Committees make corporate mush. The founder stays the final filter; delegating is fine, abdicating is not. [amGv0M4IXD8, 8nBIAUSIRnQ, vcIAAUP6b8Y]
- **Ghostwriter fit:** the founder still gives 1-2 hours a week, interviews happen biweekly, the founder can reach the writer directly, and the writer carries no more than about four clients. [jpV4OXtHuk4, Z4u7kupNmqY]

## 5. AI rules (current)

- Allowed: research, editing, stress-testing an argument, repurposing long-form material, running the interview for a solo founder. [2XeAckxuAKM, tYd_MnpXU90, SL8yfxUVFKE]
- Not allowed: AI taking a post from nothing to a draft, and AI-written comments of any kind. [8nBIAUSIRnQ, UjDZLhuEpnQ]
- Signs that read as machine-made: formula hook phrasing, bland middle-of-the-road prose, a quiet account that suddenly posts daily. [SL8yfxUVFKE, s6KMjhznXqs, 8nBIAUSIRnQ]
- In spring 2025 he still allowed Claude to draft from scratch. That is superseded. [mTXBTznvNZI, vcFh5-fynHc]

## 6. Content ecosystem (several accounts)

- Order: founder (months 1-3), co-founders and C-suite (months 4-6), other leaders, then willing employees. Expect 2-5 genuinely willing people. Give each account its own slice of the ICP, and never hand out copy-paste advocacy scripts. He says 3-5 active employee posters puts a company in roughly the top 0.1%. [N2b0QVoCEPg, gKOKuHX1wRo, tYd_MnpXU90, BMfXeo02bvo]
- Big moments get an echo chamber: founder, company page and team originals (plus investors) for one or two days. [Xt179qkyqQU]
- The company page gets a post a week at most and serves as a proof surface. [N2b0QVoCEPg, 2Yv8osegsKY]
- Give followers an owned exit (newsletter) through the featured section and the profile button. [aL-6NagH7cM, sDuWUBoHsRI]

## 7. Voice and intensity

- Raise safe drafts about 20%: frame beliefs as personal boundaries, keep a villain but punch up, and place the softening caveat lower in the post so readers argue in the comments. [nbXzXfthKl0, h3751DcSeQw, RpOt0kEWAFc]
- Small imperfections read human; over-polished template posts wear readers out ("optimization fatigue"). [_tON8MpkmS0]
