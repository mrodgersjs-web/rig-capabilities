# Iterative Clarity Framework for Idea Validation

Use when: Run bounded campaign hypothesis revisions with durable attempt state

Inputs: workspace brief, approved claims, supporting evidence

1. Start with the initial 'seed of an idea' which is often in a state of 'fog' or excitement.
2. Apply a framework to 'clear it out or clarify it' by identifying the key differentiator.
3. Determine what makes the product stand out in the market.
4. Assess if the idea is 'special' and if there is a viable advantage.
5. If the idea lacks differentiation or motivation, decide to abandon it before investing further.

Output contract:

- A clear decision on whether to proceed with the idea based on its uniqueness and market fit.

Limitations:

- Requires honest self-assessment to avoid 'drunk on the fog' bias.
- May result in abandoning ideas that could have succeeded with more iteration.
