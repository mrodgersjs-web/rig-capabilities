# Message Match Critique

Purpose: type tone, font cap, poster and ad rescue, logo brief, swoosh geometry, hero statement vs question. Owns 9, 10, 21-27, 44, 53-60, 68-70, 89-93.

Load when: the task touches this part of critique and quality gates, or a ds-critique critique names it. Part of `ds-critique`.

## Checklist
1. Work through every law below against the current artifact before calling the sub-topic done.
2. Cite the CLAIM when a law drives a decision; a law you cannot cite is labeled own reasoning.
3. Where creators disagree (see the SKILL.md), state both positions and test rather than assume.

## Anti-patterns for this sub-topic
- Playful type on a harsh message. CLAIM(k2DWClcoHFI@02:14)
- Square button matching a square illustration, in the same dark value. CLAIM(k2DWClcoHFI@14:08)
- A hand-drawn swoosh that does not match a geometric wordmark, or a logo squashed into a tile. CLAIM(wDBQ-z5-Hqc@02:13), CLAIM(wDBQ-z5-Hqc@09:13)
- Share icons with mismatched stroke and no shared frame. CLAIM(q1QKE9XOS0M@02:16)
