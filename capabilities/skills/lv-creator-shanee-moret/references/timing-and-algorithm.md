# Timing and algorithm (Shanee Moret)

Cadence, launch windows, live-event timing, the invite-quota ledger, and her algorithm claims. Opening caveat: she publishes no golden-hour doctrine. There is no first-60-to-90-minutes routine anywhere in these 66 videos; the nearest things she teaches are launch windows, event timing and fast human replies. Every algorithm statement below is her reading of the platform, unverified. Numbers are creator claims.

## Cadence

- The 90-day phased start: about 30 days of video (a short most days or at least 3 videos a week), then add a weekly newsletter for 30 days, then add lives every other week or weekly. No phase starts before the last one holds; fail a phase and restart it [ajCBOx3C-oI, 5Y_tekIdDmI, t5xc4-NE_OE, RSyNiEh-EPU].
- Weekly is the newsletter floor; 90 days of weekly editions (12-plus) before moving a newsletter to a company page; about 6 editions is her evaluation point for whether the format works [PJqb2j7d9qQ, vNNVBp_mM0g].
- Lives: one weekly until presenting is effortless, then two (one scheduled, one pop-up); solo for the first 8 lives, and 4 lives with no inbound means fix the messaging [RSyNiEh-EPU, rI4ZkkauCcU, qSt2LtOCNy8].
- Her 2026 cadence: about 10 posts a week, 8 of 10 on the category, at least 5 of 10 as video (creator claim) [bCRYL6aL3ec].
- Momentum rule from her own growth story: never slow down during a spike; when others pause, double down; her doubling moments moved her from about 800K toward 1M followers (creator claims) [i05h7QYsQIc].
- Consistency math she cites (rule of 70 framing): daily posting reaches roughly 70 touches in 70 days, twice daily in about 30, twice weekly takes 6 months or more (creator claim) [xwCLAdZTJgE].

## The windows she does teach

- Newsletter launch window: the invite reaches followers and the initial subscriber base forms in the first 48 to 72 hours; she cites 10 to 20 percent of followers subscribing at launch, up to 50 percent for a very warm audience (creator claims) [wWrbyr6_9-s, 1kqCOtDeLRA, JwnyrcJAdaQ, BDW169gFDy0].
- Event replay window: judge the live plus about 72 hours of replay; acceptances on invites also resolve over roughly 72 hours [JRGl2gwloi4, RDEugFeexyI].
- An invitation-style edition can be deleted after its job; she waits about an hour before taking one down [X8Mz2tpvN-8].
- Fast human replies (3 to 11 minutes) are an engagement tactic in her live demos, not an algorithm claim [aCUbwMrQ8GA].
- Trend videos post immediately because the feature cannot be scheduled [cY6L5HMglEQ].

## Live-event timing

- Promote an event 2 to 3 weeks out: weekly invite batches with the last batch at least 7 days before, run-up content on the event topic daily in the final week plus the morning of, and reminder emails at about 7, 3 and 1 days out plus a next-day replay link (creator claim) [g6zmV-Y90rs, 62b9R_HMLAY, Cv7QGjxA974].
- Live slots are tested, not universal: her audience attends mornings and not after 11 (her rule of thumb), others report 3 p.m. or 8 p.m.; a weekly webinar in a 2 to 3 p.m. midweek slot drew 60 to 65 registrations for one user (creator claims) [NtPWMHQfdq8, qSt2LtOCNy8, 1E75Q3jF7w4].
- No universal best newsletter weekday; test against your own audience. A Monday miss can work Wednesday [yeV4VseTmPQ, qSt2LtOCNy8].
- Never time-stamp a live greeting (replays and time zones break it), and do not be late to your own live [WbWSMLOeGW0, XSIlE64OTek].
- Monthly rhythm for events: one to two lives a month for most client situations, each planned four weeks out when using the full invite budget [ZohBpiAEvus, Ec4xbXY8sjs, bCRYL6aL3ec].

## Algorithm claims (all her reading, unverified)

- Newsletter delivery does not depend on the feed: subscribers get every edition by email and notification, which is why she calls it the algorithm-independent channel [BDW169gFDy0, 93Wgv8WAHw4, pSncKW0JBFs, ZohBpiAEvus].
- Articles and newsletters index on Google and Bing and rank on LinkedIn's domain authority, in days to about a month even for small lists; a text-plus-video post can surface in Google's AI mode the same day (2026 claim) [3NjgRJK7Yw8, 8mwU4HPHgHM, 1kqCOtDeLRA, 93Wgv8WAHw4, bCRYL6aL3ec].
- Feed reach is small relative to the network (she frames hypothetical see rates of 1 to 10 percent), and likes hide real attention from quiet senior readers [RDEugFeexyI, RSyNiEh-EPU, pSncKW0JBFs].
- No shorts means no video-feed presence and no search suggestion; the video tab distributes beyond your network even for accounts outside the beta, and tab stats differ from feed stats [toTLWp4aOtI, YggvR0eUj6E, 0cQdGNRvatM].
- The trends surface hands distribution by placement rather than engagement ratio: a clip with 50 to 100 likes can reach hundreds of thousands of impressions (creator claim) [cY6L5HMglEQ].
- Poll participation trains the feed to show you first to that voter; non-follower profile visitors get an automatic newsletter subscribe prompt; embedded video inside a newsletter keeps distribution while an external video link on the feed is penalized (all creator claims, unverified) [62b9R_HMLAY, vNNVBp_mM0g, RSyNiEh-EPU, JwnyrcJAdaQ, yeV4VseTmPQ].
- Reach follows resonance: she rejects algorithm-trick thinking and tells owners to suspect audience fit or offer when reach disappoints; never complain about the algorithm in public [sqv-zZLDGAA, xwCLAdZTJgE].
