# Job-search profile and activity lane

The job lane treats the profile as a recruiter-facing landing page and the activity feed as the proof. Sources: the Lucy Gilmour doctrine (527-video corpus, her claims marked) and the RIG job-search campaign. Everything here still obeys this stage's rule: edits shown as exact before/after values, applied only after Mike approves.

## Profile setup for the search

- Headline serves recruiter search: target role plus domain plus differentiator. Different artifact from the Lane B headline; never averaged [headline-formulas].
- Open-to-Work set deliberately (on or off is a decision, not a default), share-resume-with-hirers ON, the Easy Apply slot holds only the FINAL resume version [rig-job-search-campaign].
- Role-by-role experience descriptions with accomplishments; re-run a resume-to-LinkedIn number match after every change [rig-job-search-campaign].
- Verified badge plus workplace verification: recruiters now actively test for fake, AI-assisted candidates, and be ready to name real managers and references for every listed role [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].
- Watch for fake recruiters who bait-and-switch into paid resume fixes, especially around the public Open-to-Work frame [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].

## Activity doctrine (20-10-5, about 15-20 minutes a day)

- 20 targeted connection requests a day (hiring managers, decision-makers, some niche recruiters, future peers), 10 meaningful comments or likes, 5 personalised messages [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].
- Weekly invite cap about 100, spread across the week; bulk bursts risk account restriction. Conflicts with nothing in Clark's engine: he also cites about 20 a weekday and roughly 100 a week as the ceiling. RIG throttles in linkedin-site-mechanics bind regardless [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity, outbound-and-pipeline].
- Post 2-3 times a week to stay on recruiters' radar: entertaining, valuable, credible content rotated; hook, re-hook, body, CTA. Never "poor me" content, recruiter attacks, or desperation posts; they read as needy to decision-makers [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].
- Commenting: read the whole post, tag the author, add an insight or a respectful addition, end with a question. Skip one-word congratulations. Ring the bell on key people to be early on high-reach posts [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].
- Message the hiring manager, not the recruiter ("boss hunting"): three sentences or fewer, opened on their need, one proof point, no "am I a fit". Voice notes (mobile, first-degree only) get more replies than templated text (Lucy's claim) [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].
- Be first to apply: search title, filter by date posted, then edit the time value in the URL (seconds: 86,400 = 24h, 3,600 = 1h). Apply only where the hard requirements are met; soft skills do not count toward "80%". Vague mega-postings get direct outreach instead of a lone application [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].
- Track every application with follow-up dates; follow up on the ones Mike really wants [lucy-skills/linkedin-activity].

## Campaign gates (rig-job-search-campaign)

- Human-pace LinkedIn editing: one tab, identity guard first, notify-network switch off on every edit, type 12-30 ms per character, idle gaps between saves, at most 7 saves per session, reload and read back after each save. Hard stop on any login redirect, checkpoint, CAPTCHA, or unusual-activity warning: report, do not retry [rig-job-search-campaign].
- Human approval before every outward batch (applications, messages, asks) with the exact text shown first [rig-job-search-campaign].
- Truth rules from the fact base: unknown stays unknown, no invented numbers, never criticize a former employer [rig-job-search-campaign].
- Never publish address, birthday, or compensation outward; secrets by keychain item name only [rig-job-search-campaign].
- A frozen platform stays frozen [rig-job-search-campaign].
