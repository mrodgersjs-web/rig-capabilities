# rig-revenue-meta — worldview, contracts, router

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

Read this before any rig-revenue-* work. The contracts below govern every downstream skill and
agent. When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

## The worldview in one sentence

Sell governed, human-reviewed, E-E-A-T-grounded answer/content systems to regulated
professional-service firms — and make the **human approval gate the product**, not overhead to
automate away. The source corpus proves the demand for AI content engines and leaks the compliance
gap in the same breath; a governed operator charges for the gate.

## Load-bearing decision rules

1. Human approval before publish, always. No unattended auto-publishing. Drafts-first is the
   product feature ("checks and balances"), not a limitation.
2. Non-commodity or nothing. Every article must carry forced proprietary variables (own data,
   tests, first-hand cases) or it does not ship. See rig-revenue-forced-variables.
3. Money over traffic. Target bottom-of-funnel buyer intent ("where", "how much", "book", "hire"),
   never DIY intent ("how") for service firms. See rig-revenue-buyer-intent-screen.
4. Retrieval readiness gates everything: crawlable → indexed → ranked → cited. Don't block AI
   crawlers if AI citations are the goal.
5. Answer published, question matched. Prices and direct answers live on the page: if pricing is
   not published, an AI answer has nothing to reference and sends buyers elsewhere.
6. Consistency beats spikes. Predictable cadence; publish ramp only (rig-revenue-publish-ramp).
   Never a velocity spike.
7. E-E-A-T is load-bearing for YMYL (law, CPA, clinic, finance, health): named, verified author
   credentials and review by the licensed professional are deliverables, not decoration.
8. Evidence or invisibility. Claims ship with data and sources or they do not ship.
9. Skills and agents over prompts; adversarial review over first drafts ("AI natively wants to give
   us a rosy, shiny output"); independent evaluator over self-approval (rig-revenue-goals-protocol,
   rig-revenue-seo-council).
10. Clean authority only. Earned links, digital PR, real citations. Never bought links, never fake
    profile data, never content published under a protected professional title without verified
    credentials (see Escalations).

## Claim-hygiene contract (MANDATORY)

- Every outcome figure derived from the source corpus is `CLAIM(<videoId>)`. Never present a
  creator claim as verified fact. Evidence that something is being sold is not evidence it works.
- The source channel's revenue claims (e.g. "$10k/mo", "$417,000/mo" titles) are **unverified
  creator marketing**. They calibrate a ladder; they never justify a client promise.
- Never promise rankings or timelines. Anyone guaranteeing a rank is selling.
- Market rate cards for local-SEO builds, AEO/AI-citation work, close rates, and
  qualified-conversation rates are `[AWAITING]` external research. Until they land, price from
  RIG's own cost base and scope, not from corpus figures.
- Client-facing deliverables cite sources for every factual claim. Attribution is a deliverable.

## Affiliate-disclosure contract (MANDATORY)

The source channel monetises its tool recommendations. Any tool recommendation derived from this
corpus is a **PAID ENDORSEMENT** and must say so wherever it appears — in skills, agents, client
deliverables, and internal notes:

| Arvow / Arvo ("Arval", "Arville", "Arvin" are ASR variants of the same product) | PAID ENDORSEMENT — source channel affiliate (`Arvow.com/tim`, code `Tim10`) |
| Blotato | PAID ENDORSEMENT — source channel affiliate (`blotato.com/?ref=ytseo`) |
| Journalist AI (`tryjournalist.com` / `journalist.cafe`) | PAID ENDORSEMENT — source channel affiliate/partner; note the undisclosed dual role (vendor speaks in the maker's own voice, "we only build stuff you guys request") and the discount stated as both 30% and 10% |

Do not launder a paid endorsement into neutral advice. RIG tool choices are made on evidence and
client fit, never on corpus enthusiasm.

## Contradiction decision points (decide explicitly; never smooth over)

1. Automation without gates vs "checks and balances" mandate → RIG decision: the gate is mandatory
   and is billed as compliance. (D8tVa_kalbg vs 351/371/377)
2. AI-detection evasion claims vs the channel's own rater evidence → RIG decision: never sell
   undetectability; sell reviewable, attributed content. (ntISy-GN7Gk)
3. Link buying recommended vs "clean backlink profiles were doing better" → RIG decision: earned
   authority only. (353 vs CJzbrZYiEio)
4. "Write for humans" vs keyword-stuffing/SERP-flood tactics → RIG decision: buyer-intent content
   with natural keyword placement only.
5. Embed-credit theory vs "showcase their content as your own" → RIG decision: embedding is not a
   license; rights handling is required before any third-party asset ships. (part-254-297)
6. Journalist AI discount stated as 30% and 10% → carry both figures as-is with the contradiction
   named; never quote a single "the" discount.
7. Consistency doctrine vs daily-cadence rules ("consistency does not mean posting daily") → RIG
   decision: fixed sustainable cadence, ramp only, no spikes. (-UkHh5kbKEQ vs 315, 346)
8. Schema treated as critical vs one study finding zero AI-citation impact → RIG decision: ship
   correct schema (cheap, machine-readable) but never promise it as a citation driver. (jtS3f1R-Ubo
   vs x288PvvQ4zk)

## Step 1. Classify the goal

Match the request to a Routing table or Additional child routes row. If two rows apply, name both and run the cheaper read
first (teardown/report before build). If none applies, say so and name the missing question — a
router that fabricates a lane is the failure mode.

## Step 2. Enforce the contracts

Before dispatch: confirm the claim-hygiene contract will apply to any figure used, that every
named affiliate tool will carry its PAID ENDORSEMENT label, and that no excluded tactic is in
scope. An excluded tactic in scope = stop and refuse that part explicitly.

## Step 3. Dispatch and gate

rig-revenue-publish-gate (human approval) — this skill routes; it never approves its own output.
