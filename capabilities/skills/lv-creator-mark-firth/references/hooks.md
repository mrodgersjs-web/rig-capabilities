# Hooks

Sub-skill of `lv-creator-mark-firth`. Synthesis in RIG's words. Bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims, not verified facts.

## 1. What the first line is for

He treats the opening as the only line most people read, then the rest as optional. A 2024 readout of posts he says he analyzed (30,000, creator claim) set the first line at 5 to 8 words, ending on a colon or an ellipsis, with a number and a desire word. [gTkfDjUBXMY, -0NvIx7Kjfo]

That formula is not the current rule. The date ledger below is the rule. A March 2026 re-upload still teaches the 2024 formula. Do not treat that re-upload as a 2026 discovery. [PVX6FHGp4Uc]

## 3. Hook families he actually uses

- Buyer-problem line. The opening names a pain the buyer already feels this week, not an abstract theme. [FEOAeCCK9e0, T7G4_owju2A]
- Transformation line. A personal result, optionally with something the reader does not have to give up. Slot only. Do not copy his examples. [uEHPPU2k_OM, HMHGfcaMFlg, eubB4zi3HG4]
- Sophistication match. Newer buyers get cost or income framing. Experienced buyers get time saved or labor replaced. He claims the mismatch is why small accounts stay small. [ArYphaJtvmc, 8BidcQ5viLs]
- News plus a second-order effect, then a question aimed at the buyer. He moved this to comments on news items in January 2026, and to posts that ride a live story. [MJFCR1EJdrw, MourIQGOHQ8, r2LsebGinxs]
- Tool-list line tied to the market's wanted outcome, not a generic resource dump. [0WBbXeM5pJM, 2X4XQLvSNWg]
- Values or common-enemy line for the over-40 market he writes to. One enemy, repeated for years, not a new villain each week. [k7xn5Rn6_Sw, iubQmgPG3ZM, r2LsebGinxs]
- Belief-change line. He says write the realization first, then the opening is whatever makes that realization feel inevitable. [T7G4_owju2A]

He dropped, for cold audiences, the negative "X is dead" opening (2026-01-27) and divisive-figure bait that pulls non-buyers (2026-04-13). [T7G4_owju2A, iubQmgPG3ZM]

## 4. Slot skeletons (RIG-written; fill every slot with a sourced receipt)

- "[Buyer role] keep losing [specific hour or dollar] to [named workflow]. The turn:"
- "How I [sourced outcome] without dropping [constraint the buyer wants to keep]."
- "[Live event] changes [second-order effect] for [buyer]. Worth a look, or noise?"
- "These [count] tools cut [named task] for [market]. Which one fits [their constraint]?"
- "The quiet belief blocking [buyer] is [belief]. Here is the moment it broke."
- "I help [narrow market] get off [Monday pain] so [one outcome]."

## 5. Testing

He does not teach a formal three-variant lab. What he does teach:

- Reuse a proven template three to five times, then retire it. That was 2024 advice. By April 2025 he said templates lose to stories. By January 2026 advice posts lose to belief-change posts. Do not keep remixing a 2024 template as if the later videos had not landed. [N2AOzgp-SIk, -0NvIx7Kjfo, T7G4_owju2A]
- Remix a proven topic across formats rather than hunting a new topic every day. He cites 10 pieces times 10 formats (creator claim). The topic must already have produced DMs or matched profile visits, not just likes. [uEHPPU2k_OM, HMHGfcaMFlg, FEOAeCCK9e0]
- Model the shape of an outlier from another platform, then supply an original news angle. He says news angles stay original even when the format is borrowed. [OsIOAwGhWL4, MourIQGOHQ8]
- Headline and first line are the same job on the profile: the first 8 to 10 words are what he claims people read before they accept. [wplSBnjBxt0]

A 2026-02-04 note: some question words he associates with machine-written closers get suppressed (creator claim, unverified). He does not list a stable banned-word set in the extraction. Do not invent one. Prefer a question that only this post could ask. [0WBbXeM5pJM]
