# DM copy and openers

What a message is for and what each line is made of. Drafting is unattended; every send is a
batch-level Gate-D approval naming recipients and the exact text.

## Pick the model first, then the recipe

| model | theory of change | preconditions | volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: fast triage | 3-touch broadcast; ABCD letter routes the reply | list 100+; offer in one line; bandwidth-constrained | 25 DMs/day ceiling |
| B: trust thread | one long conversation; booking after self-identification | list up to 200; deal funds 30 min/prospect research | 10-25 DMs/day |
| C: chase override | permission-led video + 9-step pattern-interrupt chase | explicit permission to pitch was granted; contained campaign | gated, never default |

Default for Lane B: Model A. Promote to Model B when the list is small and the deal is large
(below about $5k ACV the research math does not work). Mixing recipes across models is the
failure behind reply-rate plateaus [opener-recipes].

## Model shapes

- **Model A opener**: one short message, no link, one specific observation, the offer in the
  body, ABCD close at the end. Never expand it to a 5-message thread.
- **Model B opener** (5 separate messages, each followed by Send, in this order): spontaneity
  opener; restrained compliment plus a rephrase of what they do (4-6/10 enthusiasm); one
  authority-building observation (a single named peer, not a list); one ambiguous or permission
  question; a personal connection. Do not reorder. On reply: stop the script and switch to the
  probe ladder.
- **Model C opener** is not text: a voice note or video of at most 60 seconds asking permission
  to pitch ("just send a thumbs up"). On the thumbs up: a native VSL up to 1:59, selfie quality,
  transparency then offer then social proof then mechanism then the same permission close. The
  9 follow-ups are pattern interrupts (gifts, memes), never "bumping this up". The claimed 80%
  of bookings landing on follow-ups 7-9 is a creator claim [opener-recipes].

## Line-level rules

- First message short (a long first DM is a smell): under about 300 characters.
- End on a question, a permission CTA, or a closed framing. One question per DM; binary,
  clarifying or permission questions beat open-ended, multiple-choice and compound.
- No links in a cold DM: no calendly, no loom, no PDF. The link comes after the reply.
- You:us density at least 1.0; if "I/we/our/my" outnumbers "you/your", delete and restart.
- Specificity over warmth: a named number or artefact proves the read ("2019 case study, 37
  million generated, is solid" beats "great work").
- Mirror their pace, echo their language, hold tone stable; authority by one named peer, never a
  CV dump.
- Banned openers: "I came across your profile", "I hope this finds you well", "I would love to
  connect", "explore synergies", "we'd love to help each other", "quick chat / just 10 minutes"
  before relevance exists, unfilled merge tags, any feature dump without a named observation
  [dm-copy-craft].
- DM style standard from the Dykema campaign, first-class: 3-4 sentences max, under 70 words,
  informal like texting a colleague you respect, positive energy, no jargon ("AI ops layer",
  "MCP", "synergy" are out), a research-backed hook referencing something they actually said,
  and one clear ask [dykema-dm-campaign-learnings].

## The ABCD close

Reply with one letter so the answer costs nothing. Three variants exist and their letters mean
different things (B and C swap between them), so pick one letter set per sequence; `abcd_close`
refuses a mix. The profane D is Mike's choice, not the default; what must survive: one-letter
replies, every letter mapped to a triage route, and the last option genuinely easy to pick. ABCD
is the close, not the opener: a menu on a generic pitch is still a generic pitch [abcd-method].

## Voice notes and videos

Voice note: hard ceiling about 15 seconds (45 words is the proxy), casual, with a line on why you
are sending it, and it ends by asking permission to send a video ("is it okay if I send a quick
video explaining what I mean?"). The opt-in is qualification and it caps the video work at 4-5 a
day instead of 20. For formal or regulated lists, default to text. The permission video opens on
them (their profile or site in frame), 30-60 seconds for the accept-triggered variant, 10-15
minutes only for people who opted in. Scripts are generated; audio is recorded by Mike. A
synthetic voice to a cold contact is deception about authorship and is refused
[voice-video-dms, sourcing-channels].

## The drafting contract

Every draft lands staged and stamped UNSENT with its evidence refs and measured character count;
the second agent reviews hook specificity, length, tone and ask clarity before the batch reaches
Mike, and never skips that review [dm-scribe, dykema-dm-campaign-learnings]. Three specific
observations of this person, or the draft is not written yet. Never creator-verbatim scripts:
adapt the structure, write the words.
