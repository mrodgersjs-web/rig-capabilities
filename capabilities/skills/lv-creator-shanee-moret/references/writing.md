# Writing and formats (Shanee Moret)

Body craft, post and newsletter structure, CTAs and voice. Synthesis in RIG's words; numbers are creator claims; every claim carries a video ID.

## The four-word spine: one goal, one person, one CTA

Everything she writes starts from one 90-day business goal, one reader (drawn from your top-20% paying clients: what they bought first, why, how they found you), and one destination the reader is sent to every time. Never rotate between five choices; a single unmistakable CTA is what separates her from the 9 in 10 profiles she says carry a mismatched offer [6f3ZzY5-gUg, pSncKW0JBFs, DitOkxbdSvo, bm6_uMvLagQ, sqv-zZLDGAA].

- Sell the first problem the buyer has, in their words, not the whole service menu; she reports that buyers almost always arrive asking for the first-step problem, so that is what gets marketed [DdJYKsAfWqE].
- Write only to the trigger moment (just promoted, just given a mandate, just hit a problem), not to the topic at large [6f3ZzY5-gUg, bm6_uMvLagQ].
- The CTA must continue the logic of the content it sits in: a soft link or a keyword instruction that extends the same argument, never a mismatched offer bolted on [v3TS92mls0k, WbWSMLOeGW0, YggvR0eUj6E].
- High-ticket buyers want a call, not a checklist download; a lead magnet misfits them, and if the CTA is a consultation, state what the 20 minutes delivers [bm6_uMvLagQ, DitOkxbdSvo].
- A standalone post that explains the offer, who you serve and what results look like is a deliberate format, not filler [DitOkxbdSvo].

## Post body craft

- Mirror the video in the post text: the written post carries the video's substance for readers who never press play, spaced for phone reading [EJ0QFdEXjqA, YggvR0eUj6E].
- First three lines carry the opening; white space between lines; reading level kept simple (she names the Hemingway app) [EJ0QFdEXjqA, YggvR0eUj6E].
- Hashtags: 3 to 5 maximum in her 2024 video, with no hashtag doctrine after that [EJ0QFdEXjqA].
- Line breaks, one question at the end, and no reading from a script on camera [8mwU4HPHgHM].
- Emojis only if they match your voice and stay consistent [EJ0QFdEXjqA].
- She teaches giving the playbook away in full: buyers pay for accountability, situation-specific judgment and time saved, and gatekeeping hands the opening to a competitor [NtPWMHQfdq8, yeV4VseTmPQ, xwCLAdZTJgE].
- Voice rule from her 2023-2024 arc: authenticity beat the scripted norm, delivery and emotion beat polish, and never complain about the algorithm in a post [xwCLAdZTJgE, 4kkzQTa9Y-c].
- Quality shift, dated 2025-05-06: consistency alone now earns nothing; each piece must clear a quality bar because AI personas and rival founders crowd the feed [bm6_uMvLagQ, 4iMPDAj3Mxo].

## Post types and their job

- Feed posts grow reach and followers (text, polls, images, carousels, shorts); newsletters and lives carry revenue. She splits the jobs explicitly [qSt2LtOCNy8, ZohBpiAEvus].
- Text and image posts are the crowded lane; video is the less crowded one because AI fills text, and by 2025 short video also sits in search [ajCBOx3C-oI, 0cQdGNRvatM, toTLWp4aOtI].
- Education beats entertainment when the goal is clients; beginner advice attracts non-buyers [v3TS92mls0k, 0cQdGNRvatM].
- Articles on your own site do not prove you are real the way visible video does; she advises against leading with long articles [t5xc4-NE_OE].
- Carousels appear only as a named feed format; she never teaches carousel craft, so do not attribute a carousel system to her [qSt2LtOCNy8, vNNVBp_mM0g].
- Company pages speak for the company: republish a personal newsletter there with rewritten, depersonalized commentary via the reshare-with-thoughts path, never a bare repost [zuCsE5hnCo4].
- One long training a month (around 25 minutes) can feed a whole content calendar when multiplied properly [62b9R_HMLAY].

## Newsletter writing mechanics

- The first article is the invitation, not an edition: it does not email subscribers. The second publication is the first real edition [PJqb2j7d9qQ, 1kqCOtDeLRA].
- Launch article: why now, what it is, what to expect, closing call to action written as a full sentence carrying the link; the one-line description field truncates, so write it short [1kqCOtDeLRA, wWrbyr6_9-s].
- Place the CTA in the first half of the issue; she reports about 1,300 characters show in the email preview (creator claim) [yeV4VseTmPQ].
- The title is the email subject line and a permanent filter; write for one reader (a specific CEO, not a generic audience) and do not copy famous newsletters written for a different market [93Wgv8WAHw4, pSncKW0JBFs].
- Post-launch plan: keep 4 editions prepared (roughly 2 educational deep dives answering what your best clients actually ask, 1 case study with results visuals, 1 email-style invitation to a live), publish weekly as the floor, and read analytics monthly [JwnyrcJAdaQ, PJqb2j7d9qQ, vNNVBp_mM0g].
- Two content kinds: enduring educational or inspiring pieces that stay up, and temporary event-promotion issues you can delete after the event; an SEO-oriented guide is written to rank and left up [yeV4VseTmPQ, 3NjgRJK7Yw8, X8Mz2tpvN-8].
- Rework editions into blog posts and lead assets with real rewriting, never copy-paste; embed video in educational editions [pSncKW0JBFs, JwnyrcJAdaQ].
- An abandoned newsletter (2 to 3 editions a year) signals unreliability; a run of about 12 weekly editions with zero conversions is a messaging problem to fix [pSncKW0JBFs, JwnyrcJAdaQ].
- Two competing audiences get two newsletters (up to 5 per member after the platform change she covered), launched weeks apart, each named for its segment [vNNVBp_mM0g, mrq3HvgmdcM, YntxonmZyyI].

## Length

Shorts run about 45 to 90 seconds, up to 2 minutes works; a cleaned-up live is roughly 15 to 20 minutes; newsletter editions carry an embedded video or a case study and read like a useful email [YggvR0eUj6E, rI4ZkkauCcU, JwnyrcJAdaQ]. Long trainings lose busy high-ticket buyers; calls do not build lists [1E75Q3jF7w4].

## Dated shifts

- Daily-video-or-nothing framing from March and May 2024 [ajCBOx3C-oI, t5xc4-NE_OE] relaxes by Feb 2025 into a weekly or monthly live plus consistent video [toTLWp4aOtI], and her 2026 rule is about 10 posts a week with most on the category [bCRYL6aL3ec].
- Her 2018 cold-pitch writing style is explicitly not what she advises now [i05h7QYsQIc].
- 2026 adds a machine-readable layer: text paired with a 2-minute in-depth video can surface in AI search the same day, so category naming in the copy matters to agents too (creator claim) [bCRYL6aL3ec].
