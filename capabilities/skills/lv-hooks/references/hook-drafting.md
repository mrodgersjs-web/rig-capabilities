# Hook drafting: three variants, a cold reader, invisible levers

## The three-variant rule (non-negotiable)

Draft three versions of every hook and merge the best parts. The three must differ in shape, not in
synonyms: one number-led or scene-led, one narrative, one contrast or negative claim. A hook set of
three reworded copies of one idea is one hook pretending to be three. [clark:hooks-and-media
TH63A9m1VNA, z7YQx8Qj2f0, 2XeAckxuAKM]

Write the hook AFTER the body exists or is planned, then ask "what is the biggest benefit this
reader gets?" That answer is the post's most persuasive part and it belongs in the opening.
[creator: Jay Cartere, smZXGXvrA1M]

## The cold-reader rule

Assume the reader has never heard of Mike. A stranger must understand the opening with no context:
callbacks, inside references, and brand vocabulary go in the body, never in line one. The opening
must answer, quickly, what this is about and what continuing gets them. Clear beats clever: making a
reader think harder makes them leave. [clark:hooks-and-media 2Yv8osegsKY, Y8ewcq03zxc; creator:
Jay Cartere, smZXGXvrA1M]

## The levers stay invisible

Stack two or three levers (four maximum) and keep them under a natural, conversational voice. A
recognisable formula opening now reads as machine output, so the formula-style generators (two fixed
lines, a fixed character budget, a required first-person statement) are raw material for angles and
nothing more: never lift their phrasing into a post. Prefer a fuller opening that uses the visible
lines over choppy fragments. Never hedge; a weak hook is worse than no hook. [hook-generator
superseded by clark:hooks-and-media SL8yfxUVFKE, gZwWBtiZ7qE, sDuWUBoHsRI]

## The five drafting rules (from the hook lab)

1. Speak to one direct person and call them out by role and situation. Generic is the named failure
   mode on this account: a hook with no identifiable single reader is self-dropped, not passed up.
2. Open an engagement loop the reader can answer.
3. Promise value without spending it. Hint at the reward; do not spoil it.
4. Match the hook to the post. A bold claim must be paid by proof in the body, or it is flagged
   `needs_body_proof` rather than shipped.
5. Write like Mike talks. The feed rewards a casual register, and the fix for machine-sounding copy
   is more context about Mike, not better prompting.

## The ten-type lab (when a spread is needed)

Generate one variant per named type: contrarian, curiosity, stat-led, question, results-first,
vulnerable, admission, authority-steal, personal-transformation, future-shock. This is the spread the
downstream critic and the hook-floor gate consume; the consumer's gate sets the required count (the
linked pipeline code enforces ten per plan slot). Ship the enforced count or return `blocked`; never
pad the floor with uncited variants, and say which counts disagree when they do. Each variant carries
a claim reference when it carries a number, and the first two lines stay short: roughly twelve words
before "see more" hides the rest. [hook-lab, draft-hooks]

## Two-line mechanics

- Budget the opening like a title and thumbnail: roughly 100-300 characters before truncation decide
  whether the rest is read. The sole objective of the first lines is the see-more click.
  [clark:hooks-and-media P_5mJNksHiE; hook-lab]
- One hook line plus one rehook. The rehook points at one item's payoff without spending it (for
  example, naming that one step in the list is the expensive one). [hook-selection]
- No question-only hook with no stake. No announcement-class openings ("thrilled to share"). No
  hashtags in line one. No em dashes. [hook-lab]
- Digits over spelled numbers. Every word earns its place. [hook-generator]

## Construction patterns when stuck

- Context lean (topic clarity plus common ground), one scroll-stop interjection ("but", "however"),
  then a contrarian snapback that reverses the lean. This is a construction pattern usable inside any
  family. [creator: Kallaway, LmXpbP7dD48]
- Six-part decomposition of a strong hook: subject clarity, action, objective or end-state, contrast;
  optionally a proof word and a time constraint. Education content needs the proof word more than
  entertainment does. [creator: Kallaway, S9FlxFv9dxg]
- Run five pattern-interrupting alternatives and keep the parts that make you feel something.
  [creator: Will McTighe, TTH-75XOgJE]

## Numbers hygiene

Never publish a placeholder number or an invented client. A number retold from Mike's own earlier
post is not a source: a published post is an editable artifact and he has corrected his own figures
mid-session. Take numbers from a fresh read, tag them `[ours]` with the read time, and use the four
evidence classes the pipeline gate accepts: primary, secondary, creator, ours. A creator-reported
number is context for us, never content for the post. [draft-hooks, hook-selection]
