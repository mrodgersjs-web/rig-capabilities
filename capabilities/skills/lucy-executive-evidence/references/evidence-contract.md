# Evidence contract

This is the package's claim-handling contract, not Lucy's quoted doctrine.

## Separate two kinds of status

**Claim status** describes what a source supports:

| Status | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed by user/source | A specific statement is supported by an identified source or explicit confirmation of the event | May be used within that source's exact scope |
| Candidate-reported | A resume, interview, or personal statement reports the claim | May be presented as supported career history; retain provenance and do not call it independently audited |
| Independently verified | Separate evidence corroborates the particular claim | Name that evidence and what it verifies; do not transfer the label to unrelated claims |
| Proposed interview approach | An intended action, plan, or hypothetical spoken example | First-person future/conditional language; never completed career history |
| Ideal-achievement working pattern | A polished resume achievement selected to meet the JD, pending Mike's real example | Explicit working-draft label and bracketed factual fields; past-tense resume syntax is allowed, application-ready use is not |
| Unresolved or conflicting | Necessary facts are missing or sources disagree | Ask the narrow fact question; hold the disputed clause |

“Confirmed by user” and “candidate-reported” can both apply. Confirmation of a number's wording does not prove an independent audit. An existing AI answer bank is a derivative reference, not independent confirmation of its contents.

**Requirement status** describes fit:

- Direct evidence: the source supports the requested experience or capability.
- Adjacent evidence: transferable experience supports an honest bridge; the target-industry experience remains unestablished.
- Proposed approach only: the candidate can discuss how they would proceed, with no established past example.
- Gap or unresolved: a material requirement is unestablished or contradicted.

## Small evidence ledger

Use one row per material claim. Add detail only where it affects meaning.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Evidence ID | Stable local ID, such as E-01 |
| Source | File/URL and exact section, row, or source statement; date/version if relevant |
| Claim and status | Supported proposition and status from above |
| Employer and role | Actual employer and supported title; do not rename employment to match a JD |
| Dates and duration | Source dates and derived duration only where established; otherwise unknown |
| Outcome | Achieved result versus target; metric, unit, baseline, period, and scope where known |
| Personal action | What Mike personally did; distinguish team result and contribution |
| Mechanism | Supported explanation connecting the action to the result; no invented causal link |
| Limits | Unknown period, disputed number, adjacent industry, confidential detail, or other constraint |
| Uses | Requirement IDs, answer IDs, or resume bullet IDs |

Keep employment duration separate from the time it took to achieve a result. Do not infer one from the other. If source dates conflict, retain both pointers and hold a precise duration claim.

## Meaning checks

- Cost reduction is not revenue growth. Inventory stock reduction is not recurring annual savings. A business's size is not its purchase price. A prototype is not a launched or scaled company.
- Do not merge figures, employers, or separate initiatives into a new causal story. A correct number can become false when attached to the wrong employer, period, unit, ownership, or mechanism.
- Do not retrofit AI, automation, direct retail leadership, merchandising ownership, or a client identity into a historical result.
- Use the JD's exact terminology only when the real evidence supports the same meaning. “Inventory planning” does not establish “grocery category ownership.”
- A known mechanism can support a concise causal claim. When only context and result are documented, keep them separate and ask what Mike changed personally.
- Hypothetical interview prose stays proposed after phrasing approval. It may inspire a labeled resume working pattern with bracketed fields. Promote it into history only after explicit event confirmation with employer, action, timeframe, and outcome evidence.
- Preserve truthful qualitative achievements when no metric is available. Do not invent a number to satisfy a formula.

Historical claims may proceed with a recorded unknown period if the wording does not imply one. Missing evidence that changes the claim's meaning requires a hold or a less specific version for application-ready use. Working drafts intentionally preserve bracketed fields so Mike can supply evidence after seeing the strongest achievement pattern. No pre-draft questionnaire is required. Keep unresolved placeholders out of the application-ready version.
