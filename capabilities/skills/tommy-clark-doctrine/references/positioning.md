# Positioning and content-market fit

Sub-skill of `tommy-clark-doctrine`. Synthesis; bracketed IDs are the source videos (see `source-index.md`). Numbers are creator claims.

## 1. The golden question

Before writing anything, answer one sentence: why should a specific member of my ICP follow me? Everything posted should sit inside that answer. Scattered topics are his most common diagnosis for accounts that stall. [w2_zVhExo1s, tay_EcbOi7E, _YPmpy4fVGw, -WGCvECeTcU, QKNc515DSro]

- Pair it with 1-3 contrarian beliefs about the industry and defend them repeatedly; they become the messaging pillars. [mTXBTznvNZI, _YPmpy4fVGw]
- Aim to be the go-to resource on one topic rooted in real experience (he credits that framing to Dave Gerhardt). [UKEUZHJUOCM, 5lcZl4iEjI0]
- 2026 refinement: if the goal is pipeline, the niche is a topic next to your product and buyer, not your personality. [s6KMjhznXqs]

## 2. Content-market fit

A topic earns a slot only where three circles overlap: the buyer cares about it, it sits next to what you sell, and you have credibility on it. [E5vxr1IZCpI, mTXBTznvNZI, vcFh5-fynHc, 1je0kCMMstg]

- Credibility multiplies quality. The same post lands worse from someone with no record. Without the record, either document what you are learning instead of prescribing, or put a credible teammate forward. [omkQ61UazZ0, L--kLyKyqa0, LVDaPxHbjwQ]
- His most retold failure: a client grew a large audience of people who would never buy his software; the fix was changing the product to fit the audience. Never grow an audience you cannot serve. [RpOt0kEWAFc, HFCfw6gznRM, 2XeAckxuAKM]

## 3. Choose the game

Decide what the account is for (leads, recruiting, fundraising, awareness) and weight the pillars accordingly: lead generation leans on sharp middle-of-funnel opinions, fundraising on momentum stories, recruiting on culture and hiring posts. [HFCfw6gznRM, vcFh5-fynHc, ftBbMDb91ZA]

- Customers over clout. A smaller audience of buyers beats a large generic one; he claims around 5,000 engaged followers can carry a seven-figure business, and prefers 10,000 ICP followers to 100,000 generic ones. [XCT5Mubw4AI, Y8ewcq03zxc, vdT9a62LrqA, 1qzSPOPlmuQ]
- Only a small share of buyers are shopping at any moment (his 95/5 framing), so most posts educate the rest. [-WGCvECeTcU]

## 4. One post, one person

Picture one specific buyer at their desk (a BuzzFeed habit he credits via Shaan Puri) and decide whether the post should earn a like, a comment or a save. Most posts must address buyers, not fellow founders or marketers. [szN0DF3Oqik, XCT5Mubw4AI]

## 5. Vocabulary and repetition

- Name your frameworks. Prospects repeat coined terms back on sales calls. [TH63A9m1VNA]
- Repeat the same 1-3 core ideas for years. Audiences remember far less than the author assumes, and repetition is how a person becomes known for one thing. [2Yv8osegsKY, vcIAAUP6b8Y, szN0DF3Oqik, HFCfw6gznRM]

## 6. Building in public, only when the buyer mirrors you

Radical transparency (metrics, legal letters, P&L) pulls customers only when the buyer is a founder or revenue leader. Otherwise it attracts the wrong crowd; his own 2023 test drew agency owners instead of buyers. Share directional momentum, and keep unresolved hard periods off the feed. [lnMjrfaT48I, 2XeAckxuAKM, sDuWUBoHsRI]

- Tell resolved struggles, not live ones ("scars, not open wounds"). [nXY6Tl6-uh0]

## 7. Personality as a moat

One or two personal interests and a relaxed tone prove a human is writing. Keep anecdotes tied to the buyer's world; off-topic personal posts dilute the audience. [N2b0QVoCEPg, UjDZLhuEpnQ, gZwWBtiZ7qE]

## Applying it (RIG)

- The golden question for each lane is Mike's decision. This file supplies the test, not the answer: name the buyer, the outcome, and the receipt that proves Mike can deliver it.
- Use content-market fit as the admission gate for every topic in a RIG calendar: no receipt, no slot.
- Mike's three goals (top influencer, job, clients) compete for one profile. Clark's "choose the game" says an account optimized for one goal underperforms on the others; surface that trade-off instead of blending them silently.
