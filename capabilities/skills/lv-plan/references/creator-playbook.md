# Creator playbook: planning, cadence and the week's slate

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 plus Tommy Clark) teach for this stage: how
often to post, how to shape a week, how long a test runs. Algorithm and volume figures are dated
creator claims, unverified. The cadence spread below is evidence for the band this stage already
sets, never a reason to widen it.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. Three to five posts a week is the working band; daily posting is not required. Clark's current rule
   is three better posts a week (Sept 2026) [s6KMjhznXqs, QKNc515DSro]; Kasey three to four
   [eyfNP4ipgrI]; Lara four with distinct jobs [L7a694gzKG4, 0ZVUVa4IFTw]; Harry two to five, one
   niche [XbDKE82gRf0, kK5H2Uu1L6Y]; Mark three to five (Apr 2025) [-0NvIx7Kjfo]; Michelle two or
   three for small and mid-size businesses [vxmq9UDBs6s].
2. Every slot carries a distinct job, and the week is a mix rather than one format repeated. Lara's
   fixed four teach, story, teach, sell [L7a694gzKG4, JDZYNJQOee0]; Clark's funnel split or the
   barbell slate [K2H2KGTbZ08, s6KMjhznXqs]; Will's reach, story, conversion mix [UJvAtV63vk0];
   Kasey's Monday, Wednesday, Friday job map [2t-EBcS43Lw, B18w0jPdXiI].
3. Judge the motion at 90 days and commit in months, not weeks. Clark judges at 90 days with the
   curve bending around months three to six [mJ1c1PHBoo4, mTXBTznvNZI]; Diandra holds 90 days minimum
   and a six-month horizon, expecting the first 50 posts to be mediocre [w3_109ygioY, R8nrEQx8rEY];
   Mark batches and judges at 90 days [OsIOAwGhWL4]; Pierre sees results land two to three months out
   [vzQrN8BVE9c]; Kasey argues three a week for a year beats daily for two months [eyfNP4ipgrI].
4. Plan about a week ahead and fill slots from real events, not a blank page. Clark warns month-deep
   queues go stale [8nBIAUSIRnQ]; Kasey plans Sunday from the week's real calendar, notes and mistakes
   [B18w0jPdXiI]; Michelle keeps a calendar and repurposes posts from about six months back
   [vxmq9UDBs6s, K0D6kDr-WAY]; Lara's weekly email sits beside the four posts [JDZYNJQOee0].
5. Steady beats bursts; raising volume is the wrong reflex when reach drops. Michelle [8Yrgj-zOYQ8,
   mDsy5N6jgHs], Diandra (consistency beats volume) [w3_109ygioY], Kasey (cutting from six a week to
   three or four raised engagement) [EfHijF0P6bo, TcvoXYSHqe4], Pierre (consistent frequency favored,
   irrelevant volume punished) [6RJb6qYq1gA].
6. Repetition compounds because only a slice of followers sees any one post: Kasey about 10 percent
   [bCxD79Wl6ec, vBxw5q522pY], Lara two to three percent [JDZYNJQOee0], Shanee one to ten percent
   [RDEugFeexyI, pSncKW0JBFs]. All creator claims.

## What nobody teaches

- Nobody gives a validated way to turn cadence into a revenue forecast; the numbers offered are
  impressions, engagement or creator stories.
- No creator teaches planning for a client account under another person's approval, or for a minor's
  lane where nothing auto-schedules.
- Nobody supplies a mid-test stop rule for a cadence experiment; the 90-day judgment is the only
  finish line any of them name.
- Nobody teaches what to do when the supply of real events runs dry; Clark's calendar mining is
  process, not a plan.
- No creator covers how a planned slot's result should reach a measurement tracker; the handoff to
  `linkedin-analytics-tracker` is RIG's own.

## How to apply here

- Build the week per `references/calendar-and-cadence.md`: three to five slots, one job each, no two
  in a day, distinct formats, 4:1 give-to-take. The spread above supports the band; it does not widen
  it.
- Shape the slate with Clark's barbell and funnel split for Lane B and Lara's job-per-slot week for
  Lane A's themed days. Diandra's five to seven, Shanee's ten and RYVO's Buffer relay stay dated
  experiments with a named stop, never defaults.
- Judge any cadence change at 90 days (Clark, Mark, Diandra agree) and read last week's actuals first
  per step 4 of this stage's procedure.
- Winner reuse (Will's windows) and one manufactured peak a month (Clark) enter the slate as
  proposals through `references/idea-engines.md`; each rerun or peak carries a fresh gate pass and
  Mike's yes like any other post.
