# Value-first lead capture

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

Governing contracts: rig-revenue-meta. Value twice before asking once: the article helps, the
tool/guide helps, only then the email ask ("you already wowed them... why would I not give them my
email?" — CLAIM: b6zi9A2gP-U).

## Inputs

- Traffic pages (what already ranks), buyer personas, the firm's approved free value (fee guide,
  checklist, calculator, intake worksheet)
- Email/CRM destination and the nurture the firm approves (compliance: law/CPA/clinic funnels
  carry confidentiality rules the firm signs off)

## Outputs

- Magnet spec per page type (what the visitor gets before the ask)
- Embedded magnet (interactive widget or gated download) on the target pages
- Lead flow record: source page → magnet → consent → CRM → nurture step
- Funnel math with explicit assumptions (never dressed as measured results)

## Step 1. Pick the magnet by buyer stage

1. Question-shaped traffic → interactive answer tool (calculator/estimator/quiz/checklist that
   produces a personalized result from one input — e.g. an intake-budget estimator).
2. Comparison traffic → comparison/decision worksheet (the "X vs Y" buyer is one step from
   hiring).
3. Local/urgent traffic → direct booking CTA beats a magnet; don't gate people who are ready.

## Step 2. Build the value-first sequence

1. Deliver the personalized output BEFORE any email ask. The ask is a follow-on ("more tips",
   "the full checklist"), never the toll gate.
2. The magnet's output must be genuinely useful standalone and must carry the firm's honest
   caveats (a legal/financial/medical disclaimer where the professional requires it).
3. One magnet per page type and buyer stage; test variants.

## Step 3. Wire the flow

1. Consent language approved for the jurisdiction; leads export to the client's CRM/email tool.
2. Nurture sequence reviewed by the professional where regulated; send cadence is the firm's to
   approve. Nothing auto-sends from RIG tooling without that approval.

## Step 4. State the funnel math honestly

Show impressions → CTR → opt-in → conversion as an assumption chain when projecting (the corpus's
own funnel is e-commerce with claimed ~3% CTR / ~5% opt-in / 8-12% purchase — CLAIM: TrbM_vzts4o;
it is not a professional-services benchmark). Mark projections as projections. Measure the real
funnel monthly (rig-revenue-monthly-report).
