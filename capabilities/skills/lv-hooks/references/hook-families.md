# Hook levers, families, skeletons and the kill list

## The levers (stack two or three, four at most)

These are the mechanism layer behind every family. Lever count drifted from 2-5 (2024) to 2-3 (2026);
the current rule is 2-3, and never more than 4. [clark:hooks-and-media P_5mJNksHiE, UjDZLhuEpnQ]

| Lever | Why it works | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Specific numbers | precision reads as truth; money moves B2B readers | unrounded beats rounded; a precise figure out-pulls a round one [-WGCvECeTcU, h3751DcSeQw] |
| Narrative | a story promise with stakes, a turn, "how I" | the anchor lever for 2025-26 [nbXzXfthKl0, 0EA0n1Ka41k] |

| Social proof | lead with the result or record that earns attention | proof first [P_5mJNksHiE] |

## The merged family taxonomy

Eleven active families. Five carry a recorded library composite score from
`~/Documents/JakeStudio/alfred/learnings/hook_library.json`; the rest are corpus-strong. The
cross-platform generators' angle labels (number-led, contrarian, personal transformation, authority
steal, admission, future shock) map onto these families rather than forming a second taxonomy.

| Family | Score | Mechanism | Wins / loses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit / ledger reveal | 93 | named dollar, named source, named ratio in one paragraph | wins with receipts in hand; loses without them (the receipts gate is hard) |
| Reframe incumbent as validation | 84 | threat flipped into confidence; names the gap the incumbent cannot close | loses into bravado without a named mechanism gap |
| Series cadence opener | 72, compounding | same chassis, escalating stakes, "Post N of M" | only when every part really ships |
| Numbered takeaway list | 71 | 3+ named, ranked, opinionated takeaways with commentary | save-bait; generic items kill it |
| Two-line koan | 51, conditional | reframe in two lines | only with a named mechanism attached; never standalone |
| Transformation / before-after | corpus-strong | "X ago I had Y. Now I have Z. Here is how" | journey and proof-of-work posts; numbers must be real and ours |
| Problem-agitate | corpus-strong | make them feel the pain before the fix | top-of-funnel; the failure mode is agitation nobody feels |
| Confession / lesson | corpus-strong | real failure, then the lesson, then the application | trust posts; sanitized stories with no real cost fail |
| Curiosity-gap list opener | corpus-strong | a list of negatives or specifics that opens an unanswerable loop | anti-profile positioning; dies if the gap closes ordinary |
| Specific-stat credibility drop | corpus-strong | an odd, unrounded, verifiable number in line one | only when we own the number |
| Polarizing unhedged claim | corpus-strong | a strong statement with the hedge removed | thesis posts; positions we cannot defend burn B2B trust |

Kill list: generic "AI is the future of X", long thought-leadership without receipts, vendor pitch
disguised as thought leadership, posts with no specific named mechanism, engagement bait ("comment X
and I'll DM": LinkedIn has said it filters it), and pillar-listing without protocol framing.

## Power principles

- Hook power is contrast distance: the reader believes A, the hook shows B, and the gap between them
  is the curiosity. Specificity widens the gap. [creator: Kallaway, xnOe8aA9Pmw]
- The freeze answers two questions fast: what is this about, and what do I get for continuing.
  [creator: Kallaway, pNIYikmYsyw; language-flagged caption, re-verify phrasing before quoting]
- Show the evidence and let the reader reach the conclusion. Self-reached conclusions are trusted
  faster than stated ones. [creator: Kallaway, cuVyTmbOZjk]
- The contrast trick: the same fact opened as a loop beats the plain statement ("I changed one line
  and got 47 leads" over "here is how I got 47 leads"). [creator: Will McTighe, TTH-75XOgJE]
- Milestone posts lead with the strongest metric class: revenue, then growth rate, then customers,
  then headcount. [CkoSKSZ6WIU]

## Skeleton shapes (RIG-written; fill every slot with a sourced receipt)

- Result, constraint, loop: "[specific result] in [time], with [surprising constraint]. What we changed:"
- Boundary: "I will not [common practice] unless [condition]. Here is the reason."
- Buyer conversation: "Last week a [role] at a [company size] told me [problem]. [turn]"
- Mistake count: "[number] mistakes [buyer] make with [topic]. The [ordinal] one cost [client type] [amount]."
- How-I reframe: "How I [outcome], not how you should."
- Borrowed name: "[known company] just [event]. What it changes for [buyer]:"
- Hypothetical: "If I ran [function] at a [stage] company and needed [outcome] by [date], my order of moves:"
- Data: "We reviewed [scope]. [Metric] moved [precise figure], and [surprise]."
- Origin: "[time] ago I [left / almost lost] [thing]. [stakes]. How we got here:"

## Archetype overlay (personas, not a separate taxonomy)

Six named personas from short-form doctrine map onto the families above: Fortune teller (polarizing
or future-shock), Experimentor (transformation or confession), Teacher (numbered list, authority
register needs receipts), Magician (curiosity-gap opener), Investigator (audit reveal, receipts
required), Contrarian (polarizing unhedged claim). Where an archetype's register conflicts with lane
voice, lane voice wins. [creator: Kallaway, xnOe8aA9Pmw]
