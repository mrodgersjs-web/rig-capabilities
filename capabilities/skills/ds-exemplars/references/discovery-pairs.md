# discovery: looks like / does not

Owning main skill: ds-discovery. Every row paraphrases a good-versus-bad contrast a creator showed, cited to the moment. Use as calibration before judging a screen; a card is only reusable as a visual reference after Jev ui.design scores the good side above the bad side.

| # | looks like (good) | does not (bad) | why | citation |
|---|-----|----------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | One-sentence goal written before any pixels | Template opened, look decided, purpose figured out later | Guesswork sites can look fine and still fail the job | CLAIM(wCiM8jYE5yg@00:11), CLAIM(wCiM8jYE5yg@01:54) |
| 2 | First question is what success looks like | First question is what it should look like | Look-and-feel answers skip the visitor and the action | CLAIM(wCiM8jYE5yg@01:38) |
| 3 | One action repeated, always to the same place | Three buttons sending people three ways | Split actions leave no path | CLAIM(wCiM8jYE5yg@05:45) |
