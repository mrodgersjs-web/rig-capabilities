# Writing and body craft (Lara Acosta)

Load this file for body structure, post types, length, CTAs and voice. Her two named frames survive every algorithm shift she describes.

## The two frames

- **SLAY** is her teaching frame: a story, the lesson it produced, a concrete list of steps, and a question aimed at the reader. The label is garbled in some captions, but the four-part shape is stable from 2024 to 2026 and she says it has never failed her. [_6lKN2fZgIs, ozhwN9ndetM, 0ZVUVa4IFTw]
- **PAS** is her conversion frame: name the problem, agitate it with a second line that earns the read-more click, then give the solution. [6EYqnuncRrg, Qz003lp_5vA]
- Her division of labour: teach with SLAY, convert with PAS, and put a picture on the post either way. [Foh1r-TdnTk, r7nnmWBTHUk]
- Dated wobble: in April 2026 she said a bare how-I line fails as well as a how-to, because a model writes both; by September 2026 SLAY is the default again. [Ug5Np7RNX7A, 0ZVUVa4IFTw]

## Post anatomy and rhythm

- The three-part body she repeats: hook, the meat that delivers the promised thing, and a call to action. Facts without a feeling attached do not move anyone. [ueIjOIWS4fY]
- Every line should stand alone for a scanner, with varied sentence lengths and bullets creating rhythm. No long paragraphs, no jargon, no tangents. [EACWADXrg88, 6EYqnuncRrg]
- A sentence ceiling of 8 to 10 words on a mobile-first layout was her 2024 rule; by 2025 she says formatting matters less once a post is a long step-by-step. [ZK-CBIFDR9w, 1wsfrYzwKbk]
- Length she cites in 2025 and 2026: about 800 characters for a short post and a similar size for a video caption, with the caption summarizing the script rather than restating it. Creator claim. [ibfdqyz4KhQ, 71AspHNJxBE]
- Hashtags are out and positioning keywords are in, because she believes the text is machine-scored for topic fit. Creator claim, unverified. [b_sVHSaQqGA]
- The closing comment ask is engagement bait in some of her own framing; the version she keeps is a real question at the end of a story post. [1wsfrYzwKbk, 0ZVUVa4IFTw]

## Post types and their jobs

- She assigns each post a job before writing: teach, document, tell a story, or sell. Format is not what separates posts, the job is. [JDZYNJQOee0, 1ph8i-NDz4k]
- Two readers in every post: the buyer, and the follower who cannot pay but routes the buyer to you. [L7a694gzKG4, JDZYNJQOee0]
- Teaching posts deliver the method in full. Her rule is to give all of the how; buyers pay for the implementation. [1wsfrYzwKbk, qUmkSnXzW5A]
- Documenting posts open with a real number and a readable screenshot; she warns against opening with a record-month claim. [JDZYNJQOee0]
- Story posts carry a lesson but never carry an ask on a heavy or personal announcement. [JDZYNJQOee0, qap4n06dqfw]
- Sell posts run weekly in her May 2026 advice with the price named; three weeks later she says most of the revenue sits in email instead. Record both and pick by lane. [7SKd2cchGd4, 9QiqofqNHq8]
- Unselfish content: the first line or two hints at money, time or a better life for the reader rather than the author. [L7a694gzKG4]
- Educational content is her safest money bet; pure status content builds followers without leverage. [qUmkSnXzW5A]

## Voice

- Story carries the post and advice is the point of it; not every post has to be a story, but the ones that travel are hers. [0ZVUVa4IFTw, e3E83C-Xxn0]
- Use the second person more than the first. Facts, a lesson, a list, a question. [0ZVUVa4IFTw]
- Corporate register is tired; relatable is allowed when it is useful, and false humility is not. [qap4n06dqfw]
- A scene must connect to business for her, or it is content that does not convert. [b_sVHSaQqGA]
- The how-I principle: a post carries what a model cannot produce, namely a lived scene, proprietary numbers, or a photo of the actual work. [Ug5Np7RNX7A, 6d_oxo1R9gs]

## AI in the writing process

- Her 2025 workflow: seed the model with your real profile, a pillar map, and openings that already worked elsewhere, then edit hard before publishing. She discloses that her own team built one tool in that stack. [ibfdqyz4KhQ, wYIzZqpHs3Q]
- She keeps roughly 40 to 60 percent of long-form machine output and always adds the insights the model cannot know. Creator claim. [wYIzZqpHs3Q]
- Bad tells she names: emoji stuffing and article-length jargon. Voice comes from what you saved, not from the tool. [ibfdqyz4KhQ]
- Her guardrail in 2026: your own thinking goes into the prompt first, or the output is sameness. A tip without a why is what everyone else is already posting. [7SKd2cchGd4, Ug5Np7RNX7A]
