# Hooks (Kasey Brown)

His hook doctrine is the most heavily taught part of his system: he attributes most underperformance to the opening, not the topic, and he spends most of his own drafting time there. All numbers are creator claims.

## The stop is lines 1-2 plus the photo

- Roughly 70 to 80 percent of writing effort belongs in the first two lines and the paired image, because those together decide whether a phone scroller taps "see more". [4jnS31Fh5QE, LvA5pocucUM, ycA0D55AEQ8]
- He grades the stop separately from the body and the share, and estimates about 80 percent of a post's performance sits in the stop. [hC7ZCVsG7qM, LvA5pocucUM]
- The reader decides in about a second and a half, so the opening must be readable as a fragment, not a sentence that needs its ending. [yrI3QPIUk0Q]

## Three hook slots, not one

He treats a post as having three separate hook opportunities, and a weak one anywhere loses people. [yrI3QPIUk0Q, vBxw5q522pY]

1. **Lines 1-2:** the shock statistic, the quantified contradiction, or the time contrast. The payoff is never revealed here.
2. **Line 3:** a re-hook carrying new tension or promising a revelation, never a continuation of line 2. [yrI3QPIUk0Q]
3. **The photo:** the second stop device; a wrong photo wastes a strong line. [ycA0D55AEQ8, hC7ZCVsG7qM]

## The trigger vocabulary

He teaches a small set of emotional triggers that force a tap: curiosity, confusion, contradiction, controversy, suspense and conflict, and he leans hardest on curiosity plus one of the conflict family. [bCxD79Wl6ec, LvA5pocucUM, vBxw5q522pY] He calls the mechanism a curiosity gap: the image and the opening leave one unanswered question, and the brain wants it closed. [LvA5pocucUM, m9VFFCW_qZs]

He also teaches a brutal honesty test: would a total stranger stop for this line, or is it only interesting to you? He attributes about 80 percent of underperformance to openings that fail that test, and his fix is to pull the buried strongest fact (often a stat sitting mid-post) up to line one. [yrI3QPIUk0Q]

## Slot skeletons (fill these, never paste an example)

- **Time contrast:** a low point year with a humbling reality, then a recent year with a quantified win; the gap is the hook. [yrI3QPIUk0Q, jcxbBuiW2vE]
- **Reversal:** name the belief everyone holds, then state that it is backwards. [2t-EBcS43Lw, B18w0jPdXiI]
- **Costly admission:** announce that this take will cost followers, then take it. [2t-EBcS43Lw, B18w0jPdXiI]
- **Mistake ledger:** name the mistake you made recently, promise the lesson. [2t-EBcS43Lw]
- **Result first:** a number plus a timeframe, then the named person and the story behind it. [ycA0D55AEQ8]
- **Client phrase:** open with the exact kind of sentence a struggling buyer says to you, in their words. [ycA0D55AEQ8]
- **Before the trophy:** start at the hard, weak, almost-quit part of the story and land the win as the closing beat; never open with the award. [m9VFFCW_qZs]
- **Short validating statement:** name the reader's under-discussed situation in one line, with a plain photo and two or three numbers. [bTGyrxFkB3g]

## Testing and reuse

- He runs a hook-rating loop in a small tool stack and revises from what performed for his specific audience rather than from generic rules. [Lo7cSO2XH2k]
- He keeps a doc of top posts refreshed every 90 days and reuses winners as a named recurring series instead of inventing a new angle each week. [LvA5pocucUM, vBxw5q522pY]
- Dated shift: earlier videos teach copied opener formulas adapted from studied creators [uHgHT0fg3As, 4jnS31Fh5QE], but by 2026 he rejects borrowed hook formulas and engagement bait outright, because formula phrasing marks a post as machine-made and bait damages status. [iVnkmeVj9ao, kuipymgFpjk]

## Applying it (RIG)

Before drafting, route the hook choice to `rig-s1gate check post.linkedin` and treat DEFERRED as no verdict. Write three openers per post (stat, reversal, time contrast), pick one with the brutal honesty test, then pair it with the photo as one unit the way he does. Put the strongest fact first even when it ruins the story order; his own instruction is to kill the darling and repost if the foundation is weak. In Mike's lanes the skeletons fill with real receipts only, and the re-hook line never teases something the body does not deliver.
