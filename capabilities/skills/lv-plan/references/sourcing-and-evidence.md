# Sourcing and evidence: corpus, claim ledger, receipts

Sub-skill of lv-plan. Every idea and every number in a plan carries an evidence class or does not
enter the plan. This file is the discipline that keeps fabricated figures out of the slate.

## Sourcing procedure (corpus first, never grep first)

1. Read the corpus map and pick the cluster that answers the question: algorithm, strategy,
   dm-outreach, ai-system, profile, format-carousel, networking/job-search, or monetize. Do not
   search the whole transcript store first.
2. Open only the named transcripts. Quote exactly when wording matters; paraphrase only when the
   wording is not load-bearing.
3. Tag every claim: `[primary]` LinkedIn published it, `[secondary]` trade press reading a primary
   source, `[creator]` said in a video, `[ours]` measured on Mike's own account, `[claimed]`
   unverifiable self-report.
4. If the corpus is silent, answer **NOT COVERED**. A confident answer to an unasked question is
   worse than a named gap.
5. Check the contradictions list before promoting any format, hashtag or metric claim; a creator who
   contradicts himself is one voice, not two.
6. Attribute by channel plus video title, always.

## Claim ledger rules

- Every creator-sourced number carried into planning lives in a ledger row: the claim, who said it,
  its class, and what would verify it.
- Nothing in the ledger may appear in public or client copy unmarked.
- Ordering survives citation; magnitudes do not. A creator percentage never becomes a publishable claim.
- A second citation of the same underlying source is not a second source. Several decay figures that
  all trace back to one report are one data point.
- Prefer the platform's primary statement over any creator when they disagree.
- Row example set worth re-reading before any plan: reach weights (likes about 1%, dwell larger),
  comment-reach multiples, visual-format multipliers, profile-completeness claims, and any
  follower-growth or reply-rate self-report. Treat each as ordering or drop it.

## Corpus map essentials

- Root of the transcript store and its watchlist live with the growth-os corpus files; the current
  state runs past 100 transcripts and the counts move with each re-enumeration.
- Two distilled doctrine files cover growth/algorithm and DM/AI systems; later passes added
  profile/positioning and monetization/offer doctrine plus raw extract files. A source stage that
  stops at the old files misses whole claim families.
- Date warning: anything describing the feed before 2026-03-12 describes the replaced system. Check
  the publish date in the corpus metadata before importing an algorithm claim.
- Do not conclude a channel is empty because a parser returned nothing; a renderer change can make a
  full catalog look like zero items. Fail loud on a zero-item fetch.

## Idea and fact sourcing lanes (for the plan)

- **Corpus lane:** the claims above, with tags.
- **Market lane:** live breakout posts in the lane's topics this week (Eden index; the Eden discover
  tool), used as receipt evidence that a shape works, never as copy source.
- **Workspace lane:** what Mike already saved, captured or shipped. This is the moat lane.
- **Trend lane:** the daily trend pipeline's JSON report. A trend earns a slot only with a receipt
  and a lane fit; novelty alone is not a slot.

## Checks before sourced material enters a plan

- Every claim has a class tag and an attribute.
- No ledger magnitude appears without its marker.
- NOT COVERED is stated wherever the corpus is silent.
- Pre-2026-03-12 feed claims are either dropped or labelled as describing the old system.
- Own posts are performance data only, never market evidence.
