# brand_identity: looks like / does not

Owning main skill: ds-brand-identity. Every row paraphrases a good-versus-bad contrast a creator showed, cited to the moment. Use as calibration before judging a screen; a card is only reusable as a visual reference after Jev ui.design scores the good side above the bad side.

| # | looks like (good) | does not (bad) | why | citation |
|---|-------------------|----------------|-----|----------|
| 1 | A simple mark that still could not be swapped onto another brand | A minimal mark that could sit on five brands | Reduction that deletes identity is not simplicity | CLAIM(2RO44hoGdlE@00:21, 2RO44hoGdlE@00:27) |
| 2 | A non-literal recognition hook | A bulb drawn because the firm sells electricity | The mark is not a diagram of the offer | CLAIM(T54ytwmlObg@01:55, T54ytwmlObg@02:03) |
