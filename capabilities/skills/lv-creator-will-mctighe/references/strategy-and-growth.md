# Strategy and growth

Sub-skill of `lv-creator-will-mctighe`. Synthesis in RIG's words; bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims; his channel is small (1.86K subscribers), so reach and revenue figures are unverified and inconsistent across videos.

## 1. Positioning: start wide, then narrow on purpose

His rule is to open where you can defend credibility, then narrow once early engagement is working; a post aimed too narrowly dies on weak early engagement, and he gives niche-ceiling illustrations (a small profession's ceiling versus a universally felt workplace pain) [3UcZFurI_yw]. He narrowed deliberately and accepts slower growth for better-fit buyers [3UcZFurI_yw]. A broad topic is only raw material: name the audience and write their lived experience, or it stays generic career advice [O1LeyZsrnls]. He dates the shift: education-only stopped differentiating around late 2025 [3UcZFurI_yw], and listicles without a personal angle were fading by June 2026 [5rLlzb6_vgA].

He names his own fit boundary: strong for marketing, HR, sales and consulting; weak for spirituality and visual consumer products [pyPoIYDg_a0].

## 2. The funnel decides the post

Three jobs, three scores [0_8TCrdBPVM, UJvAtV63vk0, 5rLlzb6_vgA]:

- **Top (reach):** newsjacks, awareness, education, quarterly spectacle. Must fit the whole audience even when niche-named [UJvAtV63vk0, 5rLlzb6_vgA].
- **Middle (trust), about twice a week:** lived stories, work photos, short video, plus a transformation every week or two [pyPoIYDg_a0, 5rLlzb6_vgA].
- **Bottom (conversion):** case studies (1-2 a week, daily in a push), objection posts, founder demos, lead magnets [5rLlzb6_vgA]. He cites case studies at 10,000 to 20,000 views against 500,000 to 1 million a week for reach content, and says explicitly not to judge proof posts by views [5rLlzb6_vgA, 0_8TCrdBPVM].

His weekly mix in the growth phase: 4 reach, 2 stories, 1 conversion; the first 8 months were reach-daily, then a launch phase of case studies daily [UJvAtV63vk0]. A year of no inbound, he says, usually means zero transformation case studies [0_8TCrdBPVM].

## 3. The proof engine

Transformation case study: relatable before state, tangible after, you as guide, real scarcity only if true, and a visual that proves it [0_8TCrdBPVM]. Objection posts answer in dialogue what a discovery call would [0_8TCrdBPVM]. Video testimonials should be scripted toward the buying decision; free-form praise does not [0_8TCrdBPVM]. He also teaches the high-stakes challenge: commit publicly to a scary operating bet with a date and method, teach the updates including the bad ones, and let the unknown finish carry the series; a finished result is just a case study [F8IGilhJItA]. He cites peers running this pattern (a shutdown announcement, a billboard decode campaign) with large impression counts (creator claims) [F8IGilhJItA].

## 4. The profile as a 5-second page (2025-10-10)

Who you help, the outcome, and relevant proof must land in about 5 seconds or the visitor leaves; he claims 90% of leads are lost at the profile [Ql4gXPDhalk].

- **Headline:** role, helping [audience] get [outcome], then proof relevant to the buy. Job history and certificates matter less than results; company-name headlines fail unless the company is famous to the buyer [Ql4gXPDhalk].
- **About:** fifth-grade reading level, no buzzwords, no life story [Ql4gXPDhalk].
- **Featured:** a lead magnet, a booking link, or a case study. Not a top post [Ql4gXPDhalk].
- **Premium custom button:** on, with a UTM so it is measurable; he says most profiles never use it [Ql4gXPDhalk, 5rLlzb6_vgA].
- **Rollout:** headline this week, banner next, the rest within a month [Ql4gXPDhalk].

He notes a self-disqualifying profile is a win: the page should repel non-buyers [Ql4gXPDhalk].

## 5. Outbound and audience

Engagers and filtered profile viewers are the DM list; give value before asking; he cultivated about 30 peers at similar size and reports days of 100 warm messages (creator claims) [pyPoIYDg_a0, UJvAtV63vk0]. Growth is not influence: his test is whether the follower lives in the buyer's world and is in a buying mindset [pyPoIYDg_a0]. Email matters because you own it: his first broad career newsletter was a mistake, and he pivoted the newsletter to sit on the offer [pyPoIYDg_a0]. He paid for education early (courses, coaches, a writer) and names that as part of the path [UJvAtV63vk0].

## 6. Measurement and the honest scoreboard

Month-long test, rank by impressions, hypothesize the variable, repeat winners [Ej7WFBI9NS4]. Case studies get UTMs and are ranked by signups, not views [A9acfOArp98]. Quarterly review of what the mix produced [UJvAtV63vk0]. The 90/20 rule: most of the period's views come from a small minority of posts, so plan for outliers rather than averages [pyPoIYDg_a0].

Claim hygiene note: his own numbers drift across the corpus (followers 400K then 415K, 420K, 430K; revenue $500K then $600K then $500K ARR plus partnerships; weekly reach 1-1.5 million then 500,000-1 million; a client case at 107,000 then 120,000) [3UcZFurI_yw, TTH-75XOgJE, pyPoIYDg_a0, D_MY33bviuQ, Ql4gXPDhalk, 0_8TCrdBPVM, UJvAtV63vk0]. Treat the trajectory as the claim and every individual figure as unverified.
