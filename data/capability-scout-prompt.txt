Capability scout, weekly. Work only in the rig-capabilities repo.

1. Run: python3 scripts/weekly_capability_scout.py stage --since-days 9
   If it exits non-zero, read .context/capability-scout/<week>/week.json and report the
   rejections; do not retry the scrape more than twice.

2. Read that week's brief.md and queue.json. For each queued video, read its transcript
   and produce one row per agent capability: name; mechanism as inputs -> decision rule
   -> output; the verbatim transcript quote; the paper id, which MUST appear in
   week.json arxiv_verified; the open-source repo, which MUST appear in week.json
   repos_verified, or the literal "none"; the RIG target, which MUST be a real path
   under capabilities/ that you listed, or NEW.

3. Deduplicate across videos: the same capability cited in two videos is one row with
   both video ids.

4. Propose the asset changes as DRAFT files under .context/capability-scout/<week>/drafts/.
   Do not edit capabilities/, do not install anything, do not touch another harness's
   config, do not publish, send, push, deploy, or merge. Do not attempt to reach
   channel-membership-only videos.

5. Write .context/capability-scout/<week>/REPORT.md: rows accepted; every quote that was
   NOT a verbatim substring of its transcript, reported as a fabrication; every citation
   you wanted to use that week.json had rejected; and the one-line status that this run
   installed nothing.

A capability that cannot be stated as a mechanism is a topic, not a capability: drop it.
A quote you cannot paste from the transcript does not exist: report it, do not paraphrase
it into existence.
