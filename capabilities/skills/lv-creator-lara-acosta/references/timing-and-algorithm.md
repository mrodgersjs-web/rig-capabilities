Read the sequence honestly: she taught a golden hour in 2024, a warm-up in 2025, a 30-minute window in March 2026, and by June 2026 named both as dead systems while still keeping the comment habit. Any ritual here is a dated test, not a law.

- Her scheduling position is a hard stop from December 2024: posting manually only, because in her own test scheduled posts lost about 10 percent engagement. Creator claim. [6EYqnuncRrg]

## What to do after posting (current version)

- Reply to everything in the early window and keep commenting if the post is slow. [6d_oxo1R9gs, 6EYqnuncRrg]
- Seed the three self-comments (behind the scenes, a tip, a question). [6d_oxo1R9gs]
- Put the link in the post when the goal is traffic. Her 2026 position is that unclicked links hurt, not links themselves, and that a comment link is for awareness only. This retires the pinned-comment CTA, because LinkedIn removed comment pinning. [6d_oxo1R9gs]
- If the post is a winner, extend it: same plot retold, an outlier version across topics, or a magnet that continues the best post. [Foh1r-TdnTk, JDZYNJQOee0]

## Algorithm claims (her reading, unverified)

Everything in this section is a creator claim about how the platform works, and she says in May 2026 that staff themselves do not know. [7SKd2cchGd4]

- A post reaches first-degree connections first and expands to second and third degrees only on their engagement. [6EYqnuncRrg]
- New surfaces reward early users; native video in late 2024 is her example. [3gbPTfHEWNI]
- The feed can be trained by what you engage with, and who engages with you decides who sees you. [ozhwN9ndetM, e3E83C-Xxn0]
- Profile visits raise distribution, and a like from a buyer puts you in front of more buyers. Creator claim. [_6lKN2fZgIs, MLXZ_aa2ZKw]
- Roughly 2 to 3 percent of followers see a given post, which is her argument for an owned list. Creator claim. [JDZYNJQOee0]
- 2026 model: the text is matched to interests, the profile acts as a prompt for classification, a second wave comes when the post keeps earning engagement, and network engagement with strangers extends reach. She says a widely-cited research paper on this was withdrawn and mostly misread. [e3E83C-Xxn0, 7SKd2cchGd4]
- 2026-09 model: content is machine-scored for relevance and expertise before distribution, relevance outranks recency, posts can distribute for weeks, and therefore engagement pods are obsolete. Creator claim, unverified. [b_sVHSaQqGA]
- Her structural claim about sameness: the feed is saturated, and specific expert text beats generic creator content in her 2026 reading. [OSzrHwHLT6M, b_sVHSaQqGA]
