# Creator playbook: measurement (analytics and the learning loop)

What 11 creator doctrines (10 channels added 2026-09-28 + Tommy Clark) teach for this stage.

Creator percentages, impression totals, and revenue lines below are claims. They are not Mike's baseline and they do not enter a report as facts.

## Consensus (3+ creators agree)

1. Score the right people and the business result, not raw reach. Diandra: a roughly 5,000-impression post beat 100,000-impression posts on qualified leads, and 500 likes from the right people beat 50,000 from the wrong ones [BPbf3JUm_dw, ZAqIUNAvXXo]. Clark would take 5,000 impressions from the right people over 100,000 from the wrong ones [QKNc515DSro, yBKOrgQS-q8]. Harry, from 2 Sept 2026: 10 right profile viewers beat 1,000 wrong ones [sUC5UAXni6M]. Lara watches shares, saves, and who engages; likes are a bad sales metric for her [OSzrHwHLT6M, Ug5Np7RNX7A]. Michelle, from the June 2025 shift: saves, sends, visits, and follows, not impressions [lYqfrZZA64o, Nj8yZyzXi1E]. Shanee judges emails, replies, and calls; huge impressions with zero clients is her counterexample [Ave2bYi7XRg, 6f3ZzY5-gUg]. Kasey's weekly three are meaningful DM conversations, email signups, and opportunities [2t-EBcS43Lw, B18w0jPdXiI]. Mark counts buyer-matched profile visits and the failure case of many likes and zero DMs [uEHPPU2k_OM, FEOAeCCK9e0]. RYVO/Louise judges profile views, conversations, and outside-network share, not raw impressions [ZSS7Dh2WmcE, pzkFjBumMCs].
2. Winners and outliers, not the average post. Will cites most of a period's views coming from a small minority of posts and says plan for outliers [pyPoIYDg_a0]. Lara repeats a winning format until it stops, and extends a winner rather than starting a new plot [6d_oxo1R9gs, Foh1r-TdnTk]. Pierre reuses formats that won his own test instead of the newest idea [CphfSIw7WV8]. Clark reviews the last five or six posts against the account's own baseline and reruns winners after about three months [E0uu_GLIkw0, 0EA0n1Ka41k]. Shanee mines month-one outliers to decide month-two [5Y_tekIdDmI, ajCBOx3C-oI].
3. Ask where the lead came from, or keep the conversation as the receipt. Clark asks every lead how they heard about you and warns against obsessing over per-post tracking links [lCeE49rDcH0, E0uu_GLIkw0]. Kasey saves inbound business conversations in a named folder as the real scoreboard [uHgHT0fg3As]. Shanee's stated metric for a revenue goal is emails received [sqv-zZLDGAA]. Lara measures leads, calls, and revenue attributed to content [qUmkSnXzW5A, hDknZIF9Wsc].
4. A creator's platform model is a hypothesis. Timing files for Lara, Diandra, Will, Harry, Kasey, Mark, Pierre, RYVO/Louise, Shanee, and Michelle all label algorithm lines unverified. When those lines disagree with this account's tracker, the tracker wins.

## Conflicts and dated shifts

- What the number is for. Will's month test ranks by impressions [Ej7WFBI9NS4]. Harry's newer reading (2 Sept 2026) demotes the first hour because buyers may never comment and checks who viewed the profile [sUC5UAXni6M]. Michelle ended impression-counting as the goal in June 2025 [lYqfrZZA64o]. Diandra and Clark use the same shape: a smaller right-audience post beats a larger wrong-audience one, as claims, not as Mike's result. RIG wins: the report leads with booked ICP calls; impressions corroborate only; 5,000 from the right people beat 100,000 from the wrong ones is already this stage's Lane B rule, and it is not permission to paste a creator's ARR line.
- When a quiet first hour means death. Lara and Kasey still treat the first 60 to 90 minutes as the distribution window [6d_oxo1R9gs, hC7ZCVsG7qM]. Will says the first hour is not the game. Pierre teaches no golden hour. Michelle teaches no post-publish window. Shanee teaches none. Harry taught that the first hour decides, then demoted it on 2 Sept 2026 [sUC5UAXni6M]. Newer on that question is Harry's demotion. This stage still captures t0, t+90 minutes, and the T+1h, T+4h, and T+24h series. Capture is not a verdict that a quiet hour killed the post.
- When a winner may be called and rerun. Clark: last five or six versus the account baseline, verbatim rerun after about three months, origin story quarterly [E0uu_GLIkw0, fHha-KIZeMg]. Will: exact rerun at 2 to 3 months, with an unreconciled one-month line in the same video [A9acfOArp98]. Kasey: do not read analytics until about 100 posts [uHgHT0fg3As]. Stage rules win: review weekly, do not call a tactic a winner before about 10 posts carry it, rerun proven winners at about three months, and never repost Lane A content Mike deleted without a fresh yes. Kasey's 100-post silence loses to that review.
- Follower count. Michelle's early-2026 reversal calls it social proof [0nz_nzgaJrY]. This stage's rule wins: the available API does not expose follower count; missing means unknown, not zero; a single day is never a rate.
- Windows. RYVO/Louise wants a full month, not 28 days [4RukRIBwoo4]. This stage already forbids comparing an unlabelled feed-sidebar figure to a 7-day analytics figure. Her export rule is a reminder to label the window, not a new surface.
