# Prioritize First Principles Over Tool Selection

Use when: Execute brief-to-campaign stages and stop for independent review

Inputs: workspace brief, approved claims, supporting evidence

1. Identify when discussions drift toward tool selection or technical implementation details.
2. Redirect the conversation to first principles: Does this solve a real problem? Is there market space?
3. Validate the core insight and motivation before committing to a tech stack.
4. Use the 'other team' thought experiment to identify unique competitive edges (capability, insight, motivation).

Output contract:

- A validated market hypothesis and identified competitive edge.

Limitations:

- Ignoring tools entirely can lead to feasibility issues later.
- Requires strong facilitation to keep the team focused on strategy.
