# Strategy, positioning, profile and pipeline (Lara Acosta)

Load this file for positioning, the profile rebuild, lead magnets, email, outreach and measurement. This is where she is strongest on revenue mechanics and where her advice conflicts with Tommy Clark's.

## Positioning

- One problem, one audience, one offer. She teaches a niche to win first and only then to widen, and she rejects the slogan of being your own niche until you own one. [EACWADXrg88, qUmkSnXzW5A, r7nnmWBTHUk]
- Unfair advantages are the raw material: list what you already have that a competitor cannot copy, then turn it into pillars or one lane. Her examples are life experience, an arbitrage you spotted, a monetisable skill, and a career transition. [ObW3McgGG44, r7nnmWBTHUk, b_sVHSaQqGA]
- 2025-09 she moves to a micro-niche framing: hyper-specific expertise rather than a department name, with every post showing the work. [OSzrHwHLT6M]
- 2026-06 she replaces pillars with one lane and says an off-topic post drops the whole account; 2026-09 she formalizes it as one main topic plus connected topics chosen to align with the offer, so every post solves a problem the product solves. [e3E83C-Xxn0, b_sVHSaQqGA]
- Content-market and content-offer fit: a topic sits where buyer interest, the offer and lived credibility overlap. [1ph8i-NDz4k]
- The tension to keep visible: 2025-09 says expertise or irrelevance is coming, while 2026-04 says beginners should post what is happening before they are experts. Both are hers. [OSzrHwHLT6M, qap4n06dqfw]

## Profile rebuild

- Profile as landing page: banner, headline, Featured and a custom link that points at a funnel or a magnet. She reports selling with no website at all. Creator claim. [UiKT7lxE4bo, LBa8SdmItjU]
- Headline: what you do, for whom, how, plus proof at the end; a job title or a generic growth line is the failure mode. She also argues the headline is search real estate and should carry a timeframe. [UiKT7lxE4bo, hCzcM6jV_zY, LBa8SdmItjU]
- The generic help line is overused as of July 2025, and by July 2026 a numbered, specific help line is back in favour with the buyer and the number in it. [ozhwN9ndetM, LBa8SdmItjU]
- About section as a sales page: hook, the reader's problem, the wanted result, proof, then one ask off-platform. [LBa8SdmItjU]
- Proof belongs in four places at once: headline, banner, Featured and the posts. No logos yet means work free and put the testimonial on the banner. [hCzcM6jV_zY]
- 500-plus connections is her early trust threshold, and every field should point at the same thing because she believes the profile is read like a prompt. Creator claim. [ZK-CBIFDR9w, e3E83C-Xxn0, b_sVHSaQqGA]

## Lead magnets and the owned list

- The lead machine is one burning problem, a free resource answering it in full, and a promo post that shows partial value with scarcity. She builds the resource in a notes tool and publishes it fast. [hDknZIF9Wsc]
- Her conversion figure: about 5 percent of downloads move to higher-ticket offers and the rest join the list. Creator claim. [hDknZIF9Wsc]
- The magnet is a value bridge: it continues the best post rather than starting a new topic. [JDZYNJQOee0]
- Cadence for the magnet: weekly in 2026-01, roughly monthly by 2026-07. [L7a694gzKG4, 9QiqofqNHq8]
- The email list is the asset. She reports 70 to 80 percent of revenue from email across several videos, sends weekly, and sells in 5 to 15 day windows. Creator claim. [7ZfRNgn3oHs, Qz003lp_5vA, 9QiqofqNHq8]
- Early-stage move: under 50 subscribers, message warm people directly and help only after they name a frustration. [9QiqofqNHq8]
- She says plainly that she started the list too late and had relied on inbound from an optimized profile. [7ZfRNgn3oHs]

## Outreach and the sale

- Commenters, viewers and new connections are the warm list; the first message is thanks plus a light question. [Slm6Ek465ow, 9QiqofqNHq8]
- Her sniper method from 2024: find an active creator's commenters, filter by headline, engage first, then a short personalized request. [6EYqnuncRrg]
- Cold bulk DMs are dead at a billion users in her 2026 framing; trust, redirected attention and the profile sell before the first message. She cites 80 percent of the sale happening before any DM. Creator claim. [MLXZ_aa2ZKw]
- The Trojan horse: an oversized unsolicited deliverable with one sensible next step, and 20 free-value outreaches a day while the offer is unproven. Free work for three clients, then testimonials, then raise prices. [Qz003lp_5vA]
- Price discipline: raise the rate after every close (her early figures are a few hundred dollars at a time), climb from done-for-you to done-with-you to a product or cohort, and ship a product in 4 to 6 weeks. [EACWADXrg88, 7ZfRNgn3oHs, Qz003lp_5vA]
- The offer must be findable at all times. Where it lives is genuinely unresolved in her corpus: author comment in 2024, a pinned comment that made her money in 2024-12, no calls in 2024, calls again in 2026-01, a weekly priced post in 2026-05, and email by 2026-07. The pinned option no longer exists as a platform feature. [UiKT7lxE4bo, 6EYqnuncRrg, ueIjOIWS4fY, MLXZ_aa2ZKw, 7SKd2cchGd4, 9QiqofqNHq8, 6d_oxo1R9gs]

## Measurement

- Validate ideas against saves and against posts that already won, and raise the bar every time. [L7a694gzKG4, qUmkSnXzW5A]
- Her quality bar from a guest teardown: a post is worth publishing if someone would forward or save it. [Ug5Np7RNX7A]
- Likes are a bad sales metric for her; she watches shares, saves and who is engaging instead. [OSzrHwHLT6M, Ug5Np7RNX7A]
- Repeat a winning format until it stops performing, and treat every format as a feedback loop. [6d_oxo1R9gs, ZK-CBIFDR9w]
- She measures business outcomes (leads, calls, revenue attributed to content) over reach, and warns that virality without leverage is empty. [qUmkSnXzW5A, hDknZIF9Wsc]

## Where she conflicts with Tommy Clark (2026)

- Cadence: he holds three strong posts a week with a funnel job each; she prescribes four with named jobs and lets beginners run daily to find the lane. [Qz003lp_5vA, JDZYNJQOee0, qap4n06dqfw]
- AI drafting: he forbids a model taking a post from zero to draft; she runs machine drafting from the first week as long as the profile, the pillars and a real scene go in first. [ibfdqyz4KhQ, wYIzZqpHs3Q]
- Paid: he buys thought-leader boosts for posts that under-delivered after an organic week; she boosts only a proven winner and adds a paid lever late. [OSzrHwHLT6M, Ug5Np7RNX7A]
- Agreement: real photos over branded graphics, a named buyer in the opening, comments as content, and self-reported attribution as the measure that matters.
