# The 2026 algorithm model (what to launch against)

Every statement here is a dated third-party claim until measured on Mike's accounts. Anyone who claims to fully understand the algorithm is lying [s6KMjhznXqs]; this is a working model, and the measurement loop in `lv-measure` arbitrates it.

## How the feed is described now

- **From network to interest graph.** Distribution increasingly follows topic interest rather than who follows you, echoing Meta's shift; accounts may be sorted into topics. [s6KMjhznXqs, BMfXeo02bvo]
- **Spiky and late.** A post can look dead for 12 hours and then take off. Per-post variance is high. Never read the first hours as the verdict. [s6KMjhznXqs]
- **Longer shelf life.** The feed resurfaces posts one to four weeks old and from people you do not follow, so a live post has to stay clean for weeks, not hours. [BMfXeo02bvo, _tON8MpkmS0, N2b0QVoCEPg]
- **Organic reach is structurally down.** Mid-size accounts average about 2,500 impressions and the trend is downward; the platform is squeezing organic toward ads and AI content has saturated the timeline. [BMfXeo02bvo]
- **Barbell.** The feed favors very high-effort posts and very scrappy ones; the polished middle is where AI suspicion lands. [s6KMjhznXqs]
- **Ranking is semantically matched to the reader** (a single AI model reading content against profile experience), which is why profile clarity matters; competing explanations exist and none is verified. [CM_HDRF4y0s, sUC5UAXni6M]

## Signals the feed is claimed to reward or punish

| Signal | Working rule |
|---|---|
| Person vs page | personal profiles out-reach company pages; the company page is a proof surface, not the growth surface |
| New connections | an accepted request likely shows them your next one or two posts, which is why launch-day connection hygiene matters |
| Comments | comments carry a post into the commenters' networks; real threads beat one-word replies; your own comments can earn post-level impressions |
| Early window | the first 30-60 minutes still matter, but posts recover; first 20 minutes of reception weigh heavily [Fvl_wMtcz24, MnQc73VYbJ0] |
| Saves and shares | save-worthy, forwardable assets travel off-platform; people share what makes them look smart to their network |
| Dwell time | counts for more than likes; long structured posts help |
| Hashtags | close to useless; house law says two or three at most, placed at the end after line breaks, never inline. A corpus creator says three to five: that is an open contradiction and is not silently adopted |
| Posting time | no perfect time exists, but weekday mid-morning local stays the default; launches Monday to Thursday |
| Links | body links are penalized and link-in-first-comment is now penalized too; a PS-line link is the boost exception |
| Volume | more than one or two posts a day suppresses; about one a day per account is the ceiling |
| Engagement pods | teach the feed to serve non-buyers; accounts sag after leaving |
| Off-ICP engagement | posts get served to the engagers' audiences, so wrong engagers drag reach toward the wrong people |
| Tagging | tagging a larger account in a positive curation post buys early reach; mass tagging only for fundraise launches |
| Repost button | reposting your own post hours later resurfaces it |
| Automation | a ban risk; take the conservative side of the automation argument because the account is a business asset, not a growth experiment |

## Low-engagement diagnosis, in this order

1. Opening: are two or three levers present in lines 1-3?
2. Media: a real photo with people in it?
3. Story: is each take paired with one?
4. Intensity: turned up about 20%?
5. Timing: a weekday mid-morning?
6. Audience ceiling: engineering, procurement and IT buyers read but rarely react; founder, marketing and sales audiences react. Set the benchmark before blaming the content.
7. Topic reach: when niche posts stall, widen within the buyer's world, never into empty controversy.
8. Engagers: are the people reacting actually buyers?

Judge a post against the account's own baseline; a 100-like post on a 10-20-like baseline is the signal to study. Chasing likes is the trap; the target is customers. [0EA0n1Ka41k, zMzPmTETIyU]

## What this changes about launching

Launch timing optimizes the early window but does not bet the post on it: the second save asset, the repost and the do-not-delete rule are the levers that respect spiky, delayed distribution. Keep the post clean for four weeks after launch, because it can resurface at any point in that window.
