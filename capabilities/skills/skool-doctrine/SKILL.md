## What this is

A law index distilled from Skool-community creator videos, organized by the 12 stages of `skool-growth-os`. Each law is a paraphrase carrying the video ids that support it:

- `consensus` = two or more channels say it. Usable as a default.
- `single-source` = one channel. A hypothesis to test on RIG Intelligence Lab, not a rule.

The full index: `references/law-index.md` (generated, do not hand-edit). Per-stage corpus notes with UNVERIFIED creator result claims and contested points: `data/corpora/skool/notes/` (local, gitignored).

3. **The empty-room welcome is honest** - "I'm just getting started, which is why it means the world that you're early - what were you hoping to see?" then actually do it (fXiFlwKvB3U@35:31).
4. **Owner activity precedes member activity** - engagement first, then platform exposure follows (fXiFlwKvB3U@12:10).
