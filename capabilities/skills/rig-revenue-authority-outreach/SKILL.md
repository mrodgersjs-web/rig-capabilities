# Authority outreach sprint (clean lanes only)

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

When this skill triggers, go straight to Step 1. Do not summarise.

Governing contracts: rig-revenue-meta. The corpus's link menu contains excluded items (paid niche
edits, donor links, link swaps, PBNs, 100-300-link press bursts). They stay excluded. The corpus
itself notes clean backlink profiles outperformed heavy link building (CLAIM: CJzbrZYiEio).

## Inputs

- Firm credentials and named experts (verified), genuinely linkable assets (data report, survey,
  calculator, definitive guide with original figures)
- Competitor backlink profiles (public data), industry/media/journal-request targets, local
  institutions (chamber, associations, sponsorships)

## Outputs

- Asset list (what is genuinely worth linking to — kill anything that isn't)
- Placement pipeline: target, angle, status, earned link, date
- Monthly earned-link and mention report (no fabricated counts)

## Step 1. Build or find the linkable asset

Stats-driven content is the clean magnet: the firm's own data compiled into a report others cite
(rig-revenue-stats-articles). Alternatives: definitive local guide, salary/fee benchmark, survey
results. No asset → no outreach; do not pitch fluff.

## Step 2. Prospect (rig-revenue-guest-post-prospector)

1. Sources that already cite competitors' similar assets (public backlink data).
2. Journal-request platforms (HARO-style / featured.com-class) where the named expert answers with
   real expertise — the answer is written by the expert; AI may only grammar-check (raw AI answers
   get rejected — CLAIM: hYR-_LSXbI0).
3. Industry publications, local news, associations, resource pages, podcasts. Quality filter:
   real editorial standards, real authors, topical fit, no link-seller signals.

## Step 3. Outreach

Personalized, one genuine observation about their content per pitch ("no buzzwords soup" — CLAIM:
nlVw5vOBogA). Every outreach is a DRAFT behind rig-revenue-publish-gate; nothing sends without the
human. Offer value first (the asset, a quote, a data cut).

## Step 4. Place and verify

1. Accepted pieces carry a genuine contextual link or an author byline link. Anchor text is
   natural/branded — never keyword-stuffed.
2. Record each earned placement with URL, date, and how it was earned. A paid placement is never
   recorded as earned; if a client buys a sponsored mention, it is disclosed as sponsored and
   logged as such.

## Step 5. Report honestly

Earned links, mentions, and referral traffic per month. No projection language ("this will rank
you"). Authority work compounds; timeline claims are refused.

## Tools

- Public backlink indexes (Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz-class) — neutral (n/unknown; not endorsed)
- Journal-request platforms (HARO/featured.com-class) — neutral (unknown)
- Arvow backlink pool / premium paid backlinks — PAID ENDORSEMENT (source channel affiliate) AND
  the pool mechanic is a link scheme under rig-revenue-meta → REFUSED regardless of label
- vet.com link vendor — link-buying channel → REFUSED
