# Interview handoff

## Inputs
Current role/JD/persona, submitted document hashes where applicable, verified claim ledger and actual invitation/request evidence.

## Actions
Record the interview stage accurately. Refresh employer/panel facts from public sources, marking unknown identities. Map every substantive JD requirement to likely question, supported result/personal action/strategy answer, relevant employer context, follow-up and explicit gap. Keep target-industry applications hypothetical until confirmed as personal history. Preserve useful concise answers without inventing metrics. Prepare questions to ask and a source-bound handoff; do not schedule, message, join or record an interview.

## Outputs
Interview packet with requirement map, truthful answer drafts, source IDs, gaps, current document references and invitation versus attendance state.

## Gates
Application submission alone does not prove an interview. Never invent panel beliefs, tenure, ownership or outcomes. Missing information stays a gap; interview drafting confers no outreach or application authority.

## Recovery
Refresh only changed company/JD/claim material, preserve submitted history and flag divergences. Hold unsupported stories and use supported alternatives. Return pending facts to Mike before employer-facing use.
