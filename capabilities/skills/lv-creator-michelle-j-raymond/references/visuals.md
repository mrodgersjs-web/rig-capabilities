# Visuals, images and video (Michelle J Raymond)

She teaches images and video with real positions, and she teaches almost nothing about carousels. Her media rule is simple: real people beat branded graphics, and no format is worth betting the year on [K0D6kDr-WAY, _HK3z6D0SRY, Nj8yZyzXi1E].

## Images

- **Faces are the fastest metric lift she sees in page audits.** Employee and team photos are the one visual change she credits with moving page numbers (creator claim) [K0D6kDr-WAY].
- **Stock that matches your rivals gets scrolled.** Real team photos cannot be copied by a competitor; ad-like branded images read as ads and are skipped [_HK3z6D0SRY].
- **The banner is the positioning billboard.** It has to show what you are known for (stage photos if you speak, the positioning statement if you consult), and page visitors read it as a billboard [_HK3z6D0SRY, mDsy5N6jgHs, mOguk9ZaZPk].
- **Recognizable with the logo off** is her visual test for page content [K0D6kDr-WAY].
- **A stop-scroll visual can carry the hook:** an image, a GIF, a humor beat or a screenshot of news the audience is discussing [Dd42KDThdPY, _HK3z6D0SRY].
- **An image post beats video for most people and most of her clients** (creator claim, unverified) [nC06xaoVWPw].
- Banner frames can rotate like a slideshow (she cites about a four-second cycle); useful for showing several proofs in one banner (creator claim) [J3hMUDIk7qE].

## Video (she reversed herself here)

- **Dated shift 2025-04-28:** she had written LinkedIn short video off more than once, then reversed for B2B only when the video serves the audience. Phone in daylight is enough; edit in Descript or CapCut; brand lightly so it does not read as an ad; cut long video with Opus Clip; her favorite format is LinkedIn Live [zSfu7p5LjXM].
- **Length guidance from that episode:** under 30 seconds she cites much higher completion, max around two minutes (platform figures, creator claim) [zSfu7p5LjXM].
- **The reversal did not survive 2025-2026 measurement.** By late 2025 she says do not build the year on video, her image posts beat video for most clients, and video tests came back with effort high and reach often below a normal post [nC06xaoVWPw, lYqfrZZA64o].
- **A guest agency ranked video fourth** behind galleries, carousels and single images on its own metrics, and called video ROI disappointing that year (creator claim, guest's data) [0nz_nzgaJrY].
- **"Video is not the answer"** is her February 2026 position: no format is magic, usefulness is the measure [Nj8yZyzXi1E].
- **June 2026:** the video feed returned in some countries with a claimed watch boost she doubts. Her call is watch it, do not pivot; she already fills her page with podcast clips [OCpDoffJO_I].
- **Small video uses that do count:** 30-60 second thank-you clips to people who helped you, and Live sessions as the two-way format she favors [ZbylvpCOCzA, VUkmBUjOy_c].
