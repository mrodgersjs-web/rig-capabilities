## Market and horizon

- The feed is saturated and AI-flooded: organic reach is down and the quality bar is up. Generic
  value posts no longer move anything. [_tON8MpkmS0, BMfXeo02bvo, s6KMjhznXqs]
- Every take carries one of three moats: a real story, proprietary data, or physical proof (photo,
  artifact, screenshot). No receipt, no post. [gZwWBtiZ7qE, nbXzXfthKl0]
- Commit 6-12 months; expect the curve to bend around months 3-6 and judge any motion at 90 days.
  [mJ1c1PHBoo4, mTXBTznvNZI, 1qzSPOPlmuQ]
- Buyers over reach: the right 5,000 impressions beat the wrong 100,000. [yBKOrgQS-q8]

## RIG lane law (overrides any corpus claim)

- Eden is the sole publisher of record; every scheduled artifact stays a draft until Mike approves
  and publishes. Postiz is retired and never gets instructed.
- Nothing outward fires unattended: no posts, comments, DMs or follows without the lane's explicit
  approval state.
- Three lanes never borrow each other's voice. Lane C is out of scope for the growth lanes and is
  never auto-scheduled.
- Positive-only engagement on other people's posts; never contradict to farm attention.
- Hard caps: at most 3 link-bearing comments per sweep (one carrying a site link), 14-day author
  dedupe, at most 12 comments per sweep, follow batches at most 40/day and 90/week, and never a
  follow batch and a heavy comment sweep on the same day.
- No tactic enters the hook library without a measured result over roughly 10 posts.

## Evidence classes

`[creator]` a YouTuber said it, always with the video id. `[primary]` LinkedIn published it.
`[secondary]` trade press reading a primary source. `[ours]` measured on Mike's own account.
`[claimed]` unverifiable self-report. UNVERIFIED = folklore. Ordering survives citation; magnitudes
do not. A `[creator]` percentage never becomes a publishable claim.

## Tactic tiers and the automate split (11-video how-to corpus, dated 2026-09-23)

- Tier 1 (3+ videos agree): already law, do not re-litigate. Tier 2 (2 videos): strong prior behind
  a flag, measure two weeks before gating. Tier 3 (one video): a pre-registered experiment with a
  stated falsifier, never a silent adoption.
- Automate: idea scoring, analytics pulls, recycling long material into posts, congruence lints,
  first-50-word lint, media reminders, trend mining.
- Human-gated behind Gate-D: publishing, comments, replies, connection requests.
- Never: pods, engagement rings, third-party auto-commenters, template comments.
- The algorithm claims in that corpus are secondary-source and drift; re-mine quarterly.

## Conflicts resolved here (keep the record)

1. **Cadence:** daily posting (Taki's daily post), a 7-day Lane A rhythm, and 3-5/week lanes all
   collide with Clark's "three better posts a week". Resolution: the quality bar is three better
   posts a week; a lane keeps its existing rhythm only while it beats the account's own baseline,
   and the weekly review decides. [s6KMjhznXqs, li-growth]
2. **Delete-and-repost:** 2025 advice to delete laggards is superseded. Resolution: never delete
   early; a dead post gets a second distribution wave in a new format instead of more effort.
   [zMzPmTETIyU, li-growth]
3. **Keyword-comment CTAs:** comment-a-keyword lead magnets collide with the platform's demotion of
   engagement bait. Resolution: the bait form is banned; the asset travels through the profile path
   at most monthly. [linkedin-operating-doctrine, 8VqGXuFxd3Q]
4. **External links:** the strict "never in body" rule is kept; a PS-line link is allowed only on a
   post that may be boosted. [rig-linkedin-taki, gZwWBtiZ7qE]
