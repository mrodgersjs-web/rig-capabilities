# Sitemap And Page Jobs

Purpose: planning diagram, nav cap, page jobs, competitor shots, wireframe review, and the three sitemap types.

Load when: the task touches this part of discovery and brief, or a ds-discovery critique names it. Part of `ds-discovery`.

## Checklist
1. Work through every law below against the current artifact before calling the sub-topic done.
2. Cite the CLAIM when a law drives a decision; a law you cannot cite is labeled own reasoning.
3. Where creators disagree (see the SKILL.md), state both positions and test rather than assume.

## Anti-patterns for this sub-topic
- Opening a template and deciding the look before the job is named (CLAIM(wCiM8jYE5yg@00:13)).
- About twenty pages dumped into the navigation (CLAIM(R_UYM9yEuYg@04:51)).
- Asking for four brand words in one cold question (CLAIM(qO0TbOdWado@01:10)).
- Designing with no content in hand (CLAIM(owV1GDsPpno@03:00)).
