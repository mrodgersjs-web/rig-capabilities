# Writing

Sub-skill of `lv-creator-will-mctighe`. Synthesis in RIG's words; bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims.

## 1. Craft: decisions first, prose second

His writing thesis is that slow drafting is decision fatigue: lock the framework, the problem, and the opening, then let the draft come fast, then put your own voice back in [TTH-75XOgJE]. His reported arc: about 20 rewrites taking 2 to 3 hours each in month one, versus 15 to 20 minutes per post later (creator claim) [TTH-75XOgJE]. The voice pass is the part he insists on: spoken habits, one real story, first person, one through-line, read aloud before shipping [TTH-75XOgJE, Ej7WFBI9NS4]. He also asks what a post costs in decisions, and reports cutting a post's decision count from about ten to one [TTH-75XOgJE].

A dated split from Tommy Clark: McTighe (2025-12-01) trains an AI on your business, buyer and notes to draft, then rewrites into your voice [TTH-75XOgJE]; Clark forbids AI taking a post from nothing to draft from September 2025 [2XeAckxuAKM, tYd_MnpXU90]. RIG follows Clark plus Eden: Eden drafts from a real story, transcript or receipt Mike supplied, never from a bare topic.

## 2. Frameworks he actually uses

| Framework | Shape (skeleton) | Job |
|---|---|---|
| PAS | name their pain, agitate what it costs, then 1 to 3 fixes with a soft CTA | reach; his stated favorite [EUueg8xudKk] |
| Story, lesson, application | lived moment, the universal lesson, optional application step | trust [TTH-75XOgJE] |
| Case study | before state, after state, what changed, urgent CTA, proof visual | conversion [TTH-75XOgJE, UJvAtV63vk0] |
| Objection dialogue | a high-status buyer challenges the offer, answered as a conversation | conversion [0_8TCrdBPVM] |
| Harsh truths list | "[N] truths of [niche]", the two strongest first, keep what hits you | reach [O1LeyZsrnls, D_MY33bviuQ] |
| Perception versus reality | stereotype of the job versus the real day | reach [9CsBaaYOgGM, O1LeyZsrnls] |
| Transformation | struggling state beside clearly better state, struggle in the first two lines | reach and proof [D_MY33bviuQ] |

Body rules: their problem comes before your solution [EUueg8xudKk]; the two highest-aha points go at the top of a list [O1LeyZsrnls]; a named audience with a generic body still fails, so rewrite the body into that niche's world [O1LeyZsrnls]; no off-topic asides [wVWXbYuoXYg]; a struggle beats a polished success story because it reads human [wVWXbYuoXYg].

## 3. Length and voice

Conversational length beats choppy short posts in his reading, and he cites long posts reaching up to 45% further than very short ones (creator claim, unverified) [Ej7WFBI9NS4]. Sound like you talk [Ej7WFBI9NS4, TTH-75XOgJE]. His red-flag list for AI-shaped prose: rhetorical-question openings, negation-contrast constructions, jargon, and inflated words such as "game changer" or "leverage" [TTH-75XOgJE].

## 4. The post type catalog (10 types, different jobs)

From his June 2026 rundown [5rLlzb6_vgA]:

- **Top of funnel:** newsjack (tie niche news to the problem, do not summarize, publish inside 3 days), awareness, education, and a quarterly spectacle (a big public bet or stunt).
- **Middle, about twice a week:** personal story, work photos, short video (under 60 seconds, captions, hook in the first 2 seconds).
- **Bottom:** case study (1 to 2 a week, daily during a push), objection post (does the sales call early), founder demo of the pain (including older weak features), lead magnet (comment-for-link, email on the page, pinned in Featured).

He says listicles without a personal angle are fading and generic recycled content is being down-ranked (his algorithm claim, unverified) [5rLlzb6_vgA]. His weekly mix from the growth video: 4 reach, 2 stories, 1 conversion [UJvAtV63vk0], and 1 to 2 conversion posts a week is his stated minimum for inbound [0_8TCrdBPVM].

## 5. Ideation: the problem bank

His engine for originality is a bank of real buyer problems: dump call transcripts (he names Fireflies into an AI) or prompt for the audience and offer, keep the 20 to 30 problems you actually hear, including objections, and write from those in the client's own words [EUueg8xudKk]. Trends fill the top of the funnel; the problem bank is the trust layer and grows slower [EUueg8xudKk]. Contrarian takes on real problems do better in his account, while a take on nothing does not [EUueg8xudKk]. His speed claim once set up: about 15 minutes for a text post, the whole process under 30 minutes (creator claim) [EUueg8xudKk].

## 6. CTAs and conversion copy

Above roughly a $500 to $1,000 offer he routes to a webinar or a 1:1; below that, or for a trial, direct response with UTMs on every link [0_8TCrdBPVM]. Scarcity only when true (he gives "5 new clients this month" as an honest-scarcity example, explicitly conditional) [0_8TCrdBPVM, 5rLlzb6_vgA]. The writer is the guide, never the hero; the client's result is the story [0_8TCrdBPVM, UJvAtV63vk0]. Testimonials should be scripted to the decision points; unguided praise does not move a buyer [0_8TCrdBPVM]. A case study that cannot be recognized before the see-more fold is not a case study [UJvAtV63vk0].

## Applying it (RIG)

- Eden drafts; the brief carries the framework, the buyer's problem in their words, one real story, and one receipt. Never a bare topic.
- Lane B: the problem bank comes from Mike's real discovery calls and delivery work, not a prompted list. Every metric in a case study is a sourced receipt.
- Gate-D covers publishing. A conversion post for RIG offers is a draft until Mike approves the item.
- His AI-drafting workflow conflicts with Clark's rule; the RIG reconciliation is in `SKILL.md`. Both are creator doctrine, and Mike's own post performance decides.
- Claims of "15 minutes per post" or reach multiples are his, unverified; do not quote them as RIG expectations.
