## 3. Cadence and replies

- Arrive early on the host post when the goal is the host's audience. He wants a posting-time sheet, not a guess. [gTkfDjUBXMY, 5k5Z-OJzEL8]
- Do not engage a post that is over a week old if the goal is reach. His analogy is embarrassing, the rule is the age cutoff. [lzOWlqfHz-s]
- Warm a prospect before a connect: he names two likes and one comment in one sequence, or a like plus a comment reply in another. Not a comment novel. [wplSBnjBxt0, rXs_4EsUB4w]
- On your own posts, the first comment repeats the closing question. That is a distribution mechanic in his 2024-2025 teaching, tied to the first-degree model. Keep the duplicate question as a CTA habit. Do not treat it as proof of either distribution model. [gTkfDjUBXMY, lzOWlqfHz-s]
- Reply to people who commented on you, next day, from a prepared tab list. He claims this from September 2026. It is a reply habit, not a pod. [7qDcQrCbIbI]
- Track which hosts produce comment views, not which hosts are famous. [lzOWlqfHz-s]

He does not teach a 60-to-90-minute comment pod, a like-for-like ring, or a paid engagement group. If a tactic needs a pod, it is not from this creator.
