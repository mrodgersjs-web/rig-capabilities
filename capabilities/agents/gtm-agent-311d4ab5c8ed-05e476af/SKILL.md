# Define Your 'Special Capability' to Identify Competitive Advantage

Use when: Produce evidence-backed positioning statements

Inputs: workspace brief, approved claims, supporting evidence

1. Brainstorm all your capabilities (marketing, design, coding, etc.).
2. Imagine a typical competitor and list their likely capabilities.
3. Identify the intersection where your skills are rare or superior.
4. Focus your product strategy on leveraging these standout capabilities (e.g., 'Funnel Master' + 'Designer').

Output contract:

- A clear statement of your unique value proposition based on rare skill combinations.

Limitations:

- Overestimating the value of a skill that is actually common.
- Neglecting core product functionality in favor of peripheral skills.
