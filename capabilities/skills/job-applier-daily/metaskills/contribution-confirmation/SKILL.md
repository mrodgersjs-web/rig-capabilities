# Separate factual participation from permission to include

## Enter when
A useful full review packet and claim-bound questions are ready.

## Route
Ask what Mike personally did and which outcomes can be supported. Ask separately whether to include each confirmed contribution in the resume, letter, both or neither. Revise alignment from actual responses.

Load these concrete steps or subskills when their named work starts. Preserve the inherited eight-leaf order; a router does not skip unmet upstream gates or treat configuration as execution.

2. [contribution-questions](../../subskills/contribution-questions/SKILL.md).

## Exit evidence
Participation and inclusion decisions with actual response references and current draft bindings.

## Failure and recovery
Stop the affected transition on missing evidence, malformed receipts or unavailable required adapters. Save the exact job/revision/checkpoint and unresolved hold. Resume the same operation after reconciliation. No local or JEV PASS grants submission, messaging, schedule activation or credential-change authority.
