## Purpose This card defines the **RIG Email Reply Scoring Algorithm** — a system for scoring email replies before they are sent. The goal is not to “write better emails.” The goal is to make every reply function as a small emotional composition: ```text Wound → Mirror → Autonomy → Qualification → Open Loop ``` A strong email reply should make the recipient feel: 1. **Seen** — “They understand what I actually meant.” 2. **Evaluated** — “This is not automatic access.” 3. **Safe** — “I am not being pressured.” 4. **Challenged** — “I need to decide who I am here.” 5. **Pulled forward** — “I want to know the next step.” The email should not chase. It should **hold frame**. --- ## Core Thesis Most email replies fail because they are too eager. They over-answer. They explain too much. They remove all tension. They make the sender look available, persuadable, and easy to access. A RIG email reply does the opposite. It gives enough clarity to move the conversation forward, but keeps enough selectivity to make the other person lean in. The best reply does not say:
