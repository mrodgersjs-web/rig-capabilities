# Company page from zero

The from-zero Page playbook, with RIG corrections marked. The numbers here are the source creator's claims about pages he runs, not measured RIG figures [company-page].

## Sequence

1. **Complete the shell first:** logo, banner, tagline carrying the keyword phrase, description, website, industry, size. An incomplete Page reads as abandoned, and it is the first thing a buyer checks [company-page].
2. **Employee amplification.** Page reach is structurally weaker than personal reach, so people are the distribution and the Page is the landing surface. Consistent with personal posts being classified against the author's history; it is not consistent with treating the Page as the growth channel [company-page, distribution-and-algorithm].
3. **Cadence:** the Page posts the announcement, the person posts the reasoning. Materially lower Page cadence than personal cadence; two voices, two jobs, one message framework [company-page].
4. **Comment from the Page on influencer posts.** Permitted, almost nobody does it, and at minimum it buys brand exposure at zero cost. This is the free version of borrowed attention [company-page, borrowed-attention].

## Observed on this account

The Page admin edit route (`/company/.../admin/edit-basic-info/`) has redirected to an unavailable state while the base profile rendered normally. Page admin is a separate surface and has been observed dead. Never attempt a Page edit without probing the route first, and never submit a Page-admin form into a partial render [company-page, lg-profile]. Page edits follow the same rule as profile edits: before/after values, Mike approves, a human types [viral-core].

## What not to do

- Do not run the personal-brand voice through the Page [company-page].
- Do not treat Page follower growth as a KPI that substitutes for personal reach; personal accounts out-reach pages by wide margins [company-page, distribution-and-algorithm].
- Do not schedule the Page from a tool the account does not trust to deliver; Eden is the publisher of record [company-page, viral-core].
- Page commenting still runs inside this stage's sweep caps and throttle rules [engagement-comment-led].
