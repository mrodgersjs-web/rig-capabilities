## The gate that costs the most when skipped

Editorial outlets require direct contact with the author and written confirmation before publishing on someone's behalf. That has a client-lane consequence: a ghost-written client byline is refused at the door. Sell the writing; do not sell the anonymity. The client's own direct contact with the editor is part of the deliverable.

## Writing rules for an editorial outlet

1. No product, no company, no link in the piece. Bio line only. The vendor reading happens on the first paragraph.
2. Write from the practitioner or researcher position. Standing comes from specifics, not from claimed credentials.
3. Any statistic carries its own methodology sentence. "Observed across cross-program audit data" is honest if that is what happened; "our research shows" implies a peer-review process it does not have.
4. Disclose AI assistance where the outlet asks; one major manufacturing title caps it at 10% of the piece and requires disclosure, and where the policy is unknown the pitch is refused until someone reads it.
5. First run means first run including LinkedIn. Decide the order before drafting.

## Pitch shape

Subject: contributed piece, the specific angle rather than the category. Body: two or three sentences on the thesis, one on why their readers care, one on the author's standing (what he has actually done), word count and required format in one line. Short. No pitch-deck energy, no attachment they did not ask for.

## Editorial versus paid

Editorial means an editor decides it runs, and the byline carries third-party validation. Sponsored means whoever pays decides, and a buyer who knows reads it as an ad. Applying is not a listing, sponsorship is not editorial acceptance, and a vendor partnership proves a route exists, not an unmet need. A sponsored route is labelled `route_type: sponsored_only` and is never counted as earned media in a report.

## Podcasts

The relationship channel: a specific contrarian angle, one data point that makes the host look smart for booking, and evidence the guest can hold a conversation. Target shows that interview practitioners, with more than 50 episodes and an episode in the last 30 days. Track targets (show, host, contact, episode count, relevance, status) in the lane's project page. Pitches are batch-level Gate-D items like every other send here.
