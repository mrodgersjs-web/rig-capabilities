# conversion_copy: looks like / does not

Owning main skill: ds-conversion-copy. Every row paraphrases a good-versus-bad contrast a creator showed, cited to the moment. Use as calibration before judging a screen; a card is only reusable as a visual reference after Jev ui.design scores the good side above the bad side.

| # | looks like (good) | does not (bad) | why | citation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline states the outcome or the uniqueness ("more traffic for your business"). | Headline names the category ("we build websites", "we sell coffee"). | Category labels do not give a reason to stay. | CLAIM(GTNgiTK-ic8@01:05) |
