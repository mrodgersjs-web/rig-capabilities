## What she measures

A roughly 5,000-impression post beat 100,000-impression posts on qualified leads, in her claim, repeated in February and March. A post with 500 likes from the right people beats 50,000 likes from the wrong ones. One client moved from about 800,000 to 3.6 million ARR with over 80% of pipeline from LinkedIn and zero outbound. A 70-million-plus exit founder, posting alone, got single-digit likes. All creator claims. The pattern she wants you to copy is the scoreboard (pipeline, not reach), not the ARR figure. [BPbf3JUm_dw, ZAqIUNAvXXo, R8nrEQx8rEY, _ZFncQNrOhw]

AI search is a second surface, not a follower contest. She claims small accounts, including under 500 followers, are cited at equal or higher rates, and that the median cited post has about 15 to 25 reactions and 1 comment. Technical specificity, plain-text names, and a narrow topic are the drivers she lists. Publish one 500 to 2,000 word article in the month. Strip stylized Unicode. These citation lifts are creator claims from studies she summarizes (one set of about 89,000 cited URLs, another of about 12,000 candidate posts; the second study's name was not clean in the source, so it is not repeated here).
