# Visuals, media and profile surfaces (Lara Acosta)

Load this file for the photo, banner, Featured shelf, carousels, video and image rules. Her media advice flips hardest on format, so the dated ledger is the point of the file.

## The photo and the banner

- The headshot is the logo: clear face, clean background, current, shot on a phone against white or a solid colour. It should look like the buyer, because a mismatched photo costs trust in the first second. [ozhwN9ndetM, hCzcM6jV_zY]
- The banner is standing ad space. Her working recipe is the result you deliver plus proof marks such as client logos or numbers. [UiKT7lxE4bo, hCzcM6jV_zY, LBa8SdmItjU]
- Banner width she cites is 2400 pixels; logos are chosen for the audience, not for prestige. Creator claim. [UiKT7lxE4bo, hCzcM6jV_zY]
- Dated flip: in July 2024 she argued for a plain solid colour over a designed banner, contradicting most advice and her own earlier material; by July 2025 the banner is a designed offer billboard with proof on it. [ZK-CBIFDR9w, ozhwN9ndetM]
- The photo refresh: she treats an old photo and a silent wall as the marks of a dead profile. [qap4n06dqfw]

## Featured and the offer shelf

- Featured holds one item or three, never two, and the thumbnails are designed like video thumbnails so they explain the click before it happens. [ozhwN9ndetM, hCzcM6jV_zY]
- The three slots are three offers, or an offer, a newsletter and one other surface. The newsletter belongs here, not in the profile link. [hCzcM6jV_zY]
- Her 2026 shape for Featured is a price ladder she calls a shelf: a free or cheap entry, a mid-tier, and a high tier, with the thumbnails talking and no paragraph under each link. The bands she quotes for the example profile run from free, through a mid band, to a top band in the low five figures. Creator claim. [LBa8SdmItjU]
- Never pin an old viral post as the profile's best real estate. The offer lives in Featured; the pinned post was for virality, not for selling. [Foh1r-TdnTk, hCzcM6jV_zY]

## Still images in posts

- A real photo of the work beats an infographic, and a screenshot of the tool or the number beats a designed graphic. [Foh1r-TdnTk, b_sVHSaQqGA, 6d_oxo1R9gs]
- Match the image to the post: personal photos for stories, infographics and screenshots for teaching, growth or news screenshots for updates. [6d_oxo1R9gs]
- Before-and-after and proof screenshots belong to expert posts; she says this is what a credibility-first feed rewards. Creator claim. [OSzrHwHLT6M]
- Positioning photography: conference, podcast and keynote candids, or a friend shooting usable professional-looking photos. She admits attending events partly for the photos. [Qz003lp_5vA]
- A lead-magnet post pairs with an image that makes the resource look tangible and physical. [hDknZIF9Wsc, 7ZfRNgn3oHs]
- A strong contrarian hook can carry a text-only post, but she backs it with an image when she can. [6d_oxo1R9gs]

## Carousels (dated, do not smooth)

- 2023 to early 2024: swipeable carousels were a winning format and she taught them first. [SwBgVJNZ-6Q, EACWADXrg88]
- 2025-09: she quit carousels after they had worked, calling one a hundred times better at one point and then abandoning them the same month. Creator claim. [Foh1r-TdnTk]
- 2026-01: a graphic is mandatory again for a beginner who refuses photos, because a graphic takes the feed space a photo would. [L7a694gzKG4]
- 2026-06: in her recent watching, clear text beats carousels and infographics combined. Creator claim. [e3E83C-Xxn0]

## Video (the longest reversal)

- 2024-02: video sits third behind images and carousels. [EACWADXrg88]
- 2024-11: the new native video surface is the early bet, with basic expertise clips rewarded the way early carousels were; she posted daily into it. Creator claim. [3gbPTfHEWNI]
- 2025-09-23: do not use video to grow a young account; it is authority work, and a sprint to 100,000 followers should not spend on it. [r7nnmWBTHUk]
- 2025-10-28: after a platform office visit she reverses and says educational B2B video is about to work. Production recipe she gives: phone over studio, a cheap ring light, an editor for dead air, subtitles, font and music, a hook that holds past the third second, and a caption of about 800 characters that summarizes the script. [71AspHNJxBE]
- 2026-01: text can still sell without video. [MLXZ_aa2ZKw]
- 2026-05: she declines to chase the video feed at all. [7SKd2cchGd4]
- 2026-06: video works again with a three-second hook, a long caption, and a corporate-fit topic; images still win on speed. [e3E83C-Xxn0, 6d_oxo1R9gs]
- Live video is her trust format, because writing can be faked and a live room cannot; she also uses live as the sell-day format. [_6lKN2fZgIs, Foh1r-TdnTk, JDZYNJQOee0]

## What she says does not work

- Holiday shots and Pinterest-style imagery. [r7nnmWBTHUk]
- A branded graphic next to a strong hook when a real photo was available. [1wsfrYzwKbk]
- A quote card carrying an old quote; if it must be reused, stage it as a scene with a photo. [1ph8i-NDz4k]
- Trend clips that sit outside the expertise named on the profile. [3gbPTfHEWNI]
