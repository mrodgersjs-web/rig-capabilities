# Comment targets, daily cadence, and borrowed attention

Borrowed attention is the compliant answer to a reach ask: comment where attention already exists and the eyeballs come with it [borrowed-attention]. The prize is borrowed classification, not borrowed volume.

## Target list

- Keep a standing sheet of 15-30 target accounts: buyers, the creators buyers follow, and competitors [borrowed-attention, Clark in mJ1c1PHBoo4]. If buyers never post, comment where they read [Clark in szN0DF3Oqik].
- Two target classes: ICP accounts (future buyers) and ICP-adjacent influencer accounts whose comment sections buyers lurk in [engagement-comment-led, Clark in BMfXeo02bvo].
- Thread selection: search the past week, not the past 24 hours (a 08:00 24h search returns dead threads and job ads). Floor: 5 visible reactions or 3 visible comments. No floor, no candidate. Author must have posted recently; skip dormant posters [borrowed-attention, engagement-comment-led].
- Take the author's own recent-activity URL from the card; never guess a vanity slug [borrowed-attention].
- 14-day author dedupe: never comment on the same author twice inside two weeks [engagement-comment-led].
- Mine LinkedIn's own "People you may know, from your industry" rail on the company-page admin surface for pipeline-adjacent targets before keyword search [engagement-comment-led].
- Rejected requests throttle distribution: "I don't know this person" rejections suppress profile and content reach, so target selectively [engagement-comment-led, MAVERRIK in fsw9WFwJBXA].

## Daily dose and sweep caps

- Standard dose: 5-10 real comments a day, 15-20 minutes, 30 minutes at most [Clark in SL8yfxUVFKE, mJ1c1PHBoo4]. Comment-led sweeps run 60-90 minutes a day and must not climb toward the 100-300 a day precedents that were cited precisely so people would not copy them [borrowed-attention].
- Sweep caps: max 12 comments per sweep, max 3 link-bearing, at most one site link, no two link comments in the same hour, never on a follow-batch day [engagement-comment-led].
- Clark's post-day checklist includes commenting on 10-15 posts from buyers and the voices they follow after a post goes live [Clark in Fvl_wMtcz24, DGYZ04X5pk4].
- One measured caution: 28 real comments in about an hour moved a post from 13 to 130+ impressions. Single-day evidence, not a rate, and not a licence for more [borrowed-attention].

## Sequencing with connections

- Comment first, connect after: engage their content, then reference what you read in the request [engagement-comment-led, Videll in SoW2DJC1w2Q]. The lane takes the engage-first side over the blank-request advice.
- Connect with people who engage with Mike's content; they already raised a hand [engagement-comment-led].
- Reciprocity before any ask: give something the recipient values first [engagement-comment-led, Nick Saraev in uSTGNHGFOAo].
- Connection volume stays inside linkedin-site-mechanics throttles; Clark's weekday figure is about 20 with roughly 100 a week as the ceiling [Clark in w2_zVhExo1s, outbound-and-pipeline]. Nothing sends automatically without Mike's explicit yes.

## Borrowed attention mechanics

- Comment from the company page on influencer posts: permitted, almost nobody does it, buys brand exposure at zero cost [borrowed-attention, company-page].
- Treat each comment as a mini-post in front of a borrowed audience [borrowed-attention].
- Comments carry posts into commenters' networks; your own comments can earn as many impressions as a post [Clark in DGYZ04X5pk4, 2Yv8osegsKY].

## Stop signals

- Throttle signature in composer or feed: halt the sweep, log it, resume next window [engagement-comment-led].
- Any target surface degraded or unavailable: stop, notify, never push through [engagement-comment-led].
- A sweep or follow batch already ran today: exit silently, do not stack [engagement-comment-led].
