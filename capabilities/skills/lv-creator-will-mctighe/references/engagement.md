# Engagement

Sub-skill of `lv-creator-will-mctighe`. Synthesis in RIG's words; bracketed IDs are source videos. Numbers are creator claims.

## 1. Comments: niche and real, not a quota

His position is that meaningful comments in your niche beat volume, and comment spam actively works against you [Ej7WFBI9NS4]. He bootstraps early reach with daily niche support comments [9CsBaaYOgGM], and he later narrows this to roughly 30 peers at a similar size rather than any daily quota [UJvAtV63vk0]. Two dated numbers that do not reconcile: he says top creators leave full comments on about 165 posts a week (more than 20 a day) in September 2025 [Ej7WFBI9NS4], then cites about 30 peers in January 2026 [UJvAtV63vk0]. Treat both as creator claims and prefer the later, smaller one.

He also cites a personal daily ceiling around 90 comments, marked unclear and person-dependent [Ej7WFBI9NS4].

## 2. Why comments work (his model)

- A strong comment on someone else's post spills a small slice of that audience to you; the post still has to earn it, because bad content dies no matter who comments [3UcZFurI_yw, UJvAtV63vk0].
- Wrong-niche commenters import the wrong audience, so comment targets follow the buyer, not the feed [UJvAtV63vk0].
- A single large, relevant comment can move a post's distribution in his account [D_MY33bviuQ, 5rLlzb6_vgA]. He speculates comment distribution as a mechanism (unverified) [D_MY33bviuQ].
- Agreement clicks (like, agree) are public acts on a non-anonymous network, which is why emotional-agreement hooks work; comments extend that logic [rlbI4ztEBFU].

## 3. Pods and fake engagement: rejected

He rejects engagement pods: they fail when the post is weak, and he cites platform crackdowns on pod patterns and fake tools; robot comments are out [3UcZFurI_yw, pyPoIYDg_a0]. Real comments with similar-size creators are his substitute [3UcZFurI_yw, UJvAtV63vk0]. No pod quota exists in his system [5rLlzb6_vgA].

## 4. From engagement to pipeline

- **Engagers are a lead list.** People who comment and profile viewers become a filtered DM list [pyPoIYDg_a0].
- **Give before you ask:** help first with peers, then ask; he built a circle of about 30 peers this way [UJvAtV63vk0].
- **DM volume:** he reports days of 100 messages to warm signals (creator claim) [pyPoIYDg_a0]. This is the single loudest conflict with RIG's outbound rules; see below.
- **Lead magnet flow:** comment for the link, email captured on the page, pinned in Featured; he cites a magnet post past 2,000 comments (creator claim) [5rLlzb6_vgA].
- **Replies on your own post:** objections answered in dialogue do the sales call early [0_8TCrdBPVM].

## 5. Cadence he shows

Daily niche comments during growth phases [9CsBaaYOgGM], peer support ongoing [UJvAtV63vk0], weekly transformation or case study content to reply to [pyPoIYDg_a0, UJvAtV63vk0], and no comment quota at all by 2026 [5rLlzb6_vgA]. He never claims comments are a substitute for content quality [UJvAtV63vk0].

## Applying it (RIG)

- **Mike's rule wins: comments on other people's posts are positive-only** (validate the point, add one real insight, optionally link Mike's related post) and never AI-written. McTighe's controversy hooks belong on Mike's posts, not in comment threads.
- Comment targeting follows the buyer persona; build the target list inside `linkedin-site-mechanics` throttles and never import an audience from the wrong niche.
- **DMs and connection requests are outward.** McTighe's 100-a-day habit is not run on Mike's account. Any volume send needs Mike's explicit yes per campaign, and warm-signal notes stay human-written.
- Pods, comment pods, engagement rings and auto-comment tools are refused regardless of what any creator claims; RIG's account safety rules and his own warning both say no.
- Reply management and comment gates are typed judgments (`comment.linkedin` rubric) through `rig-s1gate` before anything is sent; DEFERRED keeps the reply in draft.
